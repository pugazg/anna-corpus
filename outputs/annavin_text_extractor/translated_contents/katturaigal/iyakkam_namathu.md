## English Translation

Show the world through your condemnation that you belong to a genuine Self-Respect Movement.

Do not worry about what is to be done if the leader remains obstinate even after this condemnation.

Five lakh rupees and the labour of several years may pass to the woman who entered in the meantime.

That is not a great loss. Our honour will endure, and we can carry the movement forward in a more vigorous manner than this.

The movement is ours; it is not the leader's property. To say that he regards it as his own movement and will hand it over to Maniammai is one of the movement's waste products.

The movement exists in our sweat, tears, and labour. It will not go anywhere.

Having shown the world that we are not partners in this injustice, we can use what remains after the loss, and the strength left after all our labour, to build and protect the Self-Respect Movement.

Dravidian comrades, leave no room for confusion. Tell the leader: "We will not accept your arrangement. We condemn it. As a mark of condemnation, we now cease working with you and stand apart."

If our tears, our condemnation, and our resolve to stand apart change his heart, and if he abandons this unjust arrangement, we shall ask forgiveness, stand once again at his flower-like feet, and resume our work.

Otherwise, he, Maniammai, five lakh rupees, and this inheritance will stand on one side.

We, our pure hearts, and our capacity for labour will stand on the other. Have not even the slightest doubt about this.

This is one of the greatest trials of our lives. The country's future depends upon the purity and firmness we display in it.

Think. Do not lose heart. Decide. Do not rush. Fear not. Victory is ours.

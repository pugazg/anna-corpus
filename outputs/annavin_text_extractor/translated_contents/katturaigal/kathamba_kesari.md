## English Translation

They draw their swords; they babble with their mouths! They flare up, crying, “Just see whether I let you go!” They say, “Am I not a man of discernment?” Courage surges, and a clamour sounds along with it! They become angry and then jubilant! They hiss like snakes and immediately wander about like cows!

Tamil is in danger...

Hindi must necessarily be learned...

Opposition to Hindi is entirely just...

Tamil possesses a unique greatness...

Without knowledge of Sanskrit, Tamil culture cannot be complete.

When we saw the words shed by certain “great men” at last week’s conference of the Tamil Development Association in Coimbatore, the thought presented in our title arose in our heart. Not only to us; many friends too may wonder in various ways: “What amusement is this? Praise for Tamil! A welcome for Hindi! Surrender to Sanskrit! Are these not wholly unrelated? What connection is there between Tamil’s development and statements of this kind?”

Anger, followed immediately by jubilation! Courage, followed immediately by sorrow! A new pose every minute and a new speech every moment! When one sees such a person, one feels like asking whether the fellow is not of sound mind.

When the mind is deranged and madness mounts to the head, the brain does not remain steady. Colourful illusions appear in words; phrases become an unconnected chain; speech spills and rolls here and there without meaning, like an untied sack of gooseberries!

Those who scattered the “golden drops” quoted in our title at the Tamil development conference in Coimbatore do not belong to that category. They are great men! Men of unfaltering tongues, men distinguished by having exercised authority, men with opportunities to recline in royal luxury! With sacks upon sacks of money lying about, many people standing nearby with ingratiating smiles, and the government itself waiting upon their word, such “great men”!

At the mere sight of the list of names, our whole heart cooled with delight: “They too have now set out! What deficiency can remain for Tamil? A Raja, a former minister, and a mill magnate have come to adorn Tamil, whose beauty has been fading. Tamil is fortunate now!” A hope arose that these men, possessing both means and opportunity, would seat upon a throne the jewel cast upon the earth.

They had momentarily forgotten their iron cash boxes and lakhs and set out as virtuous men who labour for the town! What distinction! What greatness! Such thoughts were prompted by the hope that care would now vanish, the waves would subside, Tamil would flourish, and the means for it would be found.

But are they ordinary people who act as all of us desire? No! So these gentlemen set out to develop Tamil, conducted a “medley concert,” and dispersed.

Except for a few who possess boundless effort and a desire to place decaying Tamil in a state of splendour, the speeches of the “great men” assembled at Coimbatore did not even amount to a volley of words; they were exactly like a medley concert! One seemed to pat the Dravidian movement on the back, then the same man praised Sanskrit elsewhere! Another spoke of Hindi’s greatness, while yet another said Hindi domination must not be allowed! As though arranged in advance, each of the assembled great men spoke in a different fashion!

Perhaps out of a broad-minded desire not to hurt anyone’s feelings, every one of the assembled “great men” spoke in a different way. They conducted a medley concert!

Tamil is like a deer caught between storm and fire! It suffers like a mother blindfolded and abandoned in a forest where snakes and tigers roam! Through the actions of the arrogant and the strange arrangements of Vibhishanas, it is being tortured atom by atom!

This dreadful spectacle, this altar of cruelty, must be hewn down. We must labour to cut it out by the roots and make a new order blossom. For that, warriors with purity of thought and zeal to labour for the community are needed; not clay images, not silkworms, not opportunistic locusts, not medley-men, not “great men”!

(*Dravida Nadu*, 28-5-5[?])

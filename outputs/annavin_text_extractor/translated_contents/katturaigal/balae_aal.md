## English Translation

Rajaji is an extraordinary man! The title "Chanakya of the Congress" bestowed upon him is most fitting. He has no equal in pinching the child, rocking the cradle, singing a lullaby, and then claiming that the child fell asleep only because of his song!

Recently, turmoil swept through the Indian National Congress like a violent storm. Tandon resigned, and Nehru assumed the presidency. Newspapers publicised the names of many leaders and their efforts in sensational headlines concerning these events. Yet the name of "our man" appeared nowhere! Many even wondered whether he had no connection whatsoever with these developments. He remained that silent, never opening his mouth.

But this gentleman revealed his thoughts through a little mischief on September 9.

Nehru was speaking forcefully about his election as president and the tasks to be undertaken next.

But do you know what "our man" was doing then?

He was holding the hand of Tandon, who sat nearby, reading his palm and predicting his future! Saty Narayan Sinha, the whip of the Indian legislature, and R. R. Diwakar, one of the ministers, are said to have watched this amusing scene with great interest.

What mischief! Tandon had resigned his presidency only the previous day. Rajaji had apparently developed extraordinary concern for predicting what Tandon's future would be and how he would pass his time!

Yet at that very meeting, Nehru had thundered in condemnation of astrology. A Delhi newspaper had reportedly published an astrological prediction that war would break out between Pakistan and India in the coming September. The Prime Minister spoke like a storm: "There are fools in our country who believe such frauds." He passionately asked, "Are we to determine our destiny by such blind beliefs? Are we to believe what fools say and remain enslaved by it?" He went on vehemently: "As far as I am concerned, I believe there must be an extremely severe law to eradicate completely the practice of claiming to make predictions through astrology or of believing such predictions."

The Prime Minister spoke passionately against astrology.

At the same time and in the same place, Rajaji was studying the lines on Tandon's palm.

The Press Trust of India reported both items as an entertaining incident.

C. R. makes his own associate the president and then reads his opponent's palm. Such exultation, it seems!

Nehru, however, spoke against astrology.

The two are friends. Yet what a farce, and at a Working Committee meeting! Who can understand its meaning except the "all-powerful" Kulluka Bhattar!

(*Dravida Nadu*, 16-9-51)

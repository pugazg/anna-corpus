## English Translation

On this year's Independence Day, the Madras Government conducted the worthy event of presenting awards to three eminent experts in literature, music, and drama: Dr Mu. Varadarasanar, Mrs K. B. Sundarambal, and drama professor P. Sambandhanar.

Whatever varied opinions people may hold about the views and political outlook of the three, it is a matter of great joy that such a felicitation has been conducted for the first time. Dr Varadarasan brings distinction to Tamil by offering stories and essays rich in literary wealth. Sundarambal is the treasured daughter of Tamil who has spread its flavour through sweet music from that day to this. The drama professor's service to fine Tamil is no ordinary one.

Therefore, though belated, the Government's decision at least now to honour these patrons of the threefold Tamil gives joy and pride. We commend the Government for developing the intention to accord respect to Tamil. We desire that it conduct more such worthy acts in the future.

Long live Tamil! Long live the masters of the threefold Tamil!

(*Seithi*, 18 August 1957; *Dravida Nadu*)

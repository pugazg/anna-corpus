## English Translation

...possessed [an unblemished character]. He was elected by the public and, while serving as chairman of the Kanchipuram Municipal Council, performed many good works. He possessed the excellent quality of turning even opponents into friends. One of his principles was that a system of compulsory education for everyone should be put into practice.

Kanchipuram has lost a distinguished man who possessed love of language, attachment to his people, and a public-spirited outlook.

May our words of consolation go to the family and relatives grieving over his departure.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 9-5-1949)

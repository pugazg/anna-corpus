## English Translation

He grew under the name Mayilvaganan and gained distinction under the name Vipulananda. He mastered Tamil, Sanskrit, English, and other languages, rose through discipline, excelled in research, and closely examined the musical knowledge of ancient Tamils that others had dismissed as insignificant. Comparing it with musical treatises in other languages, he concluded that Tamils possess a music of their own and that it is exceedingly ancient. He composed a treatise on the yal and rendered incomparable service to Tamils and Tamil.

The news that the eminent Vipulananda died in Colombo on 20 July 1947 will undoubtedly cause pain to Tamils. Those who know the aims and course of the Ramakrishna Mission will marvel that, despite being inseparably associated with it from youth, Swami Vipulananda's Tamil character remained unchanged and that he served the Tamil language.

Let us resolve to follow the service of the scholar who lived and died for Tamils.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 27 July 1947)

## English Translation

*To the tune of a garden song*

The pest called Hindi is coming;
rise and come!
It strikes at the breast of classical Tamil;
gather and come!

Tamil, which ruled the whole region,
now stands obstructed;
the neighbouring ancient languages too
stand with their mouths sealed.

Hindi is a two-headed snake,
small only upon the page;
the northern-language pest leaps about
and enters the assembly.

From behind the Aryan screen,
someone sets the rhythm;
the political Aryan northerner makes it dance:
that is the dance we see.

Avinashilingam once told us,
"It is not compulsory."
Now the same man says midway,
"It is compulsory indeed."

They say there is no money
to remove illiteracy and spread Tamil everywhere,
yet for unworthy, disorderly Hindi
they beat the drum and spend freely.

Though the burden burns, reform will not come;
they languish in show.
Those who came to rule grow angry
when we say Hindi is not wanted.

The ancient language spoken
by our grandfathers and forefathers is enough.
Rule by the Vedas and the languages of alien spirits,
we absolutely do not want them!

Ka. Appadurai, M.A. (Tamil), M.A. (English), L.T., Visharad (Hindi)

(Poem published on the front page of *Dravida Nadu*, 8-8-48)

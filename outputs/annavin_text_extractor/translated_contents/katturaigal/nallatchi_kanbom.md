## English Translation

On Pongal, a festival for the people of the sacred land where bountiful paddy, sky-high sugarcane, pearls, and gold flourish, my younger brother brings out a special issue and lets both you and me immerse ourselves in a flood of joy.

The country knows that *Murasoli* is a sharp sword of war for the Dravidian liberation army. With distinguished literary portrayals and pictorial explanations, this journal of ideals has been rendering service on a scale worthy of pride and in a manner that fills me with satisfaction. It will continue to serve.

To remove evils by pointing out that society must not have heights on one side and hollows on the other, and to create equality; to impress upon people the proper principle that politics must not contain arrogance, a regime of repression, or a spirit of indifference, the DMK is working. It has also chosen the electoral path, one of the democratic duties needed for that purpose.

Just as the Pongal festival brings renewal to Tamils and Dravidians, may this year also bring a new change in politics, the necessary turning point.

Pongal comes with the power to fill us with satisfaction at a time when we all stand ready to strive for good governance in the country. Along with it comes the *Murasoli* special issue. Savour Pongal and rejoice! Read the special issue and rejoice! Change arrogant politics and rejoice.

Affectionately,

Annadurai

(*Murasoli*, Pongal Special Issue, 14-1-27)

## English Translation

In the August campaign of civil resistance, many mothers took part in various places. Will the future ever forget the service of those who entered the field to wipe away the humiliation suffered by the motherland? Will the motherland ever fail to thank the mothers who joined the struggle to protect Tamil? Here is the list of those worthy of praise, those “lamps of Tamil”:

Ramamirtham Ammaiyar - Mayavaram  
Sathyavani Muthu - Madras  
Duraikannammal - Madras  
Alamelu Appadurai - Madras  
Poongothai - Madras  
Arulmozhi - Madras  
A. P. S. K. Ammal - Madras  
Savithri - Vellore  
Pattammal - Madurai  
Sakunthala - Ariyakudi  
T. N. Mariammal - Gobi  
N. G. K. Udayagiri Ammaiyar - Kundrathur

34. அடக்குமுறை ௮ட்டவணை!

அவசரச் சட்டங்கள் - அவற்றின் கீழே பல அவசரமான
காரியங்கள், இவற்றைச் செய்து வருவதில் சென்னை
அரசாங்கம் பிரிட்டிஷ் ஏகாதிபத்திய கொடிய ஆட்சியையும்
மீறிவிட்டது.

எதிர்க்கட்சியா - அதுவும் திராவிடர் கழகமா அதன்
நாக்கை அறு, கையை முறி, காலை ஒடி என்ற கட்டளைகளைக்
“காருண்ய மிகுந்த” காங்கிரஸ் அரசாங்கத்தின் “செங்கோல்”
இதுகாலம், அன்றாடம் பிறப்பித்து வருவதிலே தவறுவதில்லை.

## English Translation

Tamil title: அடக்குமுறை அட்டவணை!

Emergency laws, and many urgent acts carried out beneath them: in doing these things, the Madras government has surpassed even the cruel rule of British imperialism.

Is it an opposition party, and particularly the Dravidar Kazhagam? "Cut out its tongue, break its hands, smash its legs." The "righteous sceptre" of the "compassionate" Congress government has never failed to issue such commands every day.

Today's national rulers have eagerly embraced every repressive law left behind by British autocratic power for destroying anything that differs from the government's views, whether good or bad, merely because it differs. Standing in the shade of "nonviolence," they have even enacted several laws that British imperialism forgot to create.

They have enacted a measure called the Public Safety Amendment Bill and rule in a manner that permits them to arrest anyone they "suspect" of disturbing public peace, imprison that person for as long as they please, avoid saying when the prisoner will be released, and give no reason for any of it. Such is the government of Mother India's millions of disciples.

One doctrine, one party, one voice; nothing different or contrary may exist. That is the lesson taught by fascism. The methods of today's rulers show that they studied the lesson, sat its examination, obtained a degree, and then entered parliamentary government.

The very fact that the Dravidar Kazhagam moves about the country irritates the eyes and minds of Congressmen. It especially irritates those who have mounted the seat of power. The Congress government therefore acts according to a deliberate plan: use official power to suppress the Dravidar Kazhagam by any means, so that only one party, the Congress, may prevail.

As one such act, it has confiscated *History of the Ideal*, a book belonging to the Kazhagam and written by Comrade C. N. A. The government confiscated it under Section 19. To this extent does emergency legislation make the seat of power dance to its tune.

Behold the schedule of repression undertaken over the past two and a half years by the "nonviolent" government that follows the "Gandhian path" in suppressing the Dravidar Kazhagam:

- *Ravana Kaviyam* - banned.
- *Inba Dravidam* - banned.
- *History of the Ideal* - banned.
- The play *Hiranyan, or the Peerless Hero* - banned.
- The play *The Sword of War* - banned.
- The play *The Dictator* - banned.
- The play *The Gallows* - banned.
- The publication *Kuyil* - banned.
- The play *Devotee of the Mahatma* - banned.
- Part of the play *Tears of Blood* - banned.
- The press that printed *Aryan Illusion* - security of Rs. 1,500.
- *Dravida Nadu* - security of Rs. 3,000.
- *Viduthalai* - security of Rs. 10,000.
- *Kudi Arasu* - security of Rs. 3,000.
- The Blackshirt Corps - banned.
- Kazhagam meetings in certain towns - banned.
- Certain Kazhagam comrades - required to furnish security for good behaviour.
- Kazhagam publications in school libraries - banned.
- Rationalist ideas propagated by the Kazhagam - banned from school textbooks.

All these repressive measures were hurled merely because the Kazhagam is an opposition party.

The party that has mounted the Congress seat of power may do whatever it pleases; the government does not care. In the Annamalainagar student-riot case, members of both the Congress and the Dravidar Kazhagam were implicated. Both sides were charged with offences, and proceedings were brought against both under the same section of criminal law.

The government that dispenses "justice" in Gandhi's name withdrew the case against the Congress students merely because they were Congress students, without giving any reason.

Congress Party meetings may be conducted anywhere and in any manner. Congress propagandists may go anywhere and slander anyone however they please, lying without restraint. Congressmen may inflict any degree of violence upon Kazhagam comrades, break up Kazhagam meetings and burn Kazhagam pavilions. Congress ideas may be inserted into textbooks to any extent. Congressmen may defame Kazhagam leaders with words the tongue itself would shrink from uttering, make vile accusations and write obscenely. None of this faces any prohibition.

But the Gandhian disciples have resolved to inflict upon the Dravidar Kazhagam, which stands on integrity, follows justice and spreads rationalist ideas, every hardship permitted by all the laws created thus far.

The now-confiscated *History of the Ideal* is merely a collection of articles previously published in *Dravida Nadu*. The articles appeared and were read with benefit by many thousands of people. Only several years later were they issued as a book, and that book circulated in the country for more than a year. After all this, only now has it occurred to the government that the book ought to be confiscated.

*History of the Ideal* was written to explain the historical distinction of how the Dravidar Kazhagam's ideal developed, and why the ideal "Dravida Nadu for the Dravidians" had become a necessary question demanding fulfilment in various ways. It drew literary, historical, geographical, economic and political evidence from the statements of great professors, intellectual authorities and research experts.

The book does not even contain the "communal hatred" or "atheism" routinely alleged against us by Congressmen. If the government has nevertheless moved to confiscate it, what can the motive be other than a desire to uproot and cast away the opposition party?

Why do Congressmen seek a backdoor method of this kind to oppose the Dravidar Kazhagam? Why not open their mouths and argue through a direct, honest, just and friendly method? Why do they retreat from this civilized course? Is their doctrine so weak? Why are they afraid to stand before us with the weapon of reason rather than the government's weapon of repression?

If this government dislikes rationalist thought and prefers orthodox thought, the honourable course is to spread what it prefers. If they possess intellectual courage, let them permit rationalist thought to circulate and themselves spread orthodox ideas from antiquity.

If a government devoted to orthodox religion dislikes the reformist plays we stage, let it stage puranic plays throughout the country. Let it present the story of Tara, in which she rubs oil upon Chandra while naked. Let it show the story of Nalayini, who carried her leprosy-stricken husband to a courtesan's house and brought him back again. Let it depict the three-eyed god behaving improperly towards the wives of the sages of the Daruka forest.

Let it stage the story of the dweller of Kailasa who became infatuated with Mahavishnu in the Mohini incarnation and invited that form to intercourse; the story of Iyarpagai Nayanar, who handed his wife to a devotee of Siva; the story of Uchchhishta Pillaiyar, who sits a naked woman upon his lap and performs an improper act; and the story in which sages, angered that Siva ignored them while absorbed in sexual pleasure, cursed him and caused the lingam and avudaiyar to descend towards the earth.

Let it show the story of the sea-coloured god who secretly entered Jalandhara's house while he was away at war and embraced his wife Vrinda; and the story of Indra, who embraced Ahalya and, through a curse, received unspeakable marks all over his body.

Let the rulers stage in the country such puranic stories, filled with "divine philosophy." Instead of taking the back route and banning rationalist reform dramas, let them stage the puranic dramas listed above and win!

If they wish, former and present ministers may take the principal roles and stage the *Complete Ramayana* itself. Omandur Ramasamy Reddiar may play Janaka; Kumaraswami Raja may play Rama; Avinashilingam may act as Viswamitra; and Bhaktavatsalam may be given the role of Hanuman. The other ministers may appear in roles suited to each of them.

Since it is the *Complete Ramayana*, Congress legislators too may receive roles. Sardar Vedaratnam may be Vibhishana, Vaidyanatha Iyer may be Sugriva, and Varadachari may be Maricha. Thus, without refusing anyone, roles may be distributed among all and the drama performed. At least by appearing as dramatic characters, today's ministers could try to propagate their ideas.

The present ministry knows only how to ban what it dislikes; for some reason, it does not know how to propagate what it likes.

The schedule of repression grows day by day, and evidently will continue to grow. We hear that many more Kazhagam books, waiting to be banned, lie upon the desks of Congress ministers. That is why we say the schedule of repression will keep expanding.

In no country of the world have banned books remained prohibited forever. Even when books and plays are temporarily banned, the ideas contained within them inevitably return in other forms.

*Dravida Nadu*, 31 July 1949.

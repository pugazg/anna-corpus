## English Translation

On the fifth, petitions concerning Acharya's hereditary-occupation educational scheme were presented in various places to MLAs who had won with DMK support.

Nowhere else in world politics can one see voters obtaining a pledge from their elected MLAs to fight schemes unacceptable to the people.

This occurred peacefully and democratically in many places.

In Madras alone, however, Acharya's government arrested and imprisoned fifty-seven DMK comrades on the fifth and ten DMK comrades on the ninth. Our front-line fighter Comrade A. V. P. Asaithambi was among those arrested on the fifth. A woman fighter was among those arrested the next day.

Our eyes are upon the prison. Acharya is stirring a volcano. We are filled with peace and dignity, but because authority is in his hands, he locks us in prison. Sixty-seven comrades are the first sacrifice to Acharya's cruel jaws. We salute those heroic bulls who fight the unjust Government.

Long live the heroes! Down with repression!

May the Kallakudi righteous struggle triumph!

(*Dravida Nadu*, 12 July 1953)

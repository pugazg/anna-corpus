## English Translation

We rejoiced upon seeing the statement of the twenty-eighth.

We gained new hope and called it a star shining with hope.

We said red honey flowed through our hearts. But a situation has arisen in which we must cry, "Alas! Not even that!"

Believing a way had appeared for unity and pride, *Dravida Nadu* went to press in jubilation and kindled joy in our minds.

As though saying, "Is this joy for you, Dravidian comrade? Here, see the news that will break your heart," Periyar issued a statement on the thirtieth in *Viduthalai*: "The statement published in *Viduthalai* on the twenty-eighth was not written by me. I was shocked upon seeing it."

What shall we do? We imagined some measure of comfort, hope, and renewal might bloom in the poor heart. We were deceived. Alas, apparently not even that! It was not a star of hope. Only the darkness of pain envelops us, the party, and Dravidam. What flashed was not a star. What appeared on the twenty-eighth was not a statement given by Periyar. What shall we do with this burning grief? What can we do now?

(*Dravida Nadu*, special publication, 31749)

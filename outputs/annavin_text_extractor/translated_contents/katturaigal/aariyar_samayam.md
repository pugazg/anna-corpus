நிறைவேற்றத்தக்க அரசர்களும் வெகு காலம்வரை இருந்து
வந்ததாலேயே அப்படிப்பட்ட ஆபாச அக்கிரம அவிவிவேகமான।
அவர்களது சமய ஆதாரங்கள் திராவிட மக்களுக்குள்
பரப்பப்படவும் அதனால் ஆரியர்களுக்கு மேன்மையும்
செல்வா? ம் பெருகி நிலைத்து வரவும் இடமேற்பட்டதுடன்,
ஆரியரல்டாத மக்கள் எவ்வளவு பண்டிதர்களானாலும், அந்த
ஆரியர் சமய ஆதாரங்களைப் பிரசாரம் செய்வதனால் மாத்திரமே
வாழ முடியும்படியாகவும் இருந்து வந்திருக்கிறது.

ஆரிய. சமய ஆதாரம்

உண்மையில ஆரியர்களின். சமய ஆதாரங்களில்
பெரும்பாலம் எதைப்பார்த்தாலும் ஒரு மனிதனால் சொல்லப்
பட்டதாகவோ, உற்பத்தி செய்யப்பட்டதாகவோ காண்பது
முடியாததாபிருந்தும், ஒவ்வொரு. ஆதாரத்துக்கும் அதன் கர்த்தர்
தெய்வத் தன்மை பொருந்தியவனாகவும். அறிவுக்கும்
ஆராய்ச்சிக்கும் பொருத்தமில்லாத அற்புத சக்தி பெற்றவனாகவும்
தான் கற்பிக்கப்பட்டு இருப்பான்.

ஏனெனில், அப்படிக் கற்பிக்கப்பட்ட புளுகுக் களஞ்சியங்கள்
நிலை நிற்க முடியும். அப்படி இல்லாமல் சாதாரண மனிதனால்
செய்யப்பட்டதென்றால், அது அவனுக்கு எப்படித் தெரியும், அது
எப்படி முடியும் “அத்தனை நாட்களாய் அவன் எப்படி---செய்கிற,
பேசுகிற, நினைக்கிற காரியம் எல்லாம் கடவுள் செயலால்தான்
என்று கருதுவது ஆஸ்திகர்களுக்குச் சர்வ சாதாரணமான
காரியமேயாகும்." ஆதலால் நபிபெருமான். தான் சொல்லும்
விஷயங்களைக் கடவுள். அருளால் சொல்லுகிறேன் என்று
சொன்னதில் அற்புதம் ஒன்றும் இருப்பதாக நாம் நினைக்கவில்லை.
மற்றபடியும் அவர் எப்போதும் தன்னை மனுஷத்தன்மைக்கு
மேம்பட்டவர் என்று சொல்லிக் கொண்டதாகவும் தெரியவில்லை.
ஆரிய சமய. ஆதாரம் பூராவுமே மனுஷத்தன்மைக்கு
மேம்பட்டதாகவே காணப்படும்.

அவர்களது வாழ்வுக்கு வழிகாட்டியாகவே கதைகளும் மற்றும்

அவர்களது புராணங்கள் இதிகாசங்கள்  ஆகிவையும் இருந்து
வருகின்றன இப்புராணங்களைத்தான் தங்களது சடிய
ஆதாரங்களாகக் கருதி ஆரியர்கள் நடந்து வருகிறார்கள்.

ஆரியப் புராண இதிகாசங்கள் ஒரு பெரும் சமூகத்தாரைச்
சிறு சமூகத்தார் எப்படி ஏமாற்றிப் பிரித்துச் சிறுபான்மையோராக்கி
தாங்கள் ஆதிக்கம் செலுத்துவது என்பத்ற்கு ஏற்ற வழிகளேயாகும்.
அதைப் பின்பற்றி நடந்தேதான் இன்று ஆரியர்கள் மற்றவர்கள்
மீது ஆதிக்கம் செலுத்துகிறார்கள்.

எப்படியெனில், அப்புராண இதிகாசங்களில் ஒழுக்கம்
இருக்காது; மானாபிமானம் -இருக்காது; நாணயம் இருக்காது.
எவ்வளவு இழிவான காரியத்தையும் செய்து, காரியத்தை சாதித்துக்
கொள்ள மார்க்கங்கள் இருக்கும் “பத்தியமே” கிடையாது. ஆனால்,
அவை மகா நீதியாக மொத்தம் ஒழுக்கமாகவும் பாசாங்கு
செய்துகொள்ளும்.

ஆரியத் தலைவர்கள் பெரியார்கள், ரிஷிகள், முனிவர்கள்
முதலியோரது நடவடிக்கைள் எப்படி  இருந்திருக்கின்றன என்று
பார்த்தால், “அதுவும் ஒரு ஆபாசக் களஞ்சியமாய்த்தான் இருக்கிறது.
அவர்கள் ஆதாரப்படி. எது விபசாரம், எது கற்பு என்பது
கண்டுபிடிக்க முடியாததாகவே : காணப்படும். இன்னும்
பார்க்கப்போனால், அனேக ஆரியப் பெரியார்கள் மனிதர்களுக்கு
மக்களாயில்லாமல் மிருகங்கள், பக்ஷிகள் மச்சங்களுக்குப்
பிறந்திருப்பார்கள். மற்றும் பார்க்கப்போனால், ஆணுக்கு ஆண்
சங்கமத்தின் மூலம். சந்ததியும், சங்கமமே இல்லாமல் சந்ததியும்
உற்பத்தியும் ஆகிய இவையும் இதுபோன்ற இன்னும் பலவும்
கடிவாளம் இல்லாத குதிரை மாதிரி அவர்கள். எப்படி
வேண்டுமானாலும் நடந்துகொள்ள அனுமதியளிப்பதாகக்
காணப்படுவதையும் பார்க்கலாம். இவை நடந்திருக்காது என்பது
நமது துணிவானாலும் அவர்களது வாழ்க்கை மேன்மைக்கு ஆகக்
கற்பித்துக் கொண்ட கற்பனைகளில் இந்த ஆபாசங்களையும்,
அசம்பாவிதங்களையும் லக்ஷியம் செய்யாமல். காரியமே
கண்ணாயினார் என்பதற்கு அனுகூலமான வழிகாட்டி ஆதாரங்கள்
கற்பித்துக்: கொண்டிருக்கிறார்கள் என்பதைக் காட்டவாவது
இவற்றைக் குறிப்பிடுகின்றோம்.

## English Translation

We have previously written four editorials under the heading “Tamil Nadu for Tamils.” This is the fifth. In the preceding editorial we announced that this one would discuss the religion of the Aryans. Let us write a little about it.

Whether we consider India or Dravida Nadu, the Aryans are a very small minority comprising three persons in every hundred. If we consider how they make the other ninety-seven—the true majority—and their ancient, valiant country into a minority, enslave them and exercise domination, we find that Aryan religion and the unity it creates have supplied the basis and facilities they need. They continue to do so today. We therefore wish to explain the matter.

## What Is the Religion of the Aryans?

Everyone knows that the Aryans possess no single, separate institution with one specified doctrine. Even if “Aryan religion” is called Hinduism, prescribed conditions and punishments were written down for how it should operate. Kings able to enforce them existed for a long time. This allowed what the editorial calls their obscene, unjust and irrational religious authorities to spread among Dravidian people, thereby enabling Aryan prestige and influence to grow and endure.

The condition also became such that non-Aryans, however learned they might be, could make a living only by propagating those Aryan religious authorities.

## Foundations of Aryan Religion

Most Aryan religious authorities cannot be shown to have been spoken or produced by an ordinary human being. Instead, the author of each is imagined as divine and as possessing miraculous powers incompatible with reason and inquiry.

Only treasuries of fabrication constructed in this way can endure. If they were admitted to be the work of ordinary people, questions would arise: How could that person know it? How could it be possible? How could he have existed for so many ages?

They can escape such questions by saying: “The writer or speaker was divine, and therefore capable of writing it. No one should argue about it.” When a matter becomes too difficult to explain, it is convenient to escape by saying, “God himself said it” or “a disembodied voice spoke.” That is why these authorities describe everything in them as spoken or done through powers beyond human capacity.

The religious authorities associated with Buddhism, Christianity and Islam are not generally of that kind. As far as possible they remain within the human sphere. Even where one or two matters are made divine, there is no absolute compulsion that they must not be debated.

In Islam particularly, insofar as the Prophet Muhammad is concerned, nothing in his words or actions is represented as exceeding human nature. He said that he revealed the words of the Quran under God's command; that is a separate matter. It is quite ordinary for believers to hold that everything a person does, says and thinks occurs through God's action. We therefore see nothing miraculous in the Prophet saying that he spoke through divine grace. Nor does he appear always to have described himself as superior to human nature. The foundations of Aryan religion, by contrast, appear throughout as superhuman.

## Puranas and Epics

Although the Aryans call the Vedas, Shastras and Smritis the religious authorities for their lives, stories—especially their Puranas and epics—also serve as guides. Aryans conduct themselves by treating these Puranas as religious authority.

The editorial argues that Aryan Puranas and epics provide methods by which a small community may deceive and divide a large one, reduce it to powerless minorities and establish domination. It says Aryans continue to dominate others by following these methods.

In those Puranas and epics, it claims, there is no morality, honour, self-respect or honesty. They offer ways to achieve an object through any degrading deed and impose no restraint, while pretending that all this constitutes supreme justice and complete morality.

If one examines the conduct attributed to Aryan leaders, great men, rishis and sages, the editorial says, one finds another treasury of obscenity. Under their own authorities it becomes impossible to determine what constitutes sexual misconduct and what constitutes chastity. Many Aryan great men are represented not as the children of humans but as born to animals, birds and fish. Stories also describe offspring produced through unions between men and men and without any union at all. These and many other tales appear to grant licence to behave in any fashion, like a horse without reins.

We confidently hold that these events did not occur. We mention them only to show that, in fantasies composed to exalt Aryan life, their authors disregarded obscenity and impossibility, fixed their eyes solely upon the desired end, and created authorities that would serve as convenient guides.

It is for this reason, the editorial says, that they established the rule that one must not examine the source of a rishi or a river.

## What Is the Religion of the Aryans? — Restated

It is widely known that the Aryans possess no institution founded upon a single specified doctrine. Even if Aryan religion is called “Hinduism,” researchers have concluded that Hinduism itself possesses no single definite doctrine or authority.

The authorities now treated as the religious foundations of Aryans consist of statements and writings periodically produced by Aryan strategists and people hostile to non-Aryans in order to degrade, subdue, deceive and lower them. These were collected, modified to suit changing time and place, and supplemented with new material.

Though they bear names such as Veda, Shastra, Shruti, Smriti, Purana and Itihasa, many have no relationship to one another and contradict one another. They are filled with what the essay describes as obscenities, impossibilities and absurd events, and largely fail to accord with intelligence, inquiry or reason.

For this very reason, the editorial says, non-Aryans were forbidden to study them and severe punishments prescribed for doing so. If anyone studied or heard these authorities and then debated, disputed or refused to believe them, that person was called an atheist and was to be punished by the king, expelled from society and country, and represented as someone fit to be killed or destroyed.

The source then repeats the explanation that each authority's author is imagined as divine and endowed with miraculous powers beyond reason, because acknowledging an ordinary human author would invite questions. Divine authorship and disembodied voices provide an escape from scrutiny.

It again contrasts these claims with Buddhist, Christian and Islamic sources, which it describes as remaining largely within human limits, and returns to its argument about the Puranas and epics.

Take Indra, protector and leader among the Aryan gods. Can any episode be cited in which he possessed morality or honesty? How many unjust and degrading deeds is he said to have used to defeat people seeking his position? To speak plainly, women were sent to arouse desire and deceive them. Do Aryans feel shame about any of this? On the contrary, do they not celebrate festivals for it?

Thus the stories of their gods and those gods' actions provide authority for the proposition that Aryans may employ deeds of this or any other kind to achieve their objects. A further useful guide is the command that, however immoral the gods or their actions may be, they must neither be criticised nor regarded with shame.

Consider the *Ramayana*. It shows the various ways in which Ravana, represented as the enemy of the Aryans, was deceived, and the extent of the intrigue, stratagem and cruelty used by Rama, their leader. These become another guide to Aryan life. The episodes are fashioned as authorities displaying methods for destroying an enemy.

Such Aryan religious sources continue to serve and benefit Aryans as guides precisely because Aryans use them to accomplish their purposes.

We non-Aryan Dravidian people, however, place these obscene and scheming authorities upon our heads without understanding any benefit. We call ourselves bound by them and say they suit us as well. Paying no attention to our own welfare and advancement, we fear to act in such ways for our own affairs while making ourselves and our country useful to Aryans, enabling their progress and superiority.

It is solely to end this condition that we say in every sphere: “Dravida Nadu for the Dravidians.”

With this editorial we have written five pieces under the heading “Tamil Nadu for Tamils.” Fearing that the series may have become tiresome, we conclude it here.

We intend to reserve a page in *Viduthalai* specifically for explaining matters related to this subject, and may write about them frequently there.

We shall close with one final observation. If Dravida Nadu, within Dravidian boundaries, had remained under the authority of Dravidian people, could the following situation have arisen in the Madras Presidency government? Among 49,995 low-grade subordinate posts paying less than thirty-five rupees a month, 1,513 went to Aryans and the remaining 48,482 to Dravidians; while among one thousand higher executive posts paying from five hundred to one thousand rupees a month, six hundred went to Aryans.

Could Aryans have used these offices to support their community, keep all Aryans in superior positions and all Dravidians in inferior ones, and cause Dravidians to fear and serve Aryans? We conclude by asking readers to consider this.

# The Objective of Congress

Whether sleeping, walking, working or resting, the people of Tamil Nadu—religious believers, rationalists and Congress supporters—must keep “Tamil Nadu for Tamils” in mind. It has become clear beyond doubt that when danger threatens their heritage, Tamils will forget party differences and unite.

All Tamils must thank the Honourable Rajagopalachari for providing an opportunity for people divided into many sections to unite. After lying enslaved to Aryans for many centuries, Tamils gained intellectual illumination through English education and began gradually freeing themselves from what the article calls the Aryan affliction. Schemes are now under way, in the name of nationalism, to enslave them to Aryans once more. Tamils must not consent to the success of that scheme.

The source passage at this point is displaced into its discussion of language: Brahmins enriched Sanskrit by adding material from Tamil. The late Pandit V. G. Suryanarayana Sastri, himself a Brahmin, acknowledged this.

What Tamils chiefly need now is Western scientific knowledge. Western scientific works must be translated into Tamil—good, pure Tamil. There is no need to fear a shortage of words in translation; English terms may be freely used wherever essential.

Terms employed in English works on philosophy, logic, physics and chemistry are not all English words. English scholars have freely used Greek, Latin, French, German, Sanskrit and other terms. The same practice brings no disgrace to any language. Our entire purpose must be to enrich Tamil in every possible way.

For Tamil to attain that high state, it must become an official language. Tamil can become an official language only if a separate Tamil province is created. We therefore ask Tamils to devote serious attention to the division and creation of a Tamil province.

Congress Brahmins seek to abolish all the benefits we obtained through English and enslave Tamils to northern people and Aryan civilisation.

It is therefore our great desire that the Tamil movement should labour with the objectives of creating a separate Tamil province, making Tamil its official language and retaining English as India's common language.

The scan then returns to the Triplicane meeting. Kaivalyam represented religious believers, Comrade Bharathiar represented Congressmen, and Periyar E. V. Ramasamy represented rationalists. Thus, it says again, Tamil Nadu's opinion was fully reflected there.

The people of Tamil Nadu fall into three broad sections: religious believers, rationalists and Congress supporters. The three great men publicly conveyed the opinions of those three sections to Tamils that day. It has therefore become clear that the newly arisen Tamil movement belongs to no single party, but to all Tamils without differences of caste, religion, colour or party.

From now on, “Tamil Nadu for Tamils” must be the Tamils' fundamental mantra. Tamils must keep that golden phrase constantly in mind and strive to strengthen the Tamil movement. Every Tamil should print “Tamil Nadu for Tamils” in large letters and paste it upon the wall of the home. Tamil owners of printing presses should print the golden phrase in beautiful lettering and distribute it freely to all Tamil people. It must resound throughout Tamil Nadu.

The scan repeats part of the preceding argument: all Tamils must thank Rajagopalachari for giving divided Tamils an opportunity to unite. As Tamils, enslaved to Aryans for centuries, use the illumination of English education to free themselves from the Aryan affliction, schemes are proceeding in nationalism's name to enslave them again. Tamils must never consent.

The article argues that scheming Brahmins suppressed the Siddhar movement, which had fought Aryan civilisation, through Brahmin religion. Congress Brahmins now seek, in nationalism's name, to destroy the Self-Respect Movement, which fights to abolish Aryan civilisation and is gaining success. Congress Tamils, failing to understand the scheme, assist the Brahmins and act as their accomplices. May they gain wisdom at least by observing Bharathiar, himself a Congressman.

As Comrade E. V. Ramasamy said at the Madras meeting on the eleventh, Tamils must agitate to separate Tamil Nadu. The anti-Hindi movement is the first step towards a movement for a separate Tamil province. If Tamil Nadu is separated, the Tamil language, its grammar and literature, its arts and civilisation will develop.

Tamil is an excellent and living language capable of growth. It possesses the power to contain every art and every field of knowledge. Only if a separate Tamil province is created can Tamil become an official language. We therefore ask Tamils to pay serious attention to the creation of a Tamil province.

If provinces are divided linguistically according to Congress policy, a common language for all provinces will certainly be necessary. The suitable language is English, which already serves as India's common language. English is not merely India's common language but a world language.

English, the article argues, can instil a sense of freedom and sound knowledge among people. As Maraimalai Adigal said at the meeting on the eleventh, it was English that made Indians human, gave them a sense of freedom, made them rational and breathed new life into Indian languages. English greatly helped Indian languages take modern form. Every honest scholar of language will acknowledge this.

The Congress claim that English created a servile mentality among Indians is deeply ungrateful speech. The argument that Hindi will produce unity in India is mischievous and improper.

The source is damaged at this point, but criticises an official claim that anti-Hindi volunteers used obscene language. It says the claim serves as a false justification where no proper reason exists for applying the Criminal Law Amendment Act.

Yet no volunteer has been prosecuted upon an accusation of using obscene words. The Honourable Achariyar's statement therefore appears unsupported.

Even so, so that the public may understand the true position, Comrade E. V. Ramasamy has advised the anti-Hindi protesters not to picket in front of Achariyar's house. The house stands in an isolated place without public movement. The public may therefore suspect that Achariyar's allegation is true. If picketing before his house ceases, no room will remain for that doubt.

We trust the anti-Hindi protesters will follow Comrade E. V. Ramasamy's advice. Since picketing before a school would allow the public to learn the truth, they may picket there if they wish.

The source closes with a displaced newspaper sentence about an international crisis: whatever the ultimate outcome of the present emergency, the Hitler–Chamberlain meeting prevented the crisis from reaching its conclusion with extreme speed.

## English Translation

At Melpattambakkam in South Arcot, at six in the morning on Wednesday, 12 September 1951, the marriage of Venilar, second daughter of the revolutionary poet Bharathidasan, and Dandapani, son of R. Manickanar of Melpattambakkam, took place under the presidency of A. Ponnambalanar of Poovalur.

The poets Vanidasan, Dandapani, Karunandan, Sambandan, M. N. M. Pavalar, Murugaiyan, B.A., Panruti Nagalingam, T. Rasavel, Pondicherry V. Ponnambalam, and others [addressed or blessed] the bridal couple...

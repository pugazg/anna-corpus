## English Translation

...the Madras Government has issued instructions through the Hindu Religious Endowments Board that special prayers are to be conducted every day. Not only this: a report in *Dinamani* states that a Sri Rama yajna is being performed for rain at the Kesava Perumal Temple in Madras, and that devotees believe its effect will bring heavy rain and relieve the conditions of famine.

For this purpose, 108 Vedic scholars are said to be reciting the Ramayana, Mahabharata, Srimad Bhagavatam, Prabandham, and other sacred texts. Rama bhajans are reportedly being held alongside them. A sacred fire is maintained throughout the day while recitations are conducted. There are bhajans and religious discourses in the evening. Worship is said to continue from morning until the temple doors close at night. One hundred and eight gold marriage pendants have been prepared for the day of Sita’s wedding, and 108 pairs of gold sandals for the day of the coronation of Rama’s sandals. This is what is happening here!

Meanwhile, the Bombay Government is reportedly giving serious consideration to banning advertisements used to sell magical amulets with claims such as, “Diseases will be cured; success can be attained in everything; one can obtain whatever one desires,” on the ground that they deceive the public. Home Minister Comrade Morarji Desai mentioned this on 6 April 1949 while answering a question in the Bombay Legislative Assembly.

*Dravida Nadu, 10 April 1949*

## English Translation

At last, the arrogant authorities of Malaya hanged that young man. Comrade S. R. Ganapathy, who went to Malaya seeking a place to live, was hanged for the sole reason that he possessed one revolver and six bullets.

The power-mad authorities took a Tamil, a man who had committed no crime deserving death and had acted mindful of his duty to obey the law, branded him a criminal, placed him upon the gallows, and made the noose embrace that young man.

We appealed to the Sultan of Selangor to show mercy to a Tamil who intended no crime. Many appealed; Tamil Nadu appealed; the Indian Government appealed. Yet the life of a Tamil was destroyed upon the gallows of Malaya.

The power-mad rulers must not forget that the moment a Tamil who intended no crime was made a corpse upon the gallows, the Malayan sceptre became stained. Whenever they see that stained sceptre, Tamils living in Malaya and Tamils in the homeland will stand with hearts boiling in anger. Time will not fail to reveal the result.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 85.49)

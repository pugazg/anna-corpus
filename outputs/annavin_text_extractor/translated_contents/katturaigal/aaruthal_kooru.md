## English Translation

It had not been even two years since he accepted Mrs Thirisadai as his life companion. If her unexpected death plunged everyone who knew her into grief, need one say what Comrade Annamalai's condition must be?

It was a rare blessing that Mrs Thirisadai, endowed both with education and commitment to the Kazhagam's principles, became Annamalai's life companion. Delighted by the thought that the approaching anti-Hindi struggle would provide an opportunity to enjoy the full benefit of that blessing, the two of them attended the anti-Hindi conference held in Madras. When we reflect that nature made Mrs Thirisadai its permanent guest while she had eagerly awaited becoming a guest of the government, longing and sorrow surround us. To Comrade Annamalai, who has lost his companion and is submerged in a sea of grief, we offer our deeply sympathetic consolation.

*Dravida Nadu*, 1 August 1949.

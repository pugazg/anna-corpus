## English Translation

To mark the completion of the sixtieth year of the great scholar M. V. Venugopalan, a felicitation was held at Kanchipuram on 4-17-56. Mr T. M. Narayanasamy, Vice-Chancellor of Annamalai University, presided. Mr V. N. Perumal, chairman of the celebration committee and municipal chairman, presented on behalf of the people of Kanchipuram a purse containing Rs. 5,701. Mr P. T. Rajan draped the scholar in a golden shawl.

K. Venkatasamy, M. Radhakrishnan, Mylai Sivamuthu, Dr Devasenapathi, C. V. Thiruvengadam, K. Vachiravelu, Balur Kannappanar, Su. Vai. Duraisamy, and Pandithai Krishnaveni Ammal delivered felicitations. The event was conducted splendidly with the sweet music of Isai Arasu M. M. Dandapani Desikar.

Mr N. Sanjeevi, Tamil lecturer at Pachaiyappa's College, Kanchipuram, welcomed everyone. Mr A. S. Gnanasambandan presented the jubilee volume.

(*Seithi*, 1)-11-06; *Dravida Nadu*)

## English Translation

We were startled and stunned when we heard that Comrade V. T. Periyasamy, a man of the disposition that something must always be done, fell victim to death in Madras on 4-8-1950.

For many years he had been a prominent figure in Madras, taking a major part and rendering service in the Self-Respect Movement and the Dravidian Movement. At the very thought of Washermanpet, the figure of the late Periyasamy is the first to rise before one's eyes.

We convey our consolation to the comrades of Madras who stand grieving over the loss of a man of action; and we too share the sorrow of his family, who lie stunned and anguished by Periyasamy's death. May his fame live!

(*Dravida Nadu*, 13-8-1950)

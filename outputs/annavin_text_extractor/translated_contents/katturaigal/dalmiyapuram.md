## English Translation

The General Council welcomes the decision taken at the Lalgudi conference in Tiruchirappalli district that the indignity of naming a place in the Kallakudi region of Tamil Nadu "Dalmiapuram"—as a sign of northern capitalist domination and a symbol of humiliation that destroys Dravidian self-respect—must be removed.

It commends the committee formed for this struggle for coming forward to work in consultation with the General Secretary. The General Council calls upon party comrades to gather support for the agitation and make the struggle succeed.

**A. V. P. Asaithambi**  
**Nellai Veerabahu**

## General Secretary's Greetings

"This is not a struggle that can be dismissed by asking, 'What is in a name?' It is a question of the honour and dignity of Dravidian society. It is a warning we give against northern domination. I commend the conference that adopted this decision.

"If the government could understand the anguish in the people's hearts, it could change the name with a single line. But the present government is incapable of so wise a decision. That is why the struggle must begin.

"I greet the heroes of Tiruchirappalli who will take part in it. This righteous struggle must be conducted without disturbing the peace. I know that younger brother Karunanidhi is responsible for ensuring this.

"Gather support in every village and hamlet for the Self-Respect struggle that will begin by peaceful means. Make Delhi understand the consciousness of rights that has grown among us."

Thus spoke D.M.K. General Secretary Annadurai at the conference.

## Karunanidhi Sets Out for the Field

"I offer my heartfelt respects to General Secretary Anna and the comrades of Tiruchirappalli district who have given me this opportunity. Carrying the General Secretary in my heart, I have set out for the field.

"In that field, northern fury may fall upon us. It may cause grievous injury. It may claim me as a sacrifice. If I must be sacrificed, a host of warriors stands ready to follow me.

"Dalmiapuram: that name burns and scorches me. Whenever I pass the railway station, the name written there makes my heart boil. In the land of classical Tamil, should a place bear the name of an outsider? Should it bear the name of one who grows fat through exploitation? It must not. It must not.

"We shall change this name. We shall certainly change it. The righteous struggle will be conducted peacefully. But if the rulers cannot tolerate it and hurl weapons of repression, we are ready to bear those too. Let blood flow; let our blood be shed. We do not care. We shall take the blood that flows and use it to erase the name 'Dalmiapuram' written upon the railway board."

Comrade M. Karunanidhi, chairman of the agitation committee, made these remarks while thanking those who selected him.

## "We Shall Certainly Change It" — MLA Palaniyandi

"I have worked in Dalmia's factory. The northern capitalist Dalmia deceives our people, who labour until their bodies break, and carries away enormous wealth through exploitation. Our men and women suffer in the factory. There are neither adequate wages nor facilities. If they ask for them, they lose their jobs. Even recently, 125 workers were dismissed. In this way he exploits and carries away our wealth.

"Kallakudi is an excellent place for cement production. Gypsum, difficult to obtain elsewhere, is available here in abundance, but it does not benefit us.

"Why should such cruelty continue? Why should the name of a person who exploits us in this manner remain? I wholeheartedly welcome the plan to change the name Dalmiapuram. I will rally the workers and the people of my constituency in support. I shall also strive to obtain support from members of the Legislative Assembly.

"The present government does not care about the people's welfare. It behaves as though everything were play and amusement. Recently, about a hundred houses in Dalmiapuram caught fire and burned down. Every one was the home of a poor person. I told Rajaji, 'Please provide some government assistance to those who have lost their homes.' He merely replied mockingly, 'Why did the houses burn? They should have had tiled roofs!' He did not come forward to help the poor.

"I am ready to participate in the measures undertaken by the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam against the conduct of such a government."

## The Tiruttani Righteous Struggle — D.M.K. Support

In accordance with the resolution adopted at the State Conference on establishing states on a linguistic basis, the General Council condemns the northern-dominated government for acting partially and with the intention of provoking internal conflict within the Dravidian region by failing to include Chittoor district, which belongs to Tamil Nadu, within it.

It expresses pleasure and appreciation that comrades from North Arcot and Chittoor districts played their part in the righteous struggle conducted at Tiruttani in Chittoor district to establish this right.

To oversee further action in this righteous struggle, the General Council authorises the General Secretary, as a first step and in accordance with the views of the district branches of Chittoor, North Arcot, Madras, and Chengalpattu, to form a separate committee and prepare a programme of action.

This resolution was moved by comrade K. A. Mathiazhagan, seconded by comrade C. L. C. Krishnasamy, and adopted.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 7 May 1953)

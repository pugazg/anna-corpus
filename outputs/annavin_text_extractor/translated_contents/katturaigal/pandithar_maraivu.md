## English Translation

We were saddened to hear that Comrade Pandithar Thirugnana Sambandam, head Tamil teacher at Pachaiyappa's High School in Madras, passed away on the night of 4 May 1949.

Pandithar Thirugnana Sambandam was one of the veteran workers of the Self-Respect Movement. From the movement's founding day, he stood without fear and laboured for its principles. He was a courageous man who took the lead in working to organise the first Provincial Conference of Self-Respect Volunteers at Pattukkottai.

While holding a post as a Tamil teacher at a school, he spread Self-Respect principles among the people and played an important part in reforming many. The service he rendered to the Justice Party cannot be measured in words. When Comrade A. T. Panneerselvam was president of the Tanjore District Board, Thirugnana Sambandam stood beside him as a close support and accomplished great work.

After Pattukkottai, he worked for many years at Pachaiyappa's High School in Madras. He possessed firmness of heart and an unshaken spirit. He has now passed away.

May our words of consolation reach all the close family members, relatives, friends and comrades who grieve over his loss.

*(Dravida Nadu, 15 May 1949)*

## English Translation

You know that a committee has already been formed to celebrate at the end of September the completion of sixty years by T. M. Parthasarathi, a member of the DMK General Council who has participated in party work from its earliest days, gone to prison many times, and led a life of suffering.

I serve as chairman of the jubilee committee; Comrade M. G. Ramachandran as treasurer; and Comrades N. V. Natarajan, MLC, and K. R. Balan as secretaries. Committee members include Quaid-e-Millath Mohammed Ismail, MP; Navalar R. Nedunchezhian, MLA; M. Karunanidhi, MLA; K. A. Mathiazhagan, MLA; Sathyavani Muthu; A. Govindasamy; K. Anbazhagan, MLC; K. Manoharan, MP; K. Rajaram, MP; S. S. Marisamy, MP; M. G. Chakrapani; K. R. Ramasamy, MLC; R. M. Veerappan; V. Munusamy, MC; A. K. Thangavelar, MLC; C. V. M. Annamalai; and Mayor Chittibabu.

I therefore request party comrades and supporters to help conduct the celebration splendidly at the end of September and to provide financial assistance.

Address for sending funds:

M. G. Ramachandran  
Treasurer, T. M. P. Jubilee Committee  
160, Lloyds Road  
Royapettah, Madras - 600 014

Affectionately,

Annadurai

(*Seithi*; *Kanchi*, 22 August 1965)

## English Translation

Does it not tell us: "Do not remain with one who has no confidence in you. Do not work under his leadership. Go, go"?

When we heard of the incompatible marriage, we were ashamed. The statement Periyar issued in response intensified our anguish.

Here we are being driven from the fortress we built, from the field in which we laboured. The father drives out his children after finding a new heir.

Rendered destitute and overwhelmed with bewilderment, deprived of the fruits of our labour, we leave our own home, raging, withering, and crying aloud as we see the young woman who entered it seeking to wield authority.

From inside, he issues his order: "This lady is my wife and your leader. Do you wish to remain or not?" he asks.

After seeing this statement, the assembled party comrades reached the following decision.

## Periyar's Explanation Is Unsatisfactory

### Resolution at the Madras District Meeting

Many members of the Madras District Dravidar Kazhagam executive and the leading figures of the movement named below met at 8:30 p.m. on 28 June 1949. They declared that the statement Periyar published in *Viduthalai* on 28 June 1949 concerning his marriage was in no way satisfactory. Each individually expressed his view and firm resolve to oppose it and take the necessary steps against any further action in the matter.

It was further resolved that even if the leader convened a meeting of leading figures or a committee meeting to take vigorous action against Hindi, he should be informed that they could not cooperate unless he cancelled his arrangements concerning both his marriage and the movement.

### Those Who Attended and Signed

C. T. Arasu, Kanchi Manimozhiyar, M. Ilanchezhiyan, K. T. Venu, B. S. Mani, P. Thangarasu, S. Balasundaram, V. Sankaranarayanan, P. Govindarasan, M. S. Pandian, T. V. Muthusamy, S. P. T. Dravidamani, K. Govindasamy, S. Devasahayam, R. Pandian, V. T. Periyasamy, R. Angamuthu, M. Jayaraman, S. Kanniyappan, M. Dharuman, R. Madurai, P. M. Rasan, Perumal, R. Subbarayalu, S. Bhaktavatsalam, A. S. Raman, K. Chokkalingam, R. Kannabiran, S. A. A. Balraj, K. Sankaran, P. Ganesan, K. Sundaramurthi, M. T. Natarajan, and M. S. Muthu.

We inform the comrades of the organisation and the public that we regard this as an incompatible marriage; that both the marriage and the new arrangement he proposes to make for the party through it cause us to lose confidence in him; and that, so long as the organisation remains under his leadership, we cannot work in it or take part in plans framed in its name to stroke the emotions of young people, whether described as direct action against Hindi, picketing Marwari shops, or defying Section 144.

This marriage humiliates us. It brings reproach upon our principles. It places an indelible stain upon the movement.

We consider Periyar's excuse that "this is not a marriage but merely an arrangement made in the name of marriage" to be a mockery of our rational intelligence.

We believe the arrangement he says he intends to make for the party sounds the death knell for democracy and our self-respect.

We therefore declare that, unless he abandons these arrangements, we will not work in the organisation under his leadership.

We ask comrades of the movement to reflect and communicate their respective views.

Before undertaking efforts to remove his leadership or proceeding with any other work, we ask everyone first to announce that, as a sign of condemning Periyar's arrangement, they are standing apart.

Those who agree should write to *Dravida Nadu*.

Make it known that thousands refuse to accept this arrangement.

Protect your self-respect by telling the world that we will not follow even a leader if he acts contrary to principle.

As an answer to the ridicule of our enemies, make your position known. Show that we have declared that we will not accept his leadership if he does not abandon this improper arrangement.

Dispel the belief that anything he does will simply be swallowed and digested.

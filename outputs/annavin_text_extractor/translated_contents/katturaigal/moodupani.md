## English Translation

Across the world we see a renaissance. The fetters of slavery are falling away. Hindu India, however, clings to the Sudra varna with a crocodile’s grip. Brahminism! The Sudras thank you for this. We can see prominent men spending their time, thought, and wealth to protect Brahminism. It will bear fruit!

How cunningly the art and civilization of India’s native people have been destroyed in the name of religion! The degrading title “Sudra” has been imposed upon the country’s owners. They were divided into many castes and clans and shattered. Divisions among them have become so deep that it is now difficult even to gather and unite them. The high position that was rightfully theirs in the country has been taken away. Today they possess no political power. They have become slaves who are not even paid wages. Under the upper-caste doctrine that Sudras and women must not study, Sudras were deliberately kept illiterate. British rule brought them a little education.

Brothers and sisters! Rise to fight for your rights. Strive to obtain your political power. There are 150 million of you in India:

Ahir, Kurmi; Khatriya, Kachhi; Dhuria, Murai; Teli, Kumhar; Tamboli, Nai; Bari, Burji; Barhai, Halwai; Lohar, Thathera; Kahar, Mulhas; Sonar, Kori; Darji, Koiri; Lodha, Mali; Bhar, Chamar; Kalwar.

The time has come for these and many similar communities—150 million people placed in the category of Sudras apart from Brahmins, Kshatriyas, and Vaishyas—to wage a struggle for rights.

Our condition has now become extremely bad. Those called upper-caste Hindus have seized our rights. They have taken the share that ought to be ours. From the lowest attendant’s job to the highest official post, upper-caste Hindus have captured everything. Wealth, influence, office, authority, status, landed estates, and every other advantage are in their hands. Let anyone utter a single word against this monopolistic hereditary privilege and he will be denounced and driven away as a traitor, a fomenter of division, and a communalist.

We must therefore conduct a disciplined agitation and recover the rights we have lost. The separate movement of the people called the Depressed Classes has grown strong. As a result, the slavery called untouchability will soon be eradicated. We too must establish an organization and work to secure our rights.

Last month, the All-India Hindu Backward Classes Conference was held at Kanpur. The substance of the statement issued to explain its necessity has been summarized above. The existence in North India, just as here, of backward communities who are now gaining courage to begin a struggle for rights is an effect of the spirit of liberation visible everywhere in the country today.

The realization that forty crores of people being ruled by a man from a small island was an affront to self-respect has arisen. In the same way, people now feel that it is unjust for the country’s native and labouring communities to be ruled—and the harvest of their work reaped—by a priestly group that calls its doctrine of deceiving others “religion.” That feeling is spreading everywhere like a southerly breeze. This is the effect of the speed of the times. Those who try to suppress it are arrogant people who refuse to take counsel from reason.

The Hindu Mahasabha repeatedly makes a great noise: Muslims must be given no place; Hindu rule must be established; India belongs to Hindus; Hindus are the majority in the country. This fraud concerning the “majority” will not endure for long. Here are 150 million people who have risen in valour after describing the degraded condition that “Hindu rule” has allotted them.

Because Hindus live in mansions and have gained places in His Majesty’s Government, they can perpetrate this cruelty and exercise monopolistic hereditary privilege without question. Through their newspapers, they can prevent their cruelty from becoming known. They can persuade the public and deceive the government by asserting that there is no division or conflict among Hindus and that the country’s only problem is Hindu versus Muslim, with no problem at all within Hindu society.

There is an old saying that money flows even into the underworld. We may now coin a new one: falsehood spread by money flows all the way to Parliament. It is in this condition that the Hindu Mahasabha has reportedly resolved to establish branches in London and New York. Even if it spreads its absurdity throughout the whole world, truth must emerge one day, if not today.

Despite the heavy fog of propaganda, enthusiasm has arisen strongly enough for the Kanpur conference to be held. Nor is that all. At the Sankranti gathering conducted by the Hindu Mahasabha last week, members of the “Depressed Classes” reportedly entered and said, “We are not Hindus. We shall therefore not celebrate Sankranti, a Hindu festival. We are a separate people.”

The storm has therefore begun. Yet the offenders who ought to be punished escape solely because the British government is present to maintain peace. Not content with escaping, the revived religions are working to re-establish their dominance.

The awakening now appearing in every part of the country shows that the fog called Hindu religion will be scattered. If only the country’s youth help spread this awakening, the fog can be dispersed very quickly. Then we shall see the sun of freedom rise. In that light, the affliction of many crores of people will disappear, a new life will be born, and a new age will arise. Would that it may!

28 January 1945

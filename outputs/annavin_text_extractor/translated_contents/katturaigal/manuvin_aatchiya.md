## English Translation

The families of the oppressed communities living in the villages of Singampunari, Sekaram, and Arasanampatti in Tiruppattur taluk of Ramanathapuram district are being consumed in a fire of suffering. In the Singampunari area there are many Ambalakarars known as “Ilamugar.” Because the Adi Dravidars have remained their slaves until now, the Ambalakarars do not permit them to be shaved in barbershops. They deny them entry to coffee hotels, drinking-water ponds, and temples; continually beat and fine them; and subject Adi Dravidar women to cruelty.

They also fabricate false cases against these poor people. The police act in collusion with them, take the victims before the courts, and subject them to unjust expenses and harassment. What a pitiable state!

Only those poor people themselves know the suffering they endure. This is said to be a democratic government; the Congress government is said to labour for the Adi Dravidars. During Gandhi’s lifetime they gave them the name “Harijans,” claiming to work for the *Daridra Narayanas*. Once a year they carried oil and soapnut powder to them and performed “Harijan service.” That was all!

If the exalted ministers who call themselves the partners of the poor will not come forward to free them from their suffering, one cannot tell whether to weep or laugh. Ministers, at least now, must protect them through a public inquiry from the cruelties of the Ilamuga Ambalakarars and the police who join them in their conduct. Otherwise, they will perish...

## Translator's Notes

The second scan ends mid-sentence after “they will perish.” No continuation has been supplied. The printed community name “இளமுகர்” is retained conservatively as Ilamugar because its precise local referent is uncertain.

## English Translation

The Madras Government has fired its weapon of repression by demanding securities of Rs. 70,000 from *Viduthalai* and Rs. 3,000 from *Dravida Nadu*. We are pleased that some people outside the organisation too have recognised their duty, taken a principled stand, and come forward to condemn the government's conduct. We commend them.

Comrade P. Balasubramaniam, editor of the *Sunday Observer*, has issued a statement condemning the government's conduct. He has also said that Comrade C. R. Srinivasan, President of the All India Newspaper Editors' Conference, should raise the issue with the government and demand justice, and that a meeting of the city's newspaper editors should be arranged to consider the matter and reach an appropriate decision.

The editor of *Dinasari* deserves great praise for condemning the government's conduct in an editorial entitled "The Security Clampdown," dated 14 June 1949. The editorial states:

> "It is astonishing that those who ought to be the first to understand the difference between a self-rule government and a foreign government have still not understood it... Never before in the history of Tamil Nadu's press has such a large security sum been demanded from any newspaper. Our self-rule government has now achieved this unprecedented feat... In its frantic haste, the government has thrown a cloth around the neck and dragged, demanding, 'Bring Rs. 10,000.' No sense of responsibility is evident in this clampdown... What is visible is only the belief that their authority permits them to do anything they please... It is the blundering of such men devoid of wisdom, these supposed geniuses, that earns a bad name for both the Congress and the self-rule government... Therefore, the All India Newspaper Editors' Conference, associations, and federations that claim to protect press freedom must pay urgent attention to this. We request them to agitate until this order is withdrawn."

We also commend the editor of *Engal Nadu* for writing an editorial on 14 June 1949 condemning the government's conduct.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 19-6-49)

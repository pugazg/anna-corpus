## English Translation

Distinguished comrades who stride like lions along the path of sacrifice! July 15 approaches for the second year since the sound of gunfire was heard. On that day, many of our comrades, the enlightened people of the country, kept entering prison. Everywhere we turned, we heard the heroic cry: "Down with northern domination! We shall destroy the voice of arrogance! Dravida Nadu for the Dravidians!"

In a single day, July 15 made both the country and the press understand the sacrifice of our people and the anguish in the hearts of Dravidian youth. Six dear ones, Kallakudi Natarajan, Tiruchi Kesavan, Thoothukudi Uchimakali, Ganapathi, Srinivasa Pillai, and Manthiram Nadar, were torn from this earth by a rain of bullets. Comrade Kumaravel of Manapparai returned from the six months' rigorous imprisonment bestowed by Congress rule only to become a corpse; our eyes became pools of tears. More than five thousand heroes were in cruel prisons, while over a hundred lost hands or legs. A sight never seen, a horror never heard of! Yet without even the slightest violence, our young Tamil comrades demonstrated their willingness to sacrifice themselves for the principle held in their hearts.

Two years have passed since the organisation inscribed into history the words "three-front struggle." Congressmen describe Jallianwala Bagh as a field of atrocity that revealed British fury to the world. Yet under the rule of those same supposed martyrs, they created two fields of horror at Kallakudi and Thoothukudi. Baton charges and tear gas were unleashed. Orders by telephone, commands through a glance, and a signal delivered by declaring, "I will wipe them out": Rajaji employed countless such methods.

Alas, two years have passed. He is no longer in Fort St. George. But we remain in the people's forum today, just as we did then. He has descended from the seat of power, while the "seal of sacrifice" he gave us remains in our hearts. Dyer, who perpetrated Jallianwala Bagh, is no longer in the world; but that event will endure as long as the world's liberation movements endure. Likewise, as long as Dravidam lives, July 15 will live in history.

July 15 is the day that displayed the firmness of our hearts and our ability to finish what we undertake.

July 15 is the day that demonstrated how all those among our opponents who invite us to associate with them as "kinsmen" when circumstances demand actually regarded us.

July 15 is the day of ideals that showed the entire world that the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam is a fortress of heroes, an army marching valiantly towards the land of liberation.

Rajaji is absent from the seat of power today because he believed that a small spark could never become a great fire. The same trait appears to be a contagious disease of the seat of government, and we see its symptoms in Kamaraj too. The rationalist movement that possesses the medicine for this disease will not hesitate to provide the proper treatment for political obstinacy. For it holds a rare wealth possessed by no one else: a wealth that floods cannot destroy, blazing fire cannot consume, and giving away only replenishes rather than diminishes. That is the wealth of feeling belonging to youth who awaken courage.

On this July 15, our branches throughout the country will commemorate the day through meetings, processions, and leaflets that make this wealth known to those who wield governmental domination.

Two years have passed since July 15. The passing of more and more years does not mean that we have attained the final objective of the non-violent struggle we began that day. One stage has ended. Dravidam will have to undertake many more such stages. For among the diseases gripping Dravidam, the greatest is treachery. In the past, those who came brought treachery and gained advancement. Today, those who have risen by the same route continue along it.

To fight such forces of domination, we need greater strength and a stronger remembrance of sacrifice. Dignity and discipline must grow in each person. Responsibility and patience must fill every heart. Along with conveying this appeal to you all, *Dravida Nadu* offers its loving respects to the noble ones who gave their lives in the heroic struggle and to the children of the sacred land who bear the scars of sacrifice.

Long live the heroes! May their sacred work flourish!

(*Dravida Nadu*, news item, 18-7-25)

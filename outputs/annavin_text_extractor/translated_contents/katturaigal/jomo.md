## English Translation

It is said that no one is admitted easily into this secret society. They are reportedly trained for many days and undergo religious rites. Finally, as a sign that they have joined the Mau Mau movement, the mark 7 is branded upon their hands: 7, the sacred number!

If the movement’s leader declares that killing a particular person will strengthen the liberation struggle, members of Mau Mau consider the killing a sacred deed. If the police seek witnesses, no one comes forward. If anyone nods his head or moves his lips, the man is finished! No clue can be obtained in any murder. It is so disciplined and formidable a movement. Unable to identify its leaders, the white authorities now arrest in large groups everyone upon whom they have any suspicion. Repression has borne no fruit. Mau Mau refuses to die.

Jomo Kenyatta is the heroic leader of the liberation struggle to destroy white domination over Black people.

The land that belongs to the African people has today become a hunting ground for white imperialism.

Jomo Kenyatta labours to end this cruelty. Imperialism, seeing the immense influence he was gaining among the African people, tried all manner of things to suppress and crush him, but could not.

At last they accused Jomo Kenyatta of directing the Mau Mau movement and brought a grave case against him.

Kenyatta argued repeatedly that Mau Mau was a violent movement born because people could no longer endure the suffering caused by slavery, and that he had no connection with it.

The case brought against him drew the attention of the whole world.

In the end he was sentenced to a long term of imprisonment.

On the day judgment was to be delivered, imperialism feared that the people who loved Kenyatta would march there and rescue him. It therefore placed extremely strong security arrangements both inside and outside the court.

Jomo Kenyatta was thrown into prison.

The nationalism he kindled burns brightly in African hearts.

Progressive people throughout the world strongly condemn the acts committed by imperialism in the name of spending vast sums to eradicate the formidable Mau Mau movement.

Every African heart holds the faith that Jomo Kenyatta’s heroic sacrifice will not be in vain. Those who desire freedom proclaim: “Victory to Kenyatta!”

*Dravida Nadu Pongal Annual, 1955*

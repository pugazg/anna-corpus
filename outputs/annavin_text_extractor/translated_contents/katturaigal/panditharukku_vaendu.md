## English Translation

Declaring, "The whole of South India opposes Hindi. This is not a minority calculated merely by numbers; it is the opposition expressed by one entire region of the country," all those listed below signed and sent an appeal to Pandit Nehru asking that Hindi be abandoned.

The South will certainly honour their love of Tamil and their concern that the country's future should not be surrounded by danger. Whatever answer Delhi may give, the Tamil language owes its gratitude to all these people for uniting over a worthy issue and taking action without dismissing it merely because it was something we had already said.

The public-spirited signatories to the statement were:

1. C. P. Ramaswami Iyer, former Vice-Chancellor of Annamalai and Banaras Hindu Universities
2. K. Srinivasan, Editor, *The Hindu*
3. T. M. Krishnaswami Iyer, former Chief Justice of the Travancore State High Court
4. C. Rajagopalachari, former Governor-General
5. S. G. Manavala Ramanujam, former Vice-Chancellor of Annamalai University
6. S. Anantharamakrishnan, prominent industrialist
7. R. V. Krishna Iyer, former Secretary of the Madras Legislative Assembly
8. C. R. Srinivasan, Editor, *Swadesamitran*
9. A. N. Sivaraman, Editor, *Dinamani*
10. S. P. Adithan, Editor, *Dina Thanthi*
11. S. S. Vasan, Editor, *Ananda Vikatan*

14. V. L. Ethiraj, President of the Bar Association

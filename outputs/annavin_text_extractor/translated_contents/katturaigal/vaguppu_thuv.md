## English Translation

## The *Arya Mayai* Trial

### C. N. A.'s Statement

The *Arya Mayai* case being heard in the Tiruchirappalli court against comrades Annadurai and Kannappan was taken up for examination on 9 September 1950.

"There is no communal hatred whatsoever in the book I have written. I have neither the intention of stirring up communal hatred nor of inciting violent feeling against any community. I belong to a movement whose objective is that equal rights and justice should prevail in society. Therefore, in pursuit of that objective, I wrote the book to show how Dravidian society has fallen into disorder and to set out the means by which it may regain a sound condition. That book is itself a collection of articles I published in newspapers on various occasions.

"In *Arya Mayai*, I have presented ideas found in the works of historians such as Abbe Dubois, Sir John Marshall, Havel, and P. T. Srinivasa Iyengar. *Arya Mayai* was written solely with the purpose of explaining Aryan culture, Dravidian culture, the conflict between Aryan and Dravidian cultures, its consequences, and the present condition that has resulted from it. It contains no communal hatred. I therefore request that I be found not guilty."

Annadurai submitted a statement to this effect through his lawyer. On behalf of comrade Kannappan, an advocate named Ramanuja Ananthar and a professor were examined as witnesses.

The case has been adjourned until the 14th.

The hearing that day will take place at Tiruverumbur, near Tiruchirappalli.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 17 September 1950)

## English Translation

## To Begin on 10 August

The Dravidar Kazhagam has decided to embark upon direct action against Hindi. The first agitation will begin on 10 August. Comrade C. N. Annadurai will be the first commander of the anti-Hindi struggle.

The direct-action programme consists of picketing schools, organising volunteer corps, processions and public meetings, boycotting ministers, and otherwise enabling the people to express their feelings; should the government employ prohibitory orders to prevent these activities, those orders will be defied.

A resolution was also passed supporting the struggle for responsible government being conducted by the people of Hyderabad and strongly condemning the atrocities of the Razakars.

An emergency meeting of the Executive Committee of the Central Dravidar Kazhagam was held at 10:30 a.m. on Monday, 2 August 1948, at No. 1, Meeran Sahib Street, Mount Road, Madras. Committee chairman comrade T. P. Vedachalam presided.

The following were present:

Periyar E. V. Ramasamy, T. P. Vedachalam, K. K. Neelamegam, C. N. Annadurai, E. Thirunavukkarasu, P. Seethapathi, A. V. P. Asaithambi, A. Sithaiyan, Dr Dharmambal, Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar, C. T. T. Arasu, N. V. Natarajan, N. Jeevarathinam, C. Thangaraj, K. Govindasamy, A. Dravida Mani, S. Neethimanickam, C. Munusamy, V. Rangaraj, G. Parankusam, K. A. Maniammaiyar, and P. Palanisamy.

The following resolutions were adopted:

1. This committee supports the struggle for responsible government being conducted by the people of Hyderabad and strongly condemns both the repression carried out there against it and the lawless acts of the Razakars.

2. It was resolved to co-opt P. Palanisamy of Pethampalayam as a member of the Central Committee.

3. This meeting greatly appreciates and thanks the public for providing financial assistance after learning that the government had demanded security from the newspaper *Viduthalai* and after seeing Periyar's statement.

4. In accordance with the resolution of the Executive Committee held at Tiruchirappalli on 23 June 1948, President Periyar travelled through every district. This committee is pleased to note his statement reporting the feeling and support shown by the people.

5. This meeting commends the many thousands of men and women from various regions who have thus far demonstrated their wholehearted enthusiasm for participating in direct action against Hindi by signing and expressing their consent.

8. The government first declared that Hindi would not be compulsory in Tamil Nadu from Forms I to VI, but, at the instigation of a few people, has made it effectively compulsory from Forms I to III. This committee condemns that action and resolves that Hindi must not be made a subject in those forms either as an optional or as a compulsory subject.

Although the public has expressed this view for about a month and conferences and public meetings have adopted resolutions to that effect, the government has refused to accept it in any measure. Instead, it has deceived the public with many statements while persisting in its own position. The government has thereby compelled the Dravidar Kazhagam to enter direct action to change this policy. The Kazhagam has drawn up the following programme:

1. Picket schools.

2. Express public opposition through volunteer corps, processions, public meetings, boycotts of ministers, and similar measures.

3. Defy any government prohibitions imposed upon these activities.

4. An action committee has been formed to carry out these measures effectively.

As the first action, it is resolved that from 10 August a peaceful picket will begin in Madras, principally at Tondaimandalam High School or Hindu Theological High School, asking children directed to study Hindi not to attend those classes.

The committee appoints comrade C. N. Annadurai, M.A., to serve as commander of the first contingent and to inaugurate and conduct the picket.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 8 August 1948)

## Translator's Notes

The OCR heading prints the start year as 1949, while every dated meeting and the publication line place the resolution in August 1948. English gives the day and month without silently choosing the damaged heading year. Resolution numbering jumps from 5 to 8 in the source and is retained. “சர்வாதிகாரி” is a period movement title for the designated agitation commander, not a description of dictatorial government.

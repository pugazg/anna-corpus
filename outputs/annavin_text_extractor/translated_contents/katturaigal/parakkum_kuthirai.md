## English Translation

A helicopter capable of travelling faster than any other in the world and of being highly useful for transportation is now being manufactured in Britain.

Designed especially to carry heavy loads, this W-11 helicopter is called the “Flying Horse.” It is expected to begin its maiden flight in the coming spring.

Capable of travelling at 154 miles an hour, this helicopter can carry either twenty-four passengers or three tons of cargo.

A smaller helicopter called the W-12 “Flying Horse” can carry either twelve passengers or a load weighing one and a quarter tons.

A flying horse? What a wonder! Some people here will ask, “Do you know that our ancient manuscripts contain references to this?” But if asked why, despite such a reference being in the manuscripts, they never made a machine like this, they will merely stare in bewilderment!

*(Dravida Nadu, 28 December 1947)*

## Translator's Notes

The model designations W-11 and W-12, speed, passenger capacities and payloads are translated exactly from the scan-corrected Tamil source. “Flying Horse” renders the source nickname literally. The closing paragraph satirises claims that modern inventions were already described in ancient manuscripts.

## English Translation

The case instituted by the government against Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam General Secretary Annadurai, Deputy Secretaries Nedunchezhian and Mathiazhagan, and Executive Committee members Sampath and Natarajan began on the 3rd of this month and continued on the 5th. It has been adjourned to the 10th.

The proceedings are being held before Mr. M. A. Venkataramana Naidu, Chief Presidency Magistrate, Madras. Mr. Athanasius is arguing for the government.

Mr. N. A. Narayanasamy, B.A., B.L., is handling the case on behalf of the accused.

## Witnesses

The following were examined as witnesses for the government:

1. Mr. Kuzhandaivelu, Assistant Commissioner, Madras Special Branch.
2. Mr. Gopal Rao, Police Inspector and shorthand reporter.
3. Mr. Jagaraj, Police Sub-Inspector and shorthand reporter.
4. Mr. Padmanabha Iyer, Deputy Superintendent of Police, Tiruchirappalli.
5. Mr. M. Kuzhandai, Deputy Superintendent of Police, Thoothukudi.
6. Mr. Santhanakrishnan, Railway Police Inspector.
7. Tims, Washermanpet Police Inspector.
8. Mr. Sundaram Iyer, Saidapet Police Inspector.

It appears that two more witnesses may be examined on Monday.

The five persons involved in the case may each read statements in court denying the charges.

## Public Interest

Every day, large numbers of people gather outside the court awaiting the outcome of the case. Numerous police officers have been stationed both inside and outside the court.

Each day, leading members of the organisation and MLAs Comrades A. Govindasamy and S. Deivasigamani observe the proceedings. On the first day of the case, Comrade G. Sami, B.A., B.L., an advocate from Tirupattur, and Gokulakrishnan, an advocate from Madras, were present.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 98.53)

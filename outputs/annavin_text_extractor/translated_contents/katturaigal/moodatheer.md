## English Translation

We learn that the Chengalpattu District Board has decided to close the two high schools in the towns of Kovur and Karunguzhi, as well as the girls' high school in Madurantakam.

It is said that the District Board intends to close all three high schools because its finances are not in order.

At a time when everyone desires the fragrance of education to spread throughout the country, attempting to close educational institutions is not merely a deeply regrettable act; it is an act that dams and shuts off the stream of knowledge available to the public.

Rather than cite "lack of funds" as a reason and throw sand upon the education of children, it would be better to strive somehow to keep those educational institutions running. The Chengalpattu District Board is not without the means or opportunity to obtain the necessary financial support from those in power. After all, just like the rulers, those administering the District Board are also Congressmen. The District Board must therefore press its own government and obtain the assistance it needs.

Abandoning that course and thinking instead of closing even the schools that already exist would be harmful. We ask the District Board to devote itself to finding ways to keep the schools operating.

Do not close the schools.

Do not render a service to an uneducated society.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 17-6-5])

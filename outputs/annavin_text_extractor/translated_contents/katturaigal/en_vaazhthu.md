## English Translation

## Murasoli

Next only to music, its sound brings sweetness to Tamil ears and Tamil thought.

## Murasoli

The drum attained distinction in Tamil Nadu not merely as an instrument of war, but as an instrument of fame and as a means of announcing gifts.

Tamils would hear it and rejoice; opponents would be bewildered; and upon final victory, Tamils would celebrate.

To say that Tamils lived by sounding the drum does not mean that in those days they were forever possessed by war fever, seizing other countries and reveling in conquest. They did not refuse to respect the lives and rights of others. But they never tolerated anyone attempting to destroy their own rights, and they never failed to make those who tried to rob them of their honour bow at their feet.

## Murasoli

It proclaimed valour; it was never an instrument for displaying empty noise and clamour.

## Murasoli

Just as the instrument called the *murasu* displayed Tamil valour, victory, and advancement, the journal that may be called a fortress of ideas, filled with the artistry of younger brother Karunanidhi, has been performing with distinction the work of making us conscious of Tamil Nadu's excellence.

## Murasoli

The country knows well that the journal carries the sound of ideas, the sound of epics, the sound of poetry, and the sound of purpose.

## Murasoli

Through the journal, the feeling of liberation for the homeland is spread everywhere, and it serves as a training ground preparing the people for the liberation struggle.

## Murasoli

The annual is an ornament that reaches us every year.

## Murasoli

Each year, in many thousands of homes, people say with affection that it enchants everyone with its form and draws everyone with its wealth of ideas; that in bringing sweetness it surpasses the southern breeze, in spreading fragrance it surpasses jasmine, and in giving light it surpasses a necklace of pearls.

## Murasoli

The Pongal Annual is founded upon this character.

## Murasoli

The annual can provide the awakening needed by those who seek to remove harm and by those who wish to find a path by which the country may prosper.

## Murasoli

There is no doubt that the annual will be beautifully printed and prove worthy of a place in our hearts.

## Murasoli

Long may it live and grow. May younger brother Karunanidhi's service triumph and flourish!

## Murasoli

The annual should be present in every home of those whose character honours Tamil.

## Murasoli

I express my happiness at the publication of the annual and my appreciation to those who bring it out with added beauty. Through the journal, I convey my Pongal New Year greetings to everyone.

Yours affectionately,  
Annadurai

(*Murasoli* Pongal Annual, 14 January 1962)

## Translator's Notes

The Tamil repeatedly isolates “முரசொலி” as a refrain, playing simultaneously on the journal's name and the sound of the traditional war drum. English retains the journal name as each section heading while translating the wordplay in context.

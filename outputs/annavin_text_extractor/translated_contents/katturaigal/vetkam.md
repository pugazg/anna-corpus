## English Translation

The Madras Government has announced that it has increased the price of rice.

It has informed the public that the price has been raised by four paise per *padi*.

The rulers' statement declares categorically that this order will take effect from February 11.

This is a painful time when a chorus of "there is none" rises everywhere: no food to relieve hunger, no money in hand to buy the food that is available, and no opportunity to work and earn money.

The weaver's cry, the worker's anguish, and the poor person's wail resound everywhere.

At such a time, the Provincial Government has increased the price of rice by four paise per *padi*.

The reason given by the Provincial Government deserves everyone's attention.

The price is not being raised because a higher price would bring hidden stocks into the market, nor because holders of grain would come forward hoping to sell it for money.

Only the price rises. There is no indication that the benefit will increase with it.

Only the price grows; there is no assurance that grain will become readily available or anxiety disappear.

As always, people will have to struggle for rice. Rice will remain difficult to obtain.

Nevertheless, the government has raised the price.

More weight is placed upon the person already crying that he cannot bear the burden.

Listen to the reason for this price increase:

> "Because the subsidy formerly given by the Central Government has been reduced..."

That is all. The Central Government has stopped the subsidy it gave the provinces to keep prices stable. Therefore, the Provincial Government states that it has decided to make up the amount by "collecting it from the public."

It has issued this decision as though the public were reclining amid abundant luxury: "We shall collect it from the public!"

The moment "Mother India" washes her hands of it, they pounce upon the poor.

When the Government of India opens its empty hands, they spread their hands over the people's heads.

The Central Government, which scoops up tax money, pleads that it has no subsidy, and they turn towards the people.

We become victims of their false boast of "one India."

A shameless condition!

(*Dravida Nadu*, 18-2-5])

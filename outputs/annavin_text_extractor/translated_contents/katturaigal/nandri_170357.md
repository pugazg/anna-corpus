## English Translation

My dearly beloved,

Greetings. I convey my heartfelt gratitude to the dear comrades and elders who laboured day and night for my victory in the election held on 4 March 1957 in the Kanchipuram constituency of the Madras Legislative Assembly, and to the esteemed voters who supported me and secured that victory.

Having been judged worthy of your high confidence and elected to the Madras Legislative Assembly, I humbly and affectionately request the voting public of the Kanchipuram constituency to offer their cooperation and assist me with proposals for working towards the welfare of our constituency.

Accustomed always to remaining in touch with the public and rendering genuine service, I pledge that I shall work without ever forgetting the support given to me by the voters of Kanchipuram.

I also convey my gratitude to the thousands of eminent people of Tamil Nadu, and to Tamils living in Ceylon, Singapore, Saigon, Burma, and Malaya, who sent their affectionate congratulations by telegram and letter upon learning of my victory. I pledge to undertake service in a tangible manner for the common advancement of Tamils.

Greetings.

Affectionately,

Annadurai

(17-2-57; *Dravida Nadu*)

## English Translation

*Dravida Nadu* announces with heartfelt joy to the people of its land: the fourteenth year!

Parents feel their hearts swell when a daughter of the lineage, though afflicted by illness, fed only on gruel, and sheltered in a leaking hut, nevertheless comes of age, leaves behind baby talk, and gains articulate speech, with only the arrival of a bridegroom to place the garland upon her remaining. We too experience such delight.

Fourteen years: survival is a serious problem for journals that proceed with a single ideal as their sole objective. There is no need even to describe the condition of journals that rely only upon the poor and dispossessed and are conducted by people like them. In these circumstances, *Dravida Nadu* takes its first step into its fourteenth year.

When we think of what it has accomplished rather than the hills and hollows it has crossed, a boundless flood of joy rolls forth. Compare the country's condition thirteen years ago, when *Dravida Nadu* was born and began to crawl, with its condition today, and the awakening and feeling it has acquired become clear. Then, a dark forest; today, spreading light. The Dravidian movement has become one of the liberation movements of the South—indeed, of the world.

Across the country, and wherever good people are found, stands a great army of youth sharing our joys and sorrows in life. The party flag flies majestically everywhere. The intellectual movement marches heroically. In the people's forum, we have reached the stage of counting on our fingers the rhythm of the march towards the city of liberation. Our hearts rejoice to remember that during these thirteen years *Dravida Nadu* has performed the work within its power to bring about this rare sight.

Journals of communal awakening must be messengers carrying ideas of liberation. They also bear the great task of creating ever-new ideas. At the same time, rather than moving in a way that causes opponents to recoil in mental confusion and flee, they must cause those opponents too to approach the arena of our righteous struggle.

From the new faces the movement encounters, we understand that *Dravida Nadu* has served to the extent of its ability in this great work. That understanding is our joy. To see the work bear fruit is *Dravida Nadu*'s objective. It was with this worthy purpose that the journal began its march.

There have been many stages along the way when we trembled at the thought that its stride might falter. What can one do without fear in a society where everything is said to depend upon the grace of the Lord of Money? Yet the comrades who send seven rupees a year and become subscribers have shown great support, preventing this fear from lasting and immediately wiping away our distress.

At this happy moment, we express our gratitude to them. We also state that many more annual subscriptions of this kind must be added if journals devoted to an ideal are to live and grow. Only then can a journal continue its march without fear. We affectionately ask every subscribing comrade to assume responsibility for gathering support.

We know that many of our agent comrades suffer greatly at the hands of the Lord of Money. Even so, the enthusiasm they showed towards *Dravida Nadu* during the past year, greater than in previous years, and their generosity in promptly sending their lists deserve commendation. This must continue to grow; such growth is the path by which the journal itself will prosper.

As the country has entered a new condition, the responsibility and expense borne by journals have increased. Nevertheless, as always, *Dravida Nadu* will not hesitate to offer new ideas to the homeland. The journal stands ready, as usual, to welcome writer friends who come forward to cooperate in this work. We wish that their ranks may grow.

The fourteenth year: like us, countless friends throughout the country will take pride in it. Conveying our affection and respects to those elder and younger brothers, we take our first step into the new year. May the intellectual movement grow! Come forth, Dravidian land!

(*Dravida Nadu*, news item, date printed in OCR as 10 July 5௦)

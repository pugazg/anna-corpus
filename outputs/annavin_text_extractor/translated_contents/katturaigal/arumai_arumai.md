## English Translation

At Thirukkattupalli, after speaking in praise of Sir Sivaswami Iyer's service, the Honourable Srinivasa Sastri unveiled a portrait of Sir C. P. Ramaswami Iyer.

S. R. Balasubramaniam Iyer, headmaster of the Sivaswami Iyer School, delivered the vote of thanks.

Vaidyanatha Iyer.

Justice Varadachariar, Justice Patanjali Sastri, Sir Radhakrishna Iyer, T. T. Krishnamachari, Rao Bahadur G. A. Natesan Iyer, and Dr T. S. S. Rajan Iyengar sent messages of felicitation.

This appeared in *Swadesamitran*, edited by C. R. Srinivasa Iyengar.

Behold how a community protects its own!

(*Dravida Nadu*, 13-2-1944)

## English Translation

A man walking along a perilous path engulfed in darkness repeatedly looks up at the sky. Will the moon appear? Will a star give light? Nothing can be seen. He grows anxious and looks again. He sees a light: a star!

The air is stifling. Heat assails him and he sweats. He longs for a breath of wind. Suddenly, a gentle breeze arising from somewhere touches his body. A gentle breeze!

Like the star seen by one suffering in darkness, like the gentle breeze received by those tormented by heat, the Pongal new year, the festival of Tamils, comes once a year, wiping away distress and bringing relief. It gives joy: a delightful feeling in the heart, charm throughout the home, and liveliness across the town.

The parched condition disappears and green growth appears. Worry vanishes and joy begins to dance. Milk overflows and happiness overflows. Speech gains musical grace, the gaze gains tenderness, food gains a special taste, clothing is new, and feeling receives fresh vigour. People celebrate the festival.

On this sacred day observed with distinctive affection by the people of Tamil Nadu, offering greetings has become a tradition. Inquiring after one another's welfare and recognising the preciousness of friendship are traditions too. So is the thought: "Though a thousand worries fill our hearts, let us forget them for this one day and embrace happiness."

Following that tradition, through the *Murasoli* Pongal Special Issue, I convey my greetings to all the people of Tamil Nadu. May well-being abound! May action attain excellence! May every undertaking bring success!

I also remind you that all of us must perform our work well so that the country may prosper, the language may rise, virtue may flourish, affection may grow, and dignity may increase.

This sacred day demonstrates the greatness inherent in action.

It is a good day on which the world praises the nobility of labour.

On this day, we must examine how the country, government, society, thought, and action should be ordered so that those who experience happiness today may enjoy it every day. The method and path given by that reflection must become established in the heart, and we must act accordingly and achieve success.

This sacred day provides an opportunity for that purpose. The aim of my greeting is to present this appeal.

May you live! Enable everyone to live!

May our country live and attain every prosperity! May our language triumph, overcoming its many hardships! Long live the path of the *Kural*! Long live effective action!

(*Murasoli*, Pongal Special Issue, 14-1-68)

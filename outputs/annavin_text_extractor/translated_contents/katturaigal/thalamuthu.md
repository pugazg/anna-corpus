## English Translation

Yes! A rallying cry of "Thalamuthu! Natarajan!" is heard. The struggle has begun: a struggle against a wicked system of government! Dravidam rises with valour. The tyrants are fitting metal bands to their clubs, polishing their guns, and devising plans to enlarge the prisons. The soldiers of Dravidian liberation have taken leave of their parents, embraced and kissed their sweethearts whose eyes overflow with tears and tenderness, and set out for the field, crying, "Thalamuthu! Natarajan!"

The Acharya's government is ravenously hungry. It roars like a tiger roaming in a frenzy for blood!

For Dravidam and the Dravidian people to live, this system of government, which possesses the nature of a tiger and at the same time the cunning of a fox, must be resisted and destroyed even at the cost of shedding blood.

The Madras District Conference has sounded the war drum. The DMK executive committee has framed its resolution. The army is setting forth: an army of the good who will redeem the country! A war song rings out: "Come, come, comrades, so that Dravidam may gain prosperity!" A feast awaits the brave! The arrogant are bewildered! The Acharya's disciples grow weary and ask why the Acharya, who until now acted with political calculation, is this time setting his foot down without knowing the depth. Dravidian comrades! The drum resounds! The army is departing. Come and join, come and join!

There is Karunanidhi's call: "Come, let us make it Kallakudi! Dalmiapuram is a symbol of disgrace; come, let us wash it away even with our blood!"

And here is Sampath, proclaiming: "Let us set out to oppose and destroy this new Chanakyan scheme. Those with strength of heart, stand upright! Those devoted to the cause, join the ranks! Fear not! Victory is ours!"

The Dravidian people, enraged by Nehru's words of condemnation, have designated 15 July as the day on which trains and daytime services will be stopped throughout Tamil Nadu and a voice of protest raised.

Everywhere, in every direction, resistance is arising! We must regard the opportunity to ask what our part will be in this resistance as a rare privilege. It will be difficult for so stirring a climate of struggle to arise again. O people with hearts committed to a righteous struggle! O men of courage! Comrades who have understood the great truth that the motherland cannot live unless Aryan domination is destroyed! Here is the field. Here is the struggle. Here is the righteous struggle to redeem the nation. What is your part? Where is your part?

The motherland asks for an offering! The land of our fathers calls! "Break the fetters and come; come to free me from the grasp of the arrogant!" Dravidam, the ancient and glorious land that bore and raised us, calls! On one side stands the Brahmin leader with a mocking smile, asking, "Let us see how many will hear the motherland's call and take their place in the ranks!" Do you see the scene? Fix it in your memory.

Once, during the anti-Hindi agitation, the Acharya lay swollen with pride, thinking that the strength of the sacred thread would allow him to dominate this land. Two bulls named Thalamuthu and Natarajan died in prison. His arrogance was broken; the government fell; Hindi was driven out! That is why the fighting force now sets forth with the rallying cry, "Thalamuthu! Natarajan!"

The struggle to redeem the country has begun.

When the struggle to redeem the country is underway, houses and property, wife and child, occupation and comfort: we do not belong to these!

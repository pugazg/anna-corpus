## English Translation

We are saddened to hear that Madras Minister Daniel Thomas, who had been ill for some time, passed away at his home in Mylapore on the night of 15 June 1948, aged fifty-nine.

Comrade Daniel Thomas possessed administrative ability. He served for ten years as chairman of the Palayamkottai Municipality and for six years as vice-president of the Tirunelveli District Board, administering them well.

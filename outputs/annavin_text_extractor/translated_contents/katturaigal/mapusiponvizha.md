## English Translation

...said M. Po. Si. while responding to the tribute paid to him. His voice revealed his attachment to and enthusiasm for public life. His golden jubilee, celebrated at Annamalai Hall on 27 June, demonstrated that the public will honour anyone who works honestly in public life for a definite principle.

At this splendid celebration, organized through the efforts of Nadigachelvar T. K. Shanmugam and others, Salem Venu presented him with a purse containing 50,000 rupees. Kalaivanar N. S. Krishnan presented him with a new car. Munas gave him a tape recorder, while many others offered various gifts. Comrades P. Jeeva, Devan, P. T. Rajan, Dr Mu. Va., S. Gurusami, The. Po. Mee., Annamalaiyar, M. G. R., K. R. R., S. D. Sundaram, G. Umapathi, A. P. Nagarajan, and many people from different fields participated and offered their praise. A landowner from Tiruchirappalli donated an acre of land to M. Po. Si.

The celebration became an occasion for people from many walks of life to participate and express their affection. We too convey our affectionate greetings and appreciation to M. Po. Si. Long live M. Po. Si.! May Tamil virtue flourish!

*News item, Dravida Nadu, 1 July 1956*

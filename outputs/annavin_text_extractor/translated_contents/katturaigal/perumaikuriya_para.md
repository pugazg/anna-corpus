## English Translation

The splendour of the greetings and appreciation bestowed by a people's assembly is altogether different from that of awards granted by a government.

A government may confer titles and offices upon its favourites! There may be pageantry in it, but far more distinguished are the greetings joyfully offered by the people's assembly.

Under the government that dawned with independence, they claim to honour poets and people of the arts while doing all manner of things for all manner of people! Under Delhi's rule, pebbles are prized while doors are closed against shining diamonds. Yet the people's assembly has not forgotten the true diamonds.

An example is the tribute paid to Kalaivanar in Madras on 13 November, when he was draped in a golden shawl.

Kalaivanar! King of Comedy! The words alone are enough: even a small child recognises him clearly and runs forward to praise him with delight. Our N. S. Krishnan has secured such an enduring place.

His era in the world of art brought about something new! At a time when clowning and acrobatic antics were regarded as comedy, Kalaivanar earned the foremost distinction of introducing good ideas among the people even as he made them laugh.

He has acted in more than 102 films. The fame and distinction he gathered through them are all that remain with him today! Regarding it as supremely important to help his comrades and wipe away the tears of those in distress, he spent a large portion of his income. The educational institutions, students, actors' families, and worthy charitable causes that have benefited from him are countless! Countless!!

He is the sovereign of the arts! That is why the one-sided conduct and blackout imposed by this country's hard-hearted men could not reach him. Instead, transcending them all, he occupies an unchanging place in the people's hearts. We wholeheartedly commend the South Indian Artistes' Association, which worked with the good purpose of honouring such a great man, a good man, and a philanthropist.

At the ceremony, Professor R. P. Sethu presided; Tiruchi Loganathan sang the invocation to Mother Tamil; and South Indian Artistes' Association president M. V. Sundaram welcomed everyone. The portrait of the late theatrical poet T. K. Sankaradas Swamigal was unveiled by Madurai Isakkimuthu Vathiyar.

Speaking about the dramatic poet's rare ability were comrades Thanjai Ramaiah Dass, P. T. Sambandam, K. Sarangapani, T. K. Shanmugam, dramatic scholar P. Sambanda Mudaliar, and M. S. Muthukrishnan, all of whom had become distinguished figures of the stage and now shone as writers and actors in the cinema.

Professor Sambanda Mudaliar then draped our sovereign of the arts in a golden shawl. The actors showered him with flowers! Through their applause, the flood of people poured down a shower of love! Addresses of felicitation and gifts of affection were presented.

Nadigavel M. R. Radha presented gold coins; Jupiter Pictures presented silver coins; and the master of sweet music, M. K. Thyagaraja Bhagavathar, presented silk garments to Kalaivanar and his wife, Madam Madhuram. Many others also made presentations. On behalf of his theatrical company, Nadigamani T. K. Shanmugam donated Rs. 4,001 to the Artistes' Association.

Revolutionary Actor M. G. Ramachandran thanked everyone who had attended the celebration.

Even if the government and those who make it dance to their tune do not welcome and honour our treasures, let us celebrate and cherish them with joy. May the association continue to work in that spirit.

Kalaivanar Krishnan and his companion, Madam Madhuram, have received a rare blessing. Those who move in the political sky and those who journey in the firmament of research...

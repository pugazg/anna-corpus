## English Translation

Stage actor and present-day film star “Sivaji” V. C. Ganesan accepted Comrade Kamalavalli as his life partner at Swamimalai on the first of May. Comrade Rathinasami, a Tamil teacher from Tiruchirappalli, presided over the ceremony. He read the passages in the *Tirukkural* in which the great Valluvar speaks of domestic life and gave an address urging the couple to conduct their lives accordingly. Comrade M. Karunanidhi delivered a speech congratulating the couple.

The owners of National Pictures, A. V. M. Studio, and Wellington Talkies in Tiruchirappalli; directors Krishnan-Panju; Chidambaram musician C. S. Jayaraman; S. V. Sahasranamam; the Pandari Bai sisters; T. K. Krishnasami of Sakthi Nataka Sabha; and many others presented the couple with numerous silver articles. Many people read and presented messages of congratulation. A great many telegrams had arrived praising the reform marriage.

Those attending the ceremony included film-actor comrades M. G. Ramachandran and S. V. Sahasranamam; directors Krishnan-Panju; National Pictures proprietor P. A. Perumal; Vellore Natarajan; musician C. S. Jayaraman; T. A. Mathuram Ammaiyar; Natesan of Natesa Art Productions; actor friends from Sakthi Nataka Sabha; Comrade T. K. Krishnasami; members of our movement Arangannal, Kannadasan, and Thanjai Shanmuga Vadivel; and many other actor comrades.

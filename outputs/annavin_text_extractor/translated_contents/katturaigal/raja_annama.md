## English Translation

We are saddened to hear that he died at the age of sixty-seven in his mansion in Chettinad last night.

Raja Sir performed many services through his involvement in public life. Foremost among them is Annamalai University.

We offer our condolences to his sons, Kumararaja Muthiah Chettiar, Ramanathan Chettiar, and Chidambaram Chettiar, and to his other relatives who mourn his passing.

*Dravida Nadu, 20 June 1948*

## English Translation

*Bharathidasan*

Hindi is to dominate Tamil Nadu;
people of the land, come one and all!
After harm has come to classical Tamil,
what profit is there in retaining this body?

Wondrous Tamil is our language;
we shall call it the very soul of valiant Tamils!
Those who grant privilege to Hindi,
we shall spit upon those base men!

From the day this earth first appeared,
our delightful Tamil language has existed, behold!
Those who committed wrong have never prospered here;
why such arrogance in these upstarts?

From which direction will Hindi enter?
How many armies will it bring?
We shall call that Hindi a paltry thing
and bury its domination.

Our bodies, possessions, and every breath of life
we shall give to our delightful Tamil language!
We shall declare that even the pleasure a woman gives
cannot equal our great Tamil!

Young bulls called lions,
grow fiercely resolute in the country!
If harm is done to the mother tongue,
we shall offer the fresh blood of our bodies!

Death is like sleep;
to sleep and wake again is our birth!
We shall oppose harmful Hindi,
never counting the sweetness of life!

The flower grove where the mango cuckoo sings
is the prison that thinks it can confine us!
We shall not let Mother Tamil languish
until life departs this body!

(Poem published on the front page of *Dravida Nadu*, 4-7-48)

## English Translation

We have offered nine flowers to Dravida Nadu.

Today the tenth bud has begun to bloom.

In the grove of time, it is a tree without a taproot, yet it has carried out its task without fail. Storm and thunder have not prevented its growth.

When the support and affection of the Dravidian land serve as manure and water, what cause is there for worry?

The world of newspapers is a spinning top today. Those who possess money are the strings that set it spinning.

We are only a drop within the company of those who "trust themselves." Yet we did not hesitate. Buds did not fail to appear, and our work did not fail to grow.

The life of *Dravida Nadu* has known no gentle breeze. Nevertheless, it has established a greatness that does not lose heart.

Some call us "honey"; others recoil and call us a "scorpion." Yet we have not been afraid. We stand proudly upon the step of the tenth year.

We do not possess the affection of those who rule. Instead, we have a government that gives us arrows. Spear thrusts meant to make us tremble, imprisonment, and repression continue to be presented as our gifts.

Yet no obstacle upon our path has stopped us. Even when we were obstructed, the strong shoulders, courage, and loving hearts of the Dravidian people did not permit us to be halted.

More comrades now receive the fragrance of our flower than last year. That is joyful news. May their numbers grow. As our intellectual work develops, may their support accumulate.

Our task is to transform a sewer into a sandalwood grove.

Therefore, agitation and troubles neither touch nor pierce our hearts.

*Dravida Nadu* dedicated itself to rationalist work. It therefore moves among us as a soldier singing a martial hymn.

The fruit of that work is visibly apparent in Dravidam.

The ranks of the army have grown. Those who frightened, threatened, and bullied us are now all within our ranks.

This sight grew greatly during the ninth year just ended.

Our greetings to this new condition.

"We shall perform any task. Our goal is to spread the principles of our organisation." During the middle of the year, we fulfilled this desire too. For several months, articles and stories did not hesitate to render their service as a voice.

*Dravida Nadu* contributed whatever cooperation it could to the success of Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam conferences. Disregarding the lack of rest, it also offered an annual special issue to the organisation's comrades.

The office comrades who labour day and night so that the flower may spread its fragrance deserve the appreciation of *Dravida Nadu*.

Our agent comrades are the channels through which *Dravida Nadu* gains prosperity. But how delighted would we be if some among them, departing from their usual practice, looked towards the end of each month and performed their duty properly? May their minds bear this resolve at least this year.

Subscribers are the eyes of *Dravida Nadu*. On this joyful day, we scatter our flowers of affection upon them too.

The desire to increase the journal's pages and serve in an even better manner often surges within us. But Congress rule shows no sign of resolving the paper shortage. We desire that such a condition arrive very soon. When it does, comrades will receive more enjoyable sections.

We convey our gratitude to the writer friends who supplied articles and stories this year.

Through the nine-year journey of *Dravida Nadu*, many fine Tamil writers have come to flourish. But their number must increase further. Young people generally, and college youth particularly, must become involved in the means of achieving that.

The tenth year: the very thought makes the heart rejoice.

At this moment, the affection of every comrade in our movement shines within our heart.

With the pledge that we shall labour to become worthy of their affection and strive to spread elevated ideas, we convey our loving respects to everyone.

Our duty is simply to labour and remain at our task!

(*Dravida Nadu*, 24-6-51)

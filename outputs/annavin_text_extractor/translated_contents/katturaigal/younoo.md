## English Translation

The Prime Minister of Burma is, according to the dictionary of certain political figures here who have been refined in the furnace, "a fellow who lives by writing!" For he has apparently written excellent plays and storybooks, and has also rendered books from other languages into Burmese.

There are virtuous people here who complain that writing in this fashion is an offence; writing beautifully is a grave offence; writing with flavour is an intolerable offence; and writing in a manner welcomed by the people is an unforgivable offence. In Burma, a writer and playwright is also Prime Minister. There is no hero there to pounce upon him for it!

(*Seithi*, 10 July 1955; *Dravida Nadu*)

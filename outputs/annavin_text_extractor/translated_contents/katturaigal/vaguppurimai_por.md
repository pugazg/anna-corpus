## English Translation

Mr. K. Anbazhagan, M.A., a professor at Pachaiyappa's College, Madras, has given Tamil Nadu an excellent book entitled *The Struggle for Communal Representation*. We are greatly pleased by the worthy endeavour of our friend Anbazhagan, who has been praised as one of Tamil Nadu's gifted orators. At this moment, when the demand for social justice, cast into a death pit by those crazed with domination, is struggling to regain life, his book gives us a well-documented history of social justice.

With statistics, he has presented how the demand for social justice arose, the details of its growth, the story of its decline, and related matters.

Moreover, the explanation he gives of why the cry for social justice arose and why non-Brahmin society yearns to preserve it is like the pupil within the eye.

The excellence of this book tells us that he has devoted a long period of time to this rare work.

It is not enough for this valuable book merely to adorn every home; it must also be installed in people's hearts.

We commend our friend's worthy effort. The Makkal Mandram of Coral Merchant Street, Madras, has published it in a most excellent manner.

This book is a fine mine of knowledge that explains the growing thunder of the demand for rights.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 19-6-51)

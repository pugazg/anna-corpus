## English Translation

## Madras

On 15 September 1948, fifty-one people were arrested in Madras and sentenced to three weeks in prison.

## Tiruvannamalai

Forty-two people were arrested.

## Thoothukudi

Ninety-five people were arrested.

## Madurai

Twenty-one people were arrested.

## Tirukkoyilur

Twenty-eight people were arrested.

## Polur

A baton charge took place. A prohibitory order under Section 144 has been imposed for one month.

## Vandavasi

The picketing proceeded peacefully. Ninety-eight students left school in the morning and 133 in the evening.

## Musiri

All twenty-one picketers were arrested.

## Virudhunagar

Twenty-one volunteers were arrested.

## Kanchipuram

Forty-three volunteers picketed. In the morning they covered Anderson High School and Pachaiyappa's High School; in the evening, Pachaiyappa's Branch School and the Municipal School.

## Salem

The picketing proceeded peacefully at six schools.

## Tiruchirappalli

Picketing was conducted at three schools.

In most towns, the comrades who picketed were not arrested. In every town, the token anti-Hindi picketing proceeded in a thoroughly peaceful manner.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 19 September 1948)

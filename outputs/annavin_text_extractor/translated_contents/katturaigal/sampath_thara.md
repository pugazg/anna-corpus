## English Translation

Our Member of Parliament E. V. K. Sampath met Sikh leader Master Tara Singh in Delhi on 3 February. Tara Singh, the venerable elder who has been fighting Hindu religious fanaticism and northern Hindi fanaticism, was reportedly very pleased to converse with our Sampath. When Sampath explained the growth and aims of our party, Tara Singh listened with enthusiasm.

Tara Singh said, "I occasionally obtain a little information about your party's activities from journals," and expressed the wish that our leaders should visit Punjab.

While Parliament is in session, Sampath is said to be meeting many such leaders, conversing with others, and explaining who we are and what our ideal is. MPs from other states reportedly show great eagerness to learn about us through Sampath. Consequently, in Delhi there is growing demand and desire to see our English journal, *Home Land*.

(*Seithi*; *Dravida Nadu*, 16-2-28)

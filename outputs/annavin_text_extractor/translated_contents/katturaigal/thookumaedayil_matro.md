## English Translation

Tamil Nadu, our homeland, is throbbing with anguish. Before that trembling of the heart has subsided, another cruel and sorrowful piece of news has reached our ears. Another Tamil named Sambasivan has been taken and made to stand near the gallows in Malaya. The lust for power is waiting once more to drink the life of a Tamil!

Under Malaya’s emergency law, Comrade Sambasivan was arrested and sentenced to death on the charge that he possessed a revolver and explosives.

He was arrested while lying injured after being attacked by Malayans. Once he recovered in hospital, proceedings were brought against him under the emergency law, and at the first trial he was found not guilty. The judge, however, ordered a retrial, and it was at that retrial that he received the death sentence. He now stands near the gallows awaiting its execution.

The Government of India is asking the Government of Malaya for justice. Dravidam waits in anguish to learn whether justice will be denied or granted! Seething over Ganapathy’s death and inflamed by Veerasenan’s murder...

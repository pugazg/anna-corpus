## English Translation

As compensation for the damage caused during the recent world war, the Allied countries devised a plan to obtain a large sum from Germany and Japan. They met and discussed it many times, prepared statistics, and recorded how much compensation the Madras Presidency was to receive under the arrangement. Provision was made to receive either money or machinery of equivalent value.

Three years have passed since this arrangement was discussed, yet not even half a paisa has arrived. This too is an example of the rulers' administrative ability. If excellent scientific equipment needed for industrial growth had been obtained from Germany and Japan, it would have produced immense benefit. But because the matter has proceeded at a tortoise's pace for three years, neither money nor goods in its place have been obtained.

In October 1945 the central government reportedly sent a telegram asking the Madras government how much the Presidency sought as compensation. Statistics were immediately prepared here, and Delhi was informed that the Madras Presidency should receive five and a half crore rupees for the damage caused by Japan. It was also stated that, if this compensation could not be paid in cash, modern machinery useful to the textile mills here would be welcome instead.

That happened in 1945. The year 1948 is nearing its end. Not even half a rupee of the five and a half crores has arrived, nor has a single kind of machine been received. See how briskly and efficiently "our" government works, and how much concern the Delhi government shows for our Presidency. Yet in this splendid state of affairs there is even a dedicated aeroplane for travelling to and from Delhi. Why are they so sluggish? Imagine the industrial growth that could have occurred if valuable machinery worth five and a half crores had been obtained and used. For three years they have tied up the account book and thrown it into a corner.

*Dravida Nadu*, 12 December 1949.

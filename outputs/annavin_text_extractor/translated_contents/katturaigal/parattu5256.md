## English Translation

Even the pundits who visit the Kumbh Mela and the Kumbakonam “Mahamaham” say that castes must be abolished: such is the appeal of that declaration. Whenever the leaders who rule the country speak “heroically” to foreign leaders, the latter glance sideways at the slum and the agraharam here, and at the separate justice prescribed for each caste, and laugh inwardly.

The number of people who claim not to oppose inter-caste marriage is very large and continues to grow. Yet the number of those who actually enter into inter-caste marriages is exceedingly small.

In this country, which is afflicted and ruined by caste, those who enter into inter-caste marriages perform a great revolutionary and rare act that changes the times; they create a new society. The government itself must recognize this, commend those who perform such rare acts, protect them from social opposition and bitterness, encourage others to develop the worthy desire to renew society through inter-caste marriage, and offer concessions.

They say this should be sweet as sugarcane to Kamaraj, for he too speaks at caste-abolition conferences.

Nevertheless, Bihar has now secured the opportunity to undertake this valuable work and show the way.

“If a person belonging to a ‘higher caste’ marries a ‘lower-caste’ woman, whether the former is a man or a woman, an immediate grant of Rs. 2,000 will be made. When a child is born to them, a further Rs. 1,000 will be provided.

“If a person belonging to a ‘higher caste’ marries a tribal person, the same concession will be provided.”

## English Translation

The circumstances surrounding the murder of comrade Velayudham of Udayarpalayam are now causing great agitation throughout Tamil Nadu. The police concerned officially established that comrade Velayudham had been murdered, warrants were issued against several people implicated in the killing, and some were arrested. Others who had gone into hiding surrendered to the Tiruchirappalli District Magistrate and were released on bail.

In this situation, the Tiruchirappalli District Magistrate instructed the police officers concerned to withdraw the case. The case was withdrawn. Once this became known, it caused tremendous agitation across Tamil Nadu. Resolutions condemning the withdrawal have been passed in many towns and sent to the government.

The government has stated that it will examine all records connected with the matter and take appropriate action.

It is now reported that the Tiruchirappalli District Magistrate who caused the case to be withdrawn has been transferred elsewhere. We do not know what mystery lies behind this.

Comrade Velayudham may have been a Self-Respecter or may have belonged to the Dravidar Kazhagam; that is a separate matter. When a person has been murdered, justice requires that those responsible be tried according to law and judgment delivered. After it had been established that comrade Velayudham was murdered and several people had been arrested, we cannot see how it was just to order the case withdrawn without a lawful trial.

The newspaper *Dinasari* has written editorials about this murder, calling upon the government to investigate the case in accordance with justice and integrity and condemning the District Magistrate's decision to have it withdrawn. We commend *Dinasari* for demonstrating that justice must be the same for everyone, thereby awakening the public and warning the government.

From the very day comrade Velayudham was murdered, *Viduthalai* has written about the matter and insisted that the government remain impartial and render justice.

The Congress Party, which declared that democratic government meant establishing justice in the country and paving the way for integrity, has today assumed the great responsibility of governing. We expect justice and integrity, and we remind the government that this case has become a test by which it can prove that acts contrary to justice and integrity will no longer occur in the country.

The Chief Minister has issued a statement saying that appropriate action will be taken. We therefore expect that justice will be established and integrity observed in this case, and that the Chief Minister will do what is necessary in connection with it.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 11 July 1948)

## English Translation

Tamil Nadu's Congress friends are very fond of the song, "Spin, O spinning wheel! Come loose and spin, O wheel!" Their wish and programme of work is that the spinning wheel should turn in every home. The reason for their enthusiasm for the wheel, sufficient to frame such a programme, is Gandhi's teaching. But what is the condition of North India, Gandhi's own abode and the home of his foremost disciples? We have shown many times that the mill magnates there are growing fat. That has not become an old story. Here is a new chapter.

Bombay's capitalists have entered into an agreement with a foreign company. Is it for the khadi programme? No, it is to destroy that programme in North India itself. The Bombay capitalists have contracted with a company in Rhode Island to send them modern mill machinery worth one crore rupees over three years beginning in July 1948. The company is American-owned. The Bombay capitalists, who already dominate the mills, are buying machinery worth one crore rupees to expand the mills there. Here, meanwhile, the ministers sing, "Spin, O spinning wheel! Turn and spin, O wheel!" Mills in the North; spinning wheels here!

*Dravida Nadu*, 5 July 1947.

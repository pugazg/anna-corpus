## English Translation

However much poverty, longing, and sorrow
may assail us here,
on the Pongal new year alone,
which we celebrate as our fitting sacred day,
we shall bathe in fresh water, wear new clothes,
and conduct the festival with overflowing joy!

Why is that?

Because we desire intensely
that the virtue of honouring the dignity of labour
should spread throughout the world!

Why did they honour the wealth-giving sun?
Because it nourishes living beings!

Why do we honour the greatness of farmers?
Because those who give food give life itself!

Why did we honour cattle?
Because they lovingly give sweet milk!

Therefore, this festival
is a good festival for expressing gratitude.

> Even one who destroys every other good deed may find redemption;
> none exists for the person who destroys gratitude for a kindness done.

Thus did our Valluvar clearly declare.

The path of the *Kural* must spread across the world!
We must demonstrate it by walking the way of the *Kural*!

The *Kural* does not tell us,
"Go away into a dark cave or forest."

To attain dignity it says:

> Learn thoroughly what deserves to be learned;
> after learning, live in accordance with it.

Behold what it teaches!

Let us learn the lesson shown
by forest and field, plough and bull!

Let us understand the principle of farming
that removes weeds when they sprout.

Having understood it,
let us take what is good and reject what is bad,
labouring every day so that our noble country
may attain a condition praised by all the world!

A festival is indeed one way
to remove the weariness caused by labour.

The festival's abundant happiness too
exists so that the dignity of labour may rise.

The rising sun never says,
"Yesterday I gave excellent light;
today I am going to rest."

Like the sun,
let us continue labouring so that the world may prosper!

Those noble people who continue
to perform such excellent work,
I greet them, I greet them,
with a heart full of fulfilment!

Affectionately,

Annadurai

(*Dravidan*, Pongal Special Issue, 14-1-1968)

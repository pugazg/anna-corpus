## English Translation

## America Is Restless; Can India Sleep?

The world says peace; America says atomic bomb. Good people say that war must never occur. Yet America casts out the perverse declaration, "I shall drop the atomic bomb."

America is a poisonous spider. Through its web of dollars, it traps many countries within its imperialist nest and ruins them. Its threat today to drop an atomic bomb upon Korea is the full revelation of that monstrous form.

Science produces many wonders, yet we have reached a condition where they are scarcely used to bring prosperity into human life. Things serving constructive power are very few, while thousands upon thousands of objects serving destructive power have been harvested as the fruit of modern science.

How excellent it would be if knowledge were employed in constructive work. That must not be forgotten, the great and worthy purpose must not be buried, and a course that performs a dance of cosmic destruction must not be followed.

Science is a growing art and has given many varieties of marvels. But it has also given terrifying weapons. That is not a defect in science; the cause is the strange course of the human mind that employs it.

Puranas say that the gaze of the destroying god's third eye will reduce the world to ashes. Vendors of words go about declaring that the age of deluge itself will arrive. Yet with the aid of science, humanity has invented the atomic bomb, capable of destroying even the destroying god.

From the moment the atomic bomb was invented, the world began speaking about it. When it was dropped upon Japan in the last world war, the extremity of its consequences became clear. That is why the nations of the world participated with intense concern in discussions about the atomic bomb.

All who possessed a heart laboured to bring every effort to use and expand it under a common organisation and to save the world from that great danger. Yet America boasts that it is going to drop it.

As the saying goes that the world always contains one strong enough to overcome the strong, Russia too has reportedly entered the effort with full force, as though saying, "If you have an atomic bomb, behold the caustic bomb."

What horror! What frenzy for war!

India wishes to live under the shadow of American imperialism. Prime Minister Nehru too finds delight in America's embrace.

A bomb upon Korea, and that too a dreadful atomic bomb. Yes, a great crisis would arise in Asia. Prime Minister Nehru is the light of Asia and the lamp of the entire world. How will he avert the great danger approaching Asia? India is the queen of Asia, yet it dances hand in hand with America.

Those who desire the world to become a realm of peace must condemn this war frenzy of American imperialism. The thirst for domination has exceeded every limit. It is desperate to protect its arrogant course from disturbance even if that requires dropping the atomic bomb.

The atomic bomb is a dangerous creation. The whole world detests it, beyond merely insisting that it must not be dropped.

India, which fondles and embraces American imperialism, must not support this. Empty verbal flourishes alone will achieve nothing. A bomb in Korea means danger coming to Asia. What will Prime Minister Nehru do? Can the condition be corrected through the usual "long" speeches? It cannot!

(*Dravida Nadu*, 17-12-50)

## English Translation

The Madras Provincial Students’ Anti-Hindi Conference was held with great distinction on 1 August 1948 at Memorial Hall, Madras, under the chairmanship of Professor C. Ilakkuvanar, M.O.L. Thousands of students from throughout the province attended. Thanthai Periyar and many women encouraged the students who had come to the conference.

Comrade Ko. Mo. Janarthanam, B.A., opened the conference.

The conference chairman, Comrade Ilakkuvanar, then delivered his presidential address in a clear and extensive manner that inspired the students. Next, Thanthai Periyar spoke lucidly and roused the students, after which the student comrades Subbiah, Vanchikko, and Abdul Khader spoke with great feeling.

The following comrades then spoke: Gurusami, B.A.; Kunjitham-Gurusami, B.A., L.T.; Dr Dharmambal Ammaiyar; S. G. Balasubramaniam; A. P. Janarthanam; R. Ilamvazhuthi; R. Nedunchezhiyan; Dr Krishnasami; K. Anbazhagan; and Annadurai. The conference concluded pleasantly with a vote of thanks by Comrade A. S. Venu.

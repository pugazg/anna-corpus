## English Translation

K. P. Santhosham, B.A., L.T., the learned comrade who wrote many research articles under the pen name "Magizhnan" in the monthly journal *Senthamizh Selvi*, guiding readers towards rational thought and kindling the light of knowledge among the people of Tamil Nadu with unswerving impartiality, passed away suddenly on 14 December 1943. He had served for many years as principal of Voorhees College, Vellore, and more recently had served the country as the Vellore district organiser for war propaganda. The news plunged us into inconsolable grief.

The passing of Magizhnan, who gave society articles that immersed people in a sea of delight, is truly an irreparable loss to Tamil Nadu.

Each of his beautiful articles, rich in profound thought, offered readers both humour and substance and invariably led people along the path of rational inquiry.

Comrade Magizhnan belonged to the Christian community. Yet the light of his intellect shone in such a way that no one could identify him as belonging to any particular community. The distinguished volume titled *Magizhnan's Research Essays*, written by comrade Magizhnan, itself bears witness to the truth of what we say. We convey our deepest condolences to his wife and family, who mourn the passing of comrade Magizhnan, a friend to everyone in every sphere who imparted sound knowledge.

(*Dravida Nadu*, 26 December 1943)

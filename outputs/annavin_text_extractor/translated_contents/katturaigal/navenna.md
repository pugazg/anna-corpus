## English Translation

## She Lost Her Tongue!

The offerings that the devotees of Tirupati Venkatachalapathi have made until now are one thing!

There are two unnecessary growths on the human body: hair and nails! Both grow when cut! Of these, that person had chosen one. Now, it seems, the offering itself has been changed! A woman devotee's speech might become slurred. If it were set right and she regained eloquence, she is said to have vowed to offer a portion of her tongue. And she is said to have done just that! Let us set aside even the question of whether she really gave it or not! What must be condemned are the people who permit a newspaper to present this to the country as though it were great news! The devotee lost her tongue. The people of the country must develop tongues long enough to condemn the long-tongued men who tell the nation about that severed tongue! If the people themselves become tongueless, it is not merely tongues that will be lost in the temples; sound reason will go too! Is it not already going?

## Cut It! Cut It!!

The tongue must not be cut! And certainly not in temples, under the influence of meaningless and foolish notions. Yet, before one can draw one's tongue back after saying so, a cry urges: cut, cut, cut it close! Why?

The Jagadguru, pontiff of the Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham, has graciously spoken! What might he have said? Do not imagine that he spoke about famine and starvation spreading throughout the world. One moment there is a flute in the hand, another moment a discus, another an arrow, another a bridle and whip; then, in the very hand that held the whip, the gesture of spiritual wisdom. There is also a tongue that explains all these attributes and offers an interpretation of Sri Jayanti! This tongue has stretched longer and longer, and people have perished through benighted minds! The Jagadguru speaks about this Sri Jayanti. Hearing all this again and again, a woman devotee gives away her tongue!

## Reduce the Prices!

Devotion has not died! Nor have the peddlers of devotion, who turn faith into money, ceased to roam about! Even so, the people cannot obtain enough food to fill their stomachs! A woman devotee gives her tongue! The Lokaguru, who through devotion has become the Supreme Being here on earth itself, stretches out his tongue! He proclaims the greatness of the Lord! In Calcutta there was a procession:

## Not a Devotional Procession!

Three thousand people set out! They marched shouting that the prices of food and clothing, which had risen like poison, must be reduced! They did not sing the name of Govinda! They did not chant "Hara Hara, Siva Siva"! The police surrounded them and beat them with lathis! Tear gas was used four times! Among those in the procession, six men and two women were injured; eleven policemen and a police inspector were also hurt! How did this happen? On the very earth where devotion and the earthly godhead that spreads devotion both exist, the price of food has risen all the way to "Kailasa"! The price of clothing has reached and seized "Vaikuntha"! "What the country needs is food and clothing; these..."

## English Translation

The venue was the home of our dear comrade E. V. K. Sampath. The General Council meeting began at eleven in the morning. After reviewing the events that had taken place since the previous General Council meeting, the General Secretary presented the income and expenditure accounts of the first State Conference. When he announced the surplus, delight danced in every heart.

At this point the Council dispersed for an interval. As though to double the joy already dancing in their hearts, E. V. K. Sampath provided a splendid feast. “What would you like?” Comrade E. V. K. Selvan asked eagerly. “Serve me some more, my boy!” Comrade S. R. Santhanam replied affectionately, while comrades Appavu and Perumal attended to everyone. The feast was magnificent: a feast of affection for the heart and a feast of food for the stomach! The General Council members were overwhelmed by both and could not say which was more wonderful.

When the Council reconvened at three, vigorous debates took place on many resolutions. As usual, every member participated. After the resolutions were adopted, the General Secretary summed up everything and explained the movement’s future programme of work. Finally, on behalf of the Council members and himself, he expressed gratitude to Comrade Sampath for helping conduct the meeting so well. He also conveyed his appreciation to Comrade Santhanam and comrades Appavu, Perumal, and the others who had provided the necessary assistance.

When the General Council ended at six in the evening, a great public meeting organized by the Erode D.M.K. was held with distinction. Under the chairmanship of Comrade E. V. K. Sampath, Comrade Sathyavani Muthu and comrades M. Karunanidhi, N. V. Natarajan, and R. Nedunchezhiyan delivered speeches, after which General Secretary C. N. A. also spoke.

Many comrades from other towns had come to observe the proceedings of the General Council. Although no one other than members had been admitted to earlier Council meetings, the General Secretary permitted everyone to attend this one. Permission was granted because this was the final meeting of this General Council. Many people, including Nadigavel M. R. Radha, observed the proceedings.

Dinner was provided for everyone at Comrade Sampath’s residence.

*Dravida Nadu, 13 April 1952*

## English Translation

I cannot accept the claim that the standard of education has declined.

Every year we say, “In no other year has the heat been as severe as it is this year!” We do not say so after examining measurements. We say the same thing during the rainy season: “In no other year has it rained like this.” We do not say so after measuring the rainfall. In just this way, everyone in power keeps saying that discipline among students is declining. They said the same thing even when I was a student, and they continue to say it now. I believe that this is not a statement based on knowing the students at all; it is merely a phrase that rulers have fallen into the habit of using.

In truth, students possess greater clarity of knowledge than before.

In our time Tamil had not attained the standing it enjoys today. Whenever prizes were to be given to students, they consisted of handsomely bound English books written by Western scholars such as Shakespeare and Milton. How many Tamil books we see today! In those days, if one wished to buy fifty rupees’ worth of books, a shop would not have that many Tamil books. Today books are available even if one wishes to spend five thousand rupees.

When I was a twelve-year-old boy I had never seen a radio. Today my son sits in Kanchipuram and turns the radio dial, saying, “This is London; this is Ceylon. The programmes on our own country’s radio are not good, so I shall turn to Ceylon. Listen!”

When I was a student I had never seen an aeroplane. Today my child watches an aeroplane fly and says that it can travel so many miles an hour. Such facilities have now become available.

To say that the standard of education in Tamil Nadu’s schools has deteriorated is an inappropriate claim. Do not say so, find fault with the students, and diminish their enthusiasm. Do not make them lose heart. I wish to tell the rulers that discouragement leads to defeat.

Today’s students will become good doctors and engineers in the future. Students must therefore study with worthy pride and enthusiasm, while also conducting themselves obediently toward their teachers.

*Thendral Pongal Annual, 14 January 1959*

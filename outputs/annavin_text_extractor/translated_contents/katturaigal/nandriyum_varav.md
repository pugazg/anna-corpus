## English Translation

I express my gratitude to comrade R. Nedunchezhian, who served as the person responsible for party headquarters from 8 August until the Thanjavur General Council and worked to make the D.M.K. shine and grow strong.

The D.M.K. commends him for the effort he undertook, while also attending to the party's propaganda work, to carry out duties of great responsibility and for the distinction with which he completed them. He conducted elections for the branch organisations, duly carried out every task under the party's new constitution, convened the first elected General Council, and worked in a manner that brought joy to us all.

Comrade Nedunchezhian's assignment was fraught with difficulty and responsibility. Yet he brought it to a successful conclusion, and the service he rendered so that an organised structure might flourish in every branch was exceedingly arduous and exceedingly gratifying.

The stage has now begun in which the party's constructive work must be attended to and carried forward. I asked comrade K. A. Mathiazhagan to undertake the work at this stage. I am also happy to report that he has been working for the past week. I expect that, during his term of responsibility, he will work so that the party's constructive activities flourish and gladden us all.

I express my gratitude to friend Nedunchezhian, who disregarded all his personal difficulties at a time of great responsibility and performed distinguished work that made the party strong. With great happiness, I welcome friend Mathiazhagan, who has now come forward to serve.

I ask the comrades of the D.M.K. to cooperate enthusiastically so that his work may succeed.

Yours affectionately,  
Arivagam, Madras  
Annadurai

(*Dravida Nadu*, date printed in damaged OCR as 28-12-22)

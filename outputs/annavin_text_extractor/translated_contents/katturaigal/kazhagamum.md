## English Translation

Take him, for example: he has belonged to the movement from the time when he was afraid to face me until today.

The same is true of the others. They too have been in the movement for a long time; they did not join it only after becoming celebrated artists.

One important reason they remain in the movement is that the movement itself enables art to grow.

I do not even like speaking of the artists in the movement as a separate group.

There are some doctors in it, some engineers, professors, teachers, writers, farmers, and labourers. In the same way there are some artists. That is all.

T. N. A.

*Dravida Nadu, 26 April 1959*

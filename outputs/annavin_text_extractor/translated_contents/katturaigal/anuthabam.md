## English Translation

We grieve upon hearing the painful news that Kanchipuram comrade M. Ezhumalai, who possessed a profound attachment to Self-Respect, worked selflessly, and had the nature of winning anyone over with kind words, died last week at a young age.

With a father devoted to orthodoxy on one side and friends of radical outlook on the other, that friend stood between them and worked with exceptional maturity. He took great care that everything should be done in the proper manner. A fine love of Tamil, an unblemished...

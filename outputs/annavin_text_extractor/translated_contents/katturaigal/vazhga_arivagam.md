## English Translation

The silver jubilee of Annamalai University has concluded as a magnificent celebration attended by the Maharaja, ministers, and rulers!

Far more numerous than those who conducted the celebration and those who took part in it are the young men and women students immersed in the great joy that our university has completed twenty-five years.

Everyone who attended and spoke at the celebration praised in many ways the late Raja of Chettinad and his son. The country knows that our university is the useful work accomplished by the Chettinad family. Our university is also a flourishing grove that will bring undying fame to their family.

That institution has completed twenty-five years.

Annamalai University and our knowledge movement have a close relationship. Annamalai University is the blessed place that we can proudly call "our home."

Those who know call it a grove where Tamil flourishes! The place, distinction, and honour accorded to Tamil there are worthy of pride. Even so, many feel that the fragrance of Tamil has not spread there across several fields to the extent expected.

Annamalai Nagar possesses many rare professors, able lecturers gathered around them, and brilliant students endowed with thought and clarity. Yet no one can deny that it is still at a stage where it must grow in many fields.

The work it must do in Tamil is abundant. During the vice-chancellorships of Dr Manavala Ramanujam and R. K. Shanmukham, many plans were framed for this purpose. Attention did not turn toward those among them that were most essential. Manavala Ramanujam yearned intensely for the university to undertake research on Kaveripoompattinam. It did not come to fruition! Instead, editions of the Ramayana are now being produced.

A silver jubilee is an event worthy of renown. At this moment, we who are concerned about the university's growth wish to state something. Although many gifted people serve there, situations repeatedly arise in which they soon seek a place outside. The reason is Vellaiyappan! If the administration creates conditions in that department that allow those labouring to spread the fragrance of knowledge to work with contentment in heart and circumstance, it will add distinction to the silver jubilee.

Nor is that all. In appointing vice-chancellors, the university appears to display some particular "fascination" and make its selections on that basis; consequently, it repeatedly fails to secure many people who could benefit the university. Otherwise, O most eminent secretary, there could not be a situation in which one man is vice-chancellor here and also vice-chancellor of Banaras Hindu University!

Our purpose in mentioning these matters at a time when the silver jubilee is being celebrated with splendour is that the knowledge movement has expectations of Annamalai University. It greatly hopes that the sparks issuing from there will bring welfare to the country.

To everyone labouring toward that end...

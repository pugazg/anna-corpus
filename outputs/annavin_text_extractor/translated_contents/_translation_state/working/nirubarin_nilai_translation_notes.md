# Working translator's notes — நிருபரின் நிலைமை

- Title: நிலைமை is a situation or condition; “The Reporter's Predicament” conveys the coercive employment situation dramatized here.
- Image 1: மாஜினங்கள் is retained in Tamil and rendered “worthies”; the unusual printed form appears to denote the people of standing attending despite their prohibition work. Do not silently replace it with a normalized Tamil spelling.
- Image 1: The opening reporter's answer lacks a closing quotation mark in the reprint. English closes the spoken answer where the narration resumes; Tamil remains as printed.
- Images 1–2: The Tamil music conference is the subject of a satirical newsroom narrative. Allegations and judgements about the editor and political figures are the author's presentation.
- Image 2: The sentence about the editor's assignment is printed twice and translated twice.
- Image 2: The son's song quotation is a boast about men's superiority, not an assertion by the translator; the subsequent discussion will describe the performance's equality theme.
- Image 2: எடிட்டரின் கரம் பேசியது (“the editor's hand spoke”) leaves the hand action unspecified. The English deliberately retains the figurative wording rather than inventing a slap or a particular dismissive gesture.
- Image 2: பிளேட் is a gramophone record; ஏ.ஆர்.பி. refers here to the air-raid precautions context indicated by sirens and shelters. The sesame-seed expression conveys extreme crowding.
- Source recovery: Archive Images 1–8 and 10 were reconciled against their scans. Missing dialogue was recovered from the 2 January 1944 RMRL newspaper, page 6 centre/right columns; the unrelated science Image 9 and its old OCR remain separately preserved. A final comparison of the full translation is required before canonical assembly and hold release.

## Continuity Evidence

- Image 12 ends with a member asking whether the finance minister accepts partition after proposing a ten-year postponement.
- Image 15 begins `உடனே நிதியமைச்சர் பதில் உரைப்பதற்கு முன்னால்...` and records the immediate response to that question.
- Therefore Image 15 directly continues Image 12; Images 13-14 are an insertion rather than missing text from the `1967` speech.

Do not restore these two scans to the recovered source unless independent bibliographic evidence proves that they belong to this work.

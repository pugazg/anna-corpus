## English Translation

Speech 30, dated 24-11-1967

(Members of the Communist Party moved an adjournment motion because the Central Government had dismissed the West Bengal United Front Ministry. The Chief Minister’s reply to that motion follows.)

The Honourable C. N. Annadurai: As far as adjournment motions submitted by members are concerned, the Speaker may dispose of them in either of two ways. One is to decide upon them in his own chamber. Alternatively, if those who brought the motion have something to say and there is reason for it, he may give them an opportunity to explain and then deliver his ruling.

The second method has now been adopted, and they have been asked to provide an explanation. In particular, they have been clearly asked to explain in what way the motion they have brought falls fully within the responsibility of the Government of Tamil Nadu. Only if an opportunity to give that explanation is provided will this House and the Speaker have the means to determine whether the motion should be taken up in the House.

If we do not permit even such an explanation, it will only create the impression that those holding views opposed to the motion are weak. There is no impediment to stating one’s view. The Speaker himself has directed that it be stated within the specified limits.

## English Translation

N. S. Krishnan, known as Kalaivanar, died on 30 August 1957. The light of art disappeared and the artistic world grew dark. Anna’s condolence address follows.

## Condolence Address: Opening

A great storm has blown down our Kalaivanar as though it had felled a fine fruit-bearing tree. My tongue will not rise to speak about his rare virtues. There are people here who would gladly give their own lives if doing so could save his.

It cannot be said that Kalaivanar had one private family. Tamil Nadu itself was his family. He belonged to this country and the world of art. His fame spread not only across this subcontinent but throughout the world. There is no Andhra person who did not know him, no one in Kerala who had not heard of him, and no Kannadiga who had not seen or known him.

He travelled beyond India to the innovative land of Russia and won the hearts of its people. Today the world has lost an incomparable artist. Mother Art grieves for her foremost son, and all of us experience a pain we have never before known. The man whose art, service, and good heart elevated the country has departed from it. His loss feels like a death within our own family.

## Condolence Address: Conclusion from Image 10

Our Kalaivanar was a good man who attracted every heart. The loss caused by his departure torments us beyond endurance. I believe we can reduce this grief a little only by sharing it with one another.

The departed Kalaivanar filled our hearts and possessed a place in everyone’s mind. He has obtained undying fame. This century has not produced another person like him.

News of his illness spread among the people and shocked the country. When the heartbreaking news of his death followed, people grieved without a path to consolation. The only return we can now make is to set fire to his body. He gave us joy; we give him tears. No other words can remove our suffering.

Kalaivanar had not even completed fifty years. People old enough to be his father remain alive today, yet at so young an age he has left us.

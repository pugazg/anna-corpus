## English Translation

Date: 30 January 1961

**C. N. Annadurai:** Mr Chairman, if we are told not to discuss a question eagerly awaited by the people of Tamil Nadu on the ground that it may stimulate the emotions of agitations outside, people outside will certainly regard that as a demonstration of the government's weakness.

In a democratic country, agitations must be accorded respect. I fear that if one says, "Because people are agitating outside, we shall neither do nor say anything," or postpones discussion by saying that a matter must not be debated in the Legislature because an agitation is going to occur outside, the government will become the instrument by which the Legislature fails in its duties.

What reason does the government have for hesitation in this matter? Why hesitate to give the name Tamil Nadu to the land where Tamils live?

**C. Subramaniam:** That is not the issue now.

**C. N. Annadurai:** If the leader of the government party would say in one sentence, "We are indeed going to name it Tamil Nadu," it would be like pouring honey into everyone's ears.

When everyone is waiting with such eagerness, I cannot understand why the leader of the government party asks that this debate be postponed. One is tempted to wonder whether the Finance Minister, who has toured many Western countries, saw such a device there and brought it back to tell us about it. If this trick alone is what he learned after so much expenditure, all the cost of his journey was wasted.

I inform these rulers that the righteous course is to permit discussion here of this very ordinary resolution, a resolution seeking a fundamental right.

**C. N. Annadurai:** Honourable Speaker, because the Minister repeatedly wishes us to change our view, we are obliged repeatedly to reaffirm it.

Whatever decision the government intends to take, today's debate will in no way obstruct that decision. If a democratic party bases its policy upon the notion that agitation must not be respected, people outside will conclude that the party does not truly honour democracy.

Nor is that all. If you say that a decision will be made by the majority, then when this House decides this matter, the name Tamil Nadu, it would be a disgrace for us to remain seated here...

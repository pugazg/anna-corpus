“தமிழகம் நிர்வாகத்தில் முதலிடம் பெறுகிறது. அந்த
நிர்வாகச் சிறப்பு ஒரு துளியும் மங்காமல் செயல்படுவீர்கள்.
நாட்டிலிருக்கும் ஏழ்மை, இல்லாமை, போதாமை அகற்றுவதில்
எங்களுடன் நீங்களும் சேர்ந்து துணை நிற்க வேண்டும்” என்று
கேட்டுக் கொண்டார்.

9-3-1967-ல் சென்னை மாநகராட்சி மன்றம் அளித்த
வரவேற்பில் அறிஞர் அண்ணா அவர்கள் பேசும்போது “மாலை

இருக்க இடமின்றி உண்ண உணவின்றித் துன்புற்று இருப்பதை

## English Translation

On 5-3-1967, Arignar Anna offered the following counsel to government employees:

“Tamil Nadu stands first in administration. You must work without allowing even a trace of that administrative distinction to fade. You too must join us and support the effort to eliminate the poverty, deprivation, and insufficiency that exist in the country,” he requested.

## At the Madras Corporation Reception

Speaking at the reception given by the Madras Corporation on 9-3-1967, Arignar Anna said:

“We have gathered and are dining at this delightful hour when evening fades. At the same time, we must not forget that countless people in the country suffer without a place to live or food to eat.

“Our foremost objective must be to remove the hardships of that poor and downtrodden community and enable it to rise,” Arignar Anna said.

## Legislative Assembly

Members elected to the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly in the fourth general election took their oath of office in their mother tongue, Tamil, on 15-3-1967, the first day of the new Assembly’s sitting.

## Anna on the Radio

On 14-3-1967, Arignar Anna delivered a major radio address:

“Friends! It is not merely that I exist for you. To tell the fuller truth, I exist because of you. I am not one who forgets his duty. I need the cooperation of every one of you to complete my duty.

“We must provide the people with a good life. This ideal is pure. Only when that ideal succeeds can it be said that a government of righteousness has been established.

“The ideal is immense. I am an ordinary man, but I am your comrade. Therefore, I undertake this work not by trusting in my ability alone, but by trusting in the ability of all of you.

“Only if the limits and forms of authority granted by the Constitution are restructured can a State government attain full development and improve the lives of its people. We must not hesitate to amend the Constitution with that objective.

“Embracing, binding together, and lying shackled are different things. The question of what form the relationship between the State and Central governments should take so that the State’s dignity and prosperity may rise without harming the common good must be examined with care.”

## Celebration

On 6-3-1967, celebrations were held throughout Tamil Nadu under Arignar Anna’s leadership to mark the blossoming of a new government in the State.

## General Council

The D.M.K. General Council met at Tanjore on 2-4-1967. Several important decisions were taken at the meeting.

## Anna in Delhi

On his first visit to Delhi after becoming Chief Minister, Arignar Anna met journalists in the Central Hall of Parliament. Speaking to them, he stated clearly:

“The powers now enjoyed by the Central Government must return to the State governments. The time has come for the Central and State governments to examine closely the functioning of the Constitution over the past fifteen years.”

On 11-4-1967, he met Prime Minister Indira Gandhi and stated that “the Government of Tamil Nadu wishes the Constitution to be amended so that English may continue as the official language.” Arignar Anna said that the Constitution must be amended.

Perarignar Anna’s eloquence and manner of answering captivated everyone in Delhi. All listened intently to his views.

The people of Delhi welcomed him warmly. Large crowds gathered and listened at the events in which he participated.

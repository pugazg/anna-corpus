## English Translation

Speech 38, dated 25-3-1968

The Honourable C. N. Annadurai: Honourable Speaker, the Ministry of External Affairs of the Government of India in New Delhi is already considering the question of ownership of Katchatheevu. The Government of India has asked this government to send a report containing the details concerning possession and title to Katchatheevu, together with the relevant documents. A report on the matter is being sent to the Government of India. In the interests of both countries, it would be better not to discuss the matter further here.

A. A. Subbaraja: Honourable Speaker, from what we see in today’s newspapers, warships have been stationed there as though Katchatheevu belongs to the Government of Sri Lanka. I therefore wish to know clearly whether the documents connected with the matter have been obtained from the Raja of Ramanathapuram and forwarded so that the necessary swift action may be taken.

The Honourable C. N. Annadurai: Honourable Speaker, I read another report in the same newspapers. Pilgrims from both countries went to the island and celebrated the festival amicably. This has served as an example of the good friendship existing between the two governments. Therefore, it would be better not to discuss this issue at greater length here.

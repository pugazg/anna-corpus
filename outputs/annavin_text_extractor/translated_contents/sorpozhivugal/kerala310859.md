## English Translation

Speech number 30, dated 21-8-1959

C. N. Annadurai: Honourable Speaker of the Assembly, the Finance Minister has explained that it is constitutionally improper to discuss here the conduct of the Central Government, which has acted so as to destroy the sound political convention that any government possessing the rights of a State’s people, whichever party conducts it, must be allowed to remain in office so long as it retains the people’s confidence. He remarked, “Those who wish to walk out may walk out.” Those who wish to remain inside may continue to remain inside. But I trust that the Minister, who has himself acquired the experience of walking out on several occasions, will understand that when issues arise, walking out is sometimes a means of expressing one’s condemnation. Therefore, on behalf of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, my party colleagues and I are walking out to condemn the refusal to permit discussion of this issue here.

(Anna and the members of his party walked out. K. Sattanatha Karayalar and the members of his party, the Commonweal Party, also walked out.)

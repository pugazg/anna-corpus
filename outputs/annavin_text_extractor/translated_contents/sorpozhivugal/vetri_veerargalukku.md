பேசுகின்றனர் - ஏளனம் செய்கின்றனர்; திராவிட முன்னேற்றக்

ஈடுபடுகின்றனர். அவர்களுக்கு நான் கூறவிரும்புகிறேன் நாங்கள்

பார்கள்? என்று பேசினார்கள். கழகத்தின் சார்பில் நமது பொதுச்
செயலாளர் தலைமையில், நமது தோழர்கள் ஐந்துபேர்
நாடெங்கிலும் சென்று, தேர்தல் சூழ்நிலைகளை ஆராயப்
புறப்பட்டனர்.

## English Translation

On the evening of 31 March 1957, a celebration was held at Madras Marina for Kazhagam comrades who had won the general election. Presiding over the event, Anna paid tribute to the victors:

“They mock and sneer, ‘Only fifteen people won,’ and debate whether the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam’s victory was large or small. I wish to tell them that although the victory we achieved was small in number, it was profoundly significant.

“The very leaders who ridicule us today, when we announced that we would contest the election, asked not merely, ‘Who will stand for them?’ but, ‘Who will stand for these things?’ Under the leadership of our General Secretary, five comrades travelled throughout the country to study the electoral situation.

“At that time, they mocked us by saying that the five Pandavas would tour the land, return to Krishna staying in Madras, cry, ‘Krishna! Krishna! We could not find anyone to stand in the election,’ and wipe away Krishna’s tears.

“Later, when we announced that we could contest 150 constituencies, they sneered, ‘What if 150 people stand? Not one of them will win.’

“Now, seeing that we have won fifteen seats, they say, ‘They obtained only fifteen seats, did they not?’

“Thus those who ridicule and mock us today have never wished us well. Our growth is their decline.

“They regard our growth as containing their own downfall.

“When other parties mock us by saying that we won only fifteen seats, sorrow hangs within their words. Let their ridicule give us still greater enthusiasm.

“Their mockery also contains sorrow that our Navalar did not win. When the fifteen of us enter the Legislative Assembly, we do so with hearts pierced by a spear.

“Only on the day we take our Navalar into the Legislature will we find peace of mind.

“Our comrades entering the Legislature have three kinds of responsibility. First, they must make its debates civilised. Second, each bears the great duty of working for the welfare of their constituency. Third, they must not deviate from the DMK’s fundamental principles. We must discharge these responsibilities properly. I therefore ask those who mock us to ridicule us enough to make us feel ashamed. Say, ‘You lost your deposits in forty constituencies.’ Only then will our zeal increase and our ability grow.

“Why is even a swiftly running horse struck with a whip? Because that horse can run faster still.

“Likewise, today’s taunts about us must serve like the whip that makes us run still faster.

“When Periyar mocks us, it is not really mockery. It is as though he asks, ‘After learning from me, only fifteen? How much strength I gave you, and still only fifteen?’

“That is why I often say, ‘It is one company with two labels.’

“In his introductory address, the General Secretary carefully analysed and clearly explained the nature of the DMK’s electoral victory.

“Though we secured only fifteen seats, he explained that we obtained seventeen lakh votes.

“Seventeen lakh people supported us in this election. Every opposing party spoke all manner of evil about us, conducted malicious propaganda, and hurled insults.

“They said, ‘These people are all atheists. If they win, worship will cease in the temples and belief in God will be destroyed.’

“It was after hearing this that seventeen lakh people understood that DMK members were not atheists, but people who condemned only the atrocities committed in the name of theism and exposed only injustice. Seventeen lakh people understood that they were not agents of destruction and voted for us.

“All the large parties, all those large parties that deride us as children, conducted this malicious propaganda. I call them large parties only because they occupy all the large positions. Even so, seventeen lakh people expressed confidence in us.

“Had The Hindu, Swadesamitran, and Vikatan shown then the sympathy they show today, would we have won only fifteen seats?

“We have drawn seventeen lakh people to our side. What power within us enabled us to do so?

“They asked and mocked, ‘All these people know is alliterative oratory. Is speaking in ornamented phrases enough?’ If ornamented speech is all we know, was it that speech which attracted seventeen lakh people? Yet these same critics also said ornamented speech was no power at all!

“None of our comrades elected to the Legislature who spoke before me here used such ornamented language. Will our critics at least admit that the people consider our ordered rhetoric better than the obstructive language they speak?

“Our victory, therefore, is no ordinary event. It marks a new stage in Tamil Nadu’s political history and in the history of our Kazhagam. Let us fully understand the new responsibility we have accepted.

“We know the difference between a firebrand and a torch, and between a sickle and a surgeon’s knife. We will work in the Legislature with a profound sense of responsibility.

“If asked for a political theory of how an opposition party ought to behave, I would say that the question should be put to the ruling party rather than to us. The character of the opposition will depend upon how the ruling party conducts itself.

“If the ruling party acts with restraint, the opposition will do likewise.

“If the ruling party behaves carelessly and overturns things at whim, the opposition will take a corresponding form. If members of the ruling party conduct themselves in the Legislature with a healthy spirit of debate, we will certainly perform our part properly.

“It was from the view that the ruling party bears responsibility for creating an opposition that Jayaprakash Narayan said, ‘Help form an opposition.’ An opposition has now arisen here. How will its members conduct themselves?

“Will the ruling party treat them with the respect owed to an opposition? If it acts with that sense of responsibility, we too will attend to matters responsibly. If it does not confine discussion to legislative-party and Cabinet meetings but brings every matter before the Legislature for debate and action, we will certainly participate and conduct ourselves usefully.

“If the ruling party happens, even by accident, to do something good, we will certainly support it. If it goes down the wrong path, we will not fail to condemn it.

“Look at the DMK legislators seated here.

“They have endured stones and blows of words. They have experienced prison life. They are not shaken by mockery or humiliation. They have put away the scowling brow and understood that a smile helps policy triumph. They are the country’s cherished children. They will serve as a good and responsible opposition.

“Following the saying I often repeat, ‘If this force is defeated, what force can win?’ I ask again: if these people cannot function as a good opposition, who else can?

“I wish to declare categorically that, although we sit in opposition, we will not abandon the Kazhagam’s foundational ideals and principles for any reason.

“No one denies that two cultures, Aryan culture and Dravidian culture, exist in this country. Those who possess Dravidian culture, whatever their religion or caste, are Dravidians.

“We have named ourselves the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, the organisation for the progress of the Dravidian land and culture, rather than even Dravidar Munnetra Kazhagam, an organisation merely for a people.

“To those who accuse us of fostering hatred by speaking of Aryans and Dravidians, I say that we condemn acts of oppression, not any race of people. The judges who decided the government’s case against me for writing the book Arya Mayai themselves stated this truth. What those three High Court judges said is known throughout the country.

“When we sit in the Legislature, I wish to remind the rulers that the hearts of seventeen lakh people stand behind us.

“If a person sings sweetly and truly possesses a fine voice, he is taught music properly, and he learns.

“Likewise, until now we have sung alone. Just as one trained in music sings with accompanying instruments, when we enter the Legislature, we too will sing with accompaniment.

“Accompanying instruments must support the singer. But if they depart from the song being sung, the singer’s individual voice will continue to sound on its own.

“I say once again to Kazhagam comrades: our victory is no ordinary event. We have accepted a great responsibility. We must not forget that others are examining us today.

“Even Ma. Po. Si. has analysed us and said, ‘They did not win in the Pandya, Chera, or Chola country; they won only in Thondai Nadu.’

“That is true. I will remind him of the saying, ‘Good Thondai Nadu belongs to the learned and worthy.’ Those who spoke of the Pandya, Chera, and Chola lands described Thondai Nadu with the epithet ‘good land.’ The DMK has won in the Thondai land of worthy people. These worthy people will secure victory in the other lands too.

“Our electoral experience must certainly help us seek further growth and act with responsibility.”

## English Translation

Speech delivered by Anna on comradeship in 1960:

Comrades!

“For many years, comrades in this area who are attached to the D.M.K. have been carrying out many good works. One of those good works is today’s purchase of a small site for a Comradeship Association, the construction of a fine building upon it, and its inauguration. With the assistance of many people, they built the building well and invited me with affection and love to open it. I convey my thanks for that.

“Those who spoke before me mentioned three or four thoughts about the nature of comradeship and how it may be obtained. Continuing from them, I too thought I might state a few ideas here and take my leave. Comradeship is a principle needed not only by this country but by every country in the world. Can comradeship be demonstrated merely by everyone looking at everyone else and addressing them grandly as ‘comrades’? It cannot.

“I shall give one example. Our Congress leaders said, ‘Indo-China bhai-bhai.’ They spoke of the people of India and the people of China as friends and comrades. Today, we see in the newspapers those same Congress leaders speaking of the Chinese as ‘thieves who have seized the border.’ Comradeship has been left so unprotected that it has come to this.

“We should not be like that. Comradeship must grow among us. We must strive to foster comradeship,” he said at the conclusion of his long address.

## English Translation

Speech 20, dated 7-7-1967

The Honourable C. N. Annadurai: Honourable Speaker of the Legislative Assembly, this government is attending to all the reasonable demands made by the honourable member who spoke second. In particular, when the procedure was first introduced, one had to pay money and affix a stamp to obtain a ration card. But it was announced yesterday that ration cards will now be issued free of charge.

He mentioned another matter as well. Suggestions such as reconstructing huts so that fires do not occur can all be discussed in detail by the committee established specifically to study these matters and recommend measures. For that very purpose, and so that leaders of the various parties may give thought to it, I have sent an invitation for the committee to meet today.

I do not think it necessary to speak at length about what the honourable Leader of the Opposition said.

I have invited the leaders of every party to the committee on remedying the damage caused by fires. The proper remedy is for all of them to assist by offering suitable suggestions. I do not consider it necessary to adjourn this House for a debate.

They said that law, peace, and order had all been disrupted. I would have discussed each of those matters separately. But there is neither the time, the need, nor the substance for it. (Loud commotion.)

P. G. Karuthiruman: Honourable Speaker, I wish to state that I have not brought an unjust charge irresponsibly. I have brought it with a sense of responsibility. When a charge of this kind must be brought against a government, I bear the responsibility of thinking about it not once, not twice, not three times, not four times, but a hundred times before bringing it. I have brought it only with anguish, in order to preserve peace. Therefore, I request the Chief Minister to accept it.

The Honourable C. N. Annadurai: Had the honourable Leader of the Opposition spoken with his natural sense of responsibility, I would have considered all the other matters responsibly.

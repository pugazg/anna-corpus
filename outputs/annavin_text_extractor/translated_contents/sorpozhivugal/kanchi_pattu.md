## English Translation

Speech delivered at the Zari Sales Association in 1967:

I shall certainly undertake every effort within my power to remedy the decline that has arisen in the silk-production industry. The Government of Tamil Nadu cannot undertake every kind of measure for the development and protection of the silk industry. In certain matters, the Tamil Nadu Government cannot make the necessary arrangements, because the State Government does not possess sufficient authority for them.

The Tamil Nadu Government can help establish many sales centres within Tamil Nadu. Taking measures for exports to foreign countries is the responsibility of the Central Government. Those engaged in the silk industry should send a delegation abroad and examine there how our silk trade ought to be organized. Those conducting the industry must bear that expense.

Only when the lives of the weavers attain fulfilment, when they have peace of mind, and when their standard of living rises will they weave and provide sarees of fine craftsmanship. Therefore, the weavers’ wages must be raised; sales must be increased to meet the additional expense that this will cause.

என்று கூறப்பட்டது. உங்களின் உதவியும் உறுதுணையும்
இருந்தால்தான் டில்லியில் எனது குரலுக்கு எடுப்பு இருக்கும்.
மதிப்பிருக்கும். ஆகவே, வாய்ப்புக் கிடைக்கும் போது வாணிபத்

## English Translation

Speech delivered by Arignar Anna at the National Chamber of Commerce on 23-4-1967:

The president of the Chamber of Commerce said in his address that the creation of a Southern Chamber of Commerce was both essential and urgent. It has been said that if I recommend your demand to the Government of Tamil Nadu, it will be acted upon by the Central Government. Only with your help and support will my voice carry weight and command respect in Delhi. Therefore, when the opportunity arises, I shall present the case for the development of trade and industry to the Central Government. If you support my voice and stand behind me when questions concerning plans for southern industrial development arise, I wish to state that Delhi will heed our voice as you expect.

When we become involved in issues of this kind, members of one party stand at street corners and raise objections, saying, “Those who gave promises during the election are diverting attention so that people will forget them.” You know that we are people who grew through opposition! We have swum against the current through the fire called “opposition”! Therefore, I shall never fear their threats. I pledge that I will argue before the Central Government for southern industrial development.

The president of the Chamber of Commerce said that sales tax is high and should be reduced. But not only the Government of Tamil Nadu; every government has stated that a government cannot function without imposing taxes. I also draw your attention to the fact that the Government of Tamil Nadu has presented a deficit budget to the Legislative Assembly.

A request has been made here that there should be no tax. At the same time, the authorities say that the country contains a great deal of unaccounted money. Therefore, representatives of government and commerce must confer on this matter and reach a decision.

It has been said that sales tax should be reduced. This is April, a time when every minister must speak cautiously. I therefore wish to state that I am not prepared to give any assurance concerning taxation. Nevertheless, I assure you that your views will be examined with sympathy.

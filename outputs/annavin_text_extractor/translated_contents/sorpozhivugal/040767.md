## English Translation

Speech dated 4-7-1967

The Honourable C. N. Annadurai: Honourable Speaker, our friends wish the proceedings of the House to be adjourned so that we may discuss what relief can be provided for the fires that have occurred during the past two days in two different places in Madras, or perhaps one should say three places, in a manner that causes concern to everyone.

Measures for that purpose are now being taken swiftly. Arrangements have been made to provide temporary accommodation elsewhere for those affected by the fires and those who have lost their homes. Through the Corporation, food has been provided for these two or three days. Various public-service organizations are offering assistance on their own initiative. Under the rules available to the government, action is being taken to grant relief payments to those who have lost their homes.

Bearing all this in mind, I wish to state that, instead of adjourning our House to discuss what may be done, if members show concern in this matter outside the sitting of the House, it will enable real relief to reach those people. I therefore state that there is no need to adjourn the proceedings of this Assembly over this matter.

## English Translation

Speech delivered by Arignar Anna, Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, on 19-6-1967, when the name “Tamil Nadu” was adopted:

Comrades!

This House is of the opinion that the name of this State should be changed to Tamil Nadu in the Constitution of India. It further recommends that this State take steps to secure the necessary amendments to the Constitution of India in this regard.

This resolution seeking a constitutional amendment should not be regarded as one brought by the government side. It should be regarded as a resolution brought jointly by this entire House.

The fact that the opposition has spoken in support of it is proof that it is acceptable to all the people of the country.

But I must answer some of the arguments advanced by Venkataraman so that others do not understand them differently. Rather than the world knowing only that Madras is renowned for administration and industrial prosperity, it must also come to know Tamil Nadu.

The world must know the Cheras, Cholas, and Pandyas; the *Silappathikaram*, the *Kural*, the *Civaka Cintamani*, the *Akananuru*, the *Purananuru*, and the rest. This resolution has been brought so that the world may know the Tamil heritage that has flourished with beauty for two thousand years.

When one says Madras, not only administrative distinction but Clive and Warren Hastings may come to mind. Fort St George may come to mind.

Venkataraman: All those have been forgotten.

Chief Minister: This resolution is precisely to ensure that, along with them, the Cheras, Cholas, Pandyas, and Tamil heritage are not forgotten.

I express my gratitude to the members of the opposition for giving their assent to this resolution. This demand embodies the feelings of Tamils and the people of Tamil Nadu. I trust that they will bring it to fulfilment.

## English Translation

Speech date: 31 October 1957

Subject: Debate on the motion of no confidence in the Council of Ministers.

Members representing all parties participated in the no-confidence motion introduced on 26 October 1957. On the final day of debate, 31 October 1957, the government did not agree to institute a judicial inquiry to determine the causes of the disturbances mentioned above. Condemning the government's position, all DMK members walked out. Before that walkout:

**C. N. Annadurai:** The Finance Minister said that this no-confidence motion was introduced merely to bring accusations against the Chief Minister and the Police Minister.

We remember what we said when speaking at the time this motion came before the House. On behalf of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, we did not accept that only two ministers were responsible for this problem.

During the debate, when accusations were made against the government, our party stated [passage damaged in the source] that the matter should be discussed at a later meeting and need not be pressed through a no-confidence motion.

I wish to state that we too must accept that decision. On behalf of our party, I also publicly express our regret that what happened here departed from it.

When the Honourable Ministers say that no judicial inquiry is needed into the firing connected with these disturbances, we wish to insist that it is necessary.

But if we press that demand through this no-confidence motion, we would also be insisting that we have no confidence in the two ministers. We do not wish to do so and subject the Honourable Ministers to its consequences.

For these reasons, condemning the government's refusal to agree to a judicial inquiry, I and the members of my party now walk out of this House.

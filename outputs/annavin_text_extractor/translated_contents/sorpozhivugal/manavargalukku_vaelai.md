## English Translation

Speech delivered by Arignar Anna at a meeting held on 8-5-1967:

Comrades!

The people have given us victory so that we may assume responsibility for governing Tamil Nadu. Though joy arose at first, we must examine the responsibility and difficulties contained within that victory. You must support us in this.

We have thought and thought until we have reached a certain position. Further thought alone is now of no use, nor is it appropriate. Let the forum retain the name “Thinkers’ Forum,” but let your activities make it a “Doers’ Forum.”

The government does not possess sufficient funds for everything we wish to accomplish. I am considering what work can be done through people like you, apart from the tasks performed through government.

## Students on Agricultural Farms

I have not yet arrived at a definite form for this plan. I am considering how people like you, male and female students and those wishing to participate in public work, may be used and in what fields you may be engaged.

If this were a village rather than the city of Madras, I would ask all of you to take part in food production. I have already spoken of this at an earlier meeting.

Four days ago, I was speaking with the Ceylon High Commissioner. I asked how they operated in agricultural development there.

He said that schoolboys and schoolgirls from the cities are taken to large government farms and engaged in work such as irrigation and weeding, and that this has given the rural people new confidence.

## A Law in Ceylon

Three weeks ago, the Ceylon Legislature enacted a new law. The government has taken over fallow lands and turned them into government farms.

Production is carried out through unpaid volunteers.

Similar work takes place in Israel. Since the plan of the Ceylon Government corresponded with what I had been thinking, I asked him for an explanation. The Ceylon High Commissioner has said that he will send me a copy of the plan and its report.

I am not asking you city residents to go to such work. It would take a whole day merely to travel to and from such places.

I am giving attention to how your service may be used.

If you spend one day a week or one hour every day, the value of your time will be equivalent to crores of rupees.

## Voluntary Work on Poultry Farms

I am considering many possible avenues for this.

For example, the government operates poultry farms, but their accounts show only losses. The reason is that there are officials for everything, paid salaries of eight hundred, six hundred, and three hundred rupees.

If people like you earn your livelihood elsewhere and spend one day a week or one hour every day working on these poultry farms, the number of officials can be reduced, losses avoided, and good results obtained. I wish to involve you in a practical plan of this kind.

When handloom cloth once lay unsold in heaps, we D.M.K. volunteers carried it on our shoulders and went street by street selling it.

What would it cost to appoint people for that work? If M.A. graduates such as Navalar were invited to sell it, what salary would have to be paid? If I quote too little, he will feel hurt. At least one hundred rupees a day would have to be paid.

The government then in office itself acknowledged that the handloom cloth we sold was worth two crores of rupees.

If everyone contributes a small measure of knowledge and ability to government, one day a week or one hour a day, great benefit can be obtained.

I shall prepare plans for this and then approach you.

People like you can become involved in issues such as food production, family planning, the danger of adulteration, and methods of clean living.

We ourselves have established a government of our choosing. It was you who longed to create such a government.

I said, “No, no.” You said, “Yes, yes.” Now, after placing the responsibility in my hands, it is not enough merely to call Anna an Abraham Lincoln or an unsurpassed hero. You must come forward to play your part in the responsibility of governing.

## English Translation

Speech delivered by Arignar Anna at the Women’s Food Council on 2-4-1967:

We cannot tell poor and ordinary people, “Accustom yourselves to eating fruits instead of rice.” If we say so, a mistaken impression will arise among them. Eating rice has become a long-established habit of our people. The poor cannot change it immediately. Those who are comfortably placed in life may change their dietary habits if they wish; I welcome their doing so.

When rice is available in surplus, we may tell people to accustom themselves to alternative foods. If we say it when rice is unavailable, the mistaken impression will arise that “the government is unable to provide it.” In particular, it is the well-to-do who should follow this plan.

They campaign for people to eat fruit and then export bananas! What is said is one thing and what is done is another. Consequently, the price of fruit is also high, and the poor cannot afford to buy and eat it.

For twenty years, the statistics supplied on the food question have been imaginary rather than true. Using them, it has not been possible to state the extent of the food shortage accurately. For agriculture to flourish, its foundations must be strong. Only then can permanent progress be achieved.

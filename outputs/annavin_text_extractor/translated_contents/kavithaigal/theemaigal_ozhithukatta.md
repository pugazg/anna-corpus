**Tamil title:** தீமைகளை ஒழித்துக் கட்ட  
**English title:** To Root Out Evil  
**Source:** <http://www.annavinpadaippugal.info/kavithaigal/theemaigal_ozhithukatta.htm>

## English Translation

For whom is the Congress Party? For the wealthy capitalist. Like a tale in which every tiger in the forest performs severe penance, the Congress gentleman tells story after story about social equality.

**Kanni and Ponni**

Kanni asks who came bending and twisting, mixing jaggery into his speech and staring helplessly. Ponni answers: it was the Congressman whose taxes tormented them and made life impossible; she boldly told him, “No vote - go!”

Kanni asks why Ponni's earring has disappeared. Ponni sold it to pay the tax imposed under Congress rule. Her husband has no work except painting boards that say “No work”; even after labouring all day he earns only one and a half rupees.

Congress spoke sweetly of self-rule in which the poor would live, and they were deceived. But the election is coming: their votes can drive out the Congress that tormented them. The DMK labours so the Rising Sun may shine and workers' lives may flourish. Anna says the same, raises the two-coloured flag, and daily wishes them certain victory. The Rising Sun is their symbol; they will cast their votes for it.

**Kasi and Masi**

Kasi asks what grows under Congress rule. Masi's heart groans as he lists it: the black market, wealthy men's profits, barrages of taxes, poverty and disease, permits and licences, brokers who procure them, piles of election money - while truth, love, virtue, and character grow weak. Arrogance in government increases, and many people live in fear. If Congress wins the next election, Tamil enslavement will become complete.

Kasi asks whether there is any way to end the injustice. Masi answers that there is both a way and a golden opportunity: recognize one's responsibility in the general election, unite to abolish evil, and vote for the movement's symbol. They need not weep that they are helpless; the DMK stands beside them. The Rising Sun is the DMK symbol - the Rising Sun that will root out evil.

## Translator's Notes

The piece uses two colloquial dialogues to turn household taxation, unemployment, corruption, and electoral choice into a campaign song. The source Tamil is retained verbatim.

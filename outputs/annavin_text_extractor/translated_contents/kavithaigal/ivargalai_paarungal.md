# இவர்களைப் பாருங்கள் / Look at These People

**Tamil title:** இவர்களைப் பாருங்கள்  
**English title:** Look at These People

## English Translation

Leaders and elegant ladies of the nation! Leave your fragrant mansions,
halls, and flower-soft beds; come with me and witness this sight.

A savage cry such as our ancestors heard from cave-dwellers in a forest
wrapped in black darkness rises here today. Bend your ears, delicate ones!
You will see little lives trapped in cages, crying out. Look upon the
picture your own hands have produced.

Hear one form make a noise like the snore of deep sleep; hear another
growl like a frenzied beast. There is a wail - the sob of an awakened
animal. Elsewhere a disease-ridden creature raises a scratching sound.

Leaders and elegant ladies, come see this spectacle. Your handiwork
created it. This is the production house of your pleasure and pride.
These are the creatures harnessed to the chariot of your procession.
Their flowing blood becomes your feast fragrant with ghee.

I ask you to look. Think, noble ones: what would become of you if,
as in ancient times, the Supreme sent to earth his spear bearing
destruction? What if God broke the cages and released these “animals”?
The poor would throw every one of your streets into turmoil - know it today!

## Translator's Notes

The source calls this a revolutionary invitation to rulers to see people reduced
by their conditions to the state of animals. The translation preserves the poem's
deliberately dehumanizing imagery as an accusation against those conditions.

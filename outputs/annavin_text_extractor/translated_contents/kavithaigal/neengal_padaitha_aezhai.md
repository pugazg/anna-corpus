# நீங்கள் படைத்த ஏழை! / The Poor Man You Created

**Tamil title:** நீங்கள் படைத்த ஏழை!  
**English title:** The Poor Man You Created  
**Source:** <http://www.annavinpadaippugal.info/kavithaigal/neengal_padaitha_aezhai.htm>

## Source Tamil (verbatim)

அவன் கூனிக் கிடக்கிறான்.

## English Translation

A lifetime of hard labour has left him bent. He stares at the hard earth and leans upon his spade. Time has cut lines into his face; the world has loaded a great burden upon his back. Joy has vanished and hope has burned away. Who brought him to this fate? He lies bewildered, without thought. Is he a brother born to the ox?

Whose hand turned his brow into a line of care? Whose breath extinguished the lamp of his thought? Did the mighty creator fashion this form to rule the sea-clad earth, see the light of stars and the sky, and gain the refinement to perceive eternity? Is this the dream discovered by the one who calculated the sun's path?

There is no form more terrible. This silent figure condemns the world's empires and blind knowledge. He bears the sign that reveals the destruction of the soul and can bring immense danger to the world.

What can he know of Veda and Vedanta, or taste of music's sweetness? What does he know of the sun's golden rays or the fragrance of flowers floating on air? How could he know them? He remains bent, bearing the cruelty the world has inflicted.

Look: they erased his humanity, seized his property, and destroyed his rights. See and listen; know what is coming. “Gods of justice, hold this in your hearts,” says this silent figure - the farmer digging the soil.

Upper classes decorated with titles from many lands, rulers and lords: is this burnt body and tormented heart the offering you present to God? Can you make a way for him to straighten his chest and walk with grace, give light back to his eyes, and restore a life in which dreams of desire may move? Can you wipe away the harm you have done through the ages and render justice?

Your answer will decide where the future carries him. On the new day when a world-shaking revolution erupts, how will you answer the question he raises like thunder? What condition will then befall the governments and rulers who gave him this form?

Before the Lord who repeatedly permitted cruelty in silence, this silent terror will speak. You will not know how to answer - alas!

## Translator's Notes

The poem addresses ruling and propertied classes through the body of an exhausted farmer. Its “silent figure” becomes both evidence and revolutionary warning.

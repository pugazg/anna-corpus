## English Translation

I saw a bee singing a melody -  
a bee singing the melody of love:  
“Will you not look? Will you not join me?  
Will you not give me joy?” - *(refrain)*

It became a flower-cluster for the young woman to cherish,  
dancing upon the plant  
to delight the flower-maiden's heart. - *(refrain)*

It offered honey-sweet music,  
sought me out and called to me,  
circling round and round,  
raising its loving pitch.

Thinking that this love  
had deeply melted my heart,  
I smiled. - *(refrain)*

It leapt among the flowers,  
tasted their nectar,  
drank deeply until it was full,  
and took its pleasure;

then, forgetting the melody it had sung before,  
it abandoned the flower  
and flew elsewhere. - *(refrain)*

As though proclaiming to me,  
“The villains who draw guileless maidens into love  
and betray them once their purpose has ripened  
are of our kind, of our kind!

Beware! Beware!” -  
such was the lesson of delight  
the bee made known to me. - *(refrain)*

## Translator's Notes

The bee's musical courtship becomes a warning about men who seduce and abandon
women. `மது` means both floral nectar and an intoxicating drink, an ambiguity
that strengthens the satire.

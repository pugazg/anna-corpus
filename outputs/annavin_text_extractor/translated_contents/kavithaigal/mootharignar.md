# மூதறிஞர் மூவர் / Three Elder Savants

**Tamil title:** மூதறிஞர் மூவர்  
**English title:** Three Elder Savants  
**Source:** <http://www.annavinpadaippugal.info/kavithaigal/mootharignar.htm>

## English Translation

Three learned men left their ancient town to live elsewhere. One knew medicine, another astrology, and the third music, yet no one valued them where they lived. Travelling together, they stopped at a crumbling roadside shelter and planned to cook. The physician went for vegetables, the astrologer for rice, and the musician stayed to light the stove.

The shopkeeper offered cheap eggplant, but the physician said it produced excessive heat and stomach pain. Okra caused phlegm, potato made the body swell, and even yam, though good, caused severe gas. Finding a disease in every vegetable, he bought nothing.

The astrologer saw a cat cross his path and sat terrified upon a veranda until the bad period passed. Then a Brahmin crossed him and an old woman sneezed. Calculating one bad omen after another, he concluded that the favourable time for buying rice would come only at sunset and lay exhausted beneath a peepal tree.

The musician lit dry wood, placed a pot of water over the fire, and heard it boil with a rhythm like “thala-thala, thathala.” Deciding this the perfect time to practise, he began singing with the pot as percussion. As the water changed its boil, the rhythm changed. He warned the pot three times to accompany his song properly. When it continued its own rhythm, he struck it with a stick as punishment for ruining the music. The pot shattered, its water extinguished the fire, and cooking became impossible.

The physician returned carrying explanations instead of vegetables. The astrologer returned without rice, saying the shopkeeper's fortune did not accord with his own and that astrology, learned for six years, never lied. The three had each established his ancient wisdom in his own way but failed to find a way to end their pinching hunger. They congratulated themselves for honouring medicine, astrology, and great music because they were geniuses, while their stomachs burned.

So too, people searching for an ideal may forget it and dwell on their private tastes and attachments. Their ability collapses under plundering thought; they lose the objective and wander with minds turned aside. Once an aim is chosen as good, one must labour tirelessly to attain it. Sleep, discouragement, and dragging in unrelated matters only summon obstacles and destroy the goal.

These three experts could not accomplish even ordinary cooking because limited intelligence ruined their objective and each was mad with the belief that he possessed extraordinary genius.

## Translator's Notes

The source says this was written about Sampath's attempt to leave the DMK. It labels the piece “not a poem; it may be made into one,” signalling its fable-like form.

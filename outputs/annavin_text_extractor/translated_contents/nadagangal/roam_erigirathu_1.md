## English Translation

**Mussolini:** Adolf! The flood of destruction has broken through the dam and is rushing towards us. Important bases in Sicily have been lost. The wicked British forces hiss like cobras emerging from their mounds.

**Hitler:** Yes. Seeing the situation, I have become deeply troubled. I went to catch them and was caught myself.

**Mussolini:** What is this? Which matter are you talking about?

**Hitler:** Why, the Orel front. I thought our army would annihilate the Russian forces there. The present situation instead creates terror that the German forces may be encircled. Before the scar from our suffering at Stalingrad has faded, another danger arises.

**Mussolini:** Why are you telling me your troubles? Did I ask about that? My empire in Africa has collapsed. I thought that at least that curse had passed. Now the blow falling upon Sicily shatters my mind.

**Hitler:** Oh, were you speaking of that story? Our troops are there too, are they not?

**Mussolini:** They were.

**Hitler:** Why, are they not fighting?

**Mussolini:** Only three things happen to your troops: surrender, a disorderly retreat, or entry into the grave.

**Hitler:** Is that so? But you could not endure for even a second the suffering we face on the Russian field. They have recaptured a hundred places. The fighting is fierce and the losses immense. My very entrails hurt when I think of that horror.

**Mussolini:** Adolf, they say that after Sicily falls they will leap upon Italy.

**Hitler:** Let them leap! There is a great distance between Rome and Berlin. Only after crossing it can they approach my city.

**Mussolini:** Listening to you, it seems you have already decided that Italy's fall is certain and cannot be prevented, and that you do not care even if it falls. Adolf, is this the mark of friendship? How many times must I plead with you? It appears matters will pass beyond remedy. Tell me, should I seek refuge?

**Hitler:** Is that not the act of a coward?

**Mussolini:** If we remain heroes, it seems we shall be burned to ash. The Allied troops are advancing so rapidly.

**Hitler:** What do you know of the speed of Timoshenko's attack? You would understand if you stood upon the Russian field. Is your trouble alone so great?

**Mussolini:** Adolf, do you know how many times I have come to see you?

**Hitler:** Five times.

**Mussolini:** What is to become of us?

**Hitler:** Did you read the American minister's speech? He says there are eighty lakhs of Axis troops to oppose the Allied armies. When he comes to understand our strength...

**Mussolini:** An army once numbering more than a crore has now diminished. It has been lost, Adolf.

**Hitler:** What of it? I am the master of Europe. I shall fight as long as it remains.

**Mussolini:** War, war, is it war forever? Even after taking a beating that squeezes us dry, still war? Without understanding, I fell into the net and now I suffer.

[A telephone rings. Hitler goes to it.]

**Hitler:** What? What is it? Is the Orel front in peril? Are the Russians attacking fiercely? Alas, my plan is being ruined.

[He puts down the telephone. After a short while it rings again. Hitler answers it and listens.]

**Hitler:** Musso, someone is calling for you.

**Mussolini:** [Taking the telephone] Who is it? The commander? What is the news?

**Commander:** The situation in Sicily is growing steadily worse.

**Mussolini:** To hell with it. Is there no good news?

**Commander:** There is no good news. There is terrible news. Allied aircraft have dropped seven and a half lakh pounds of bombs upon the city of Rome.

**Mussolini:** What? Seven and a half lakhs?

**Commander:** Yes. Rome is on fire.

[Mussolini puts down the telephone and looks at Hitler. Hitler studies the map of the Orel front. The eyes of the peoples of the world watch those two roaring men. The chapter depicting the fall of the fanatics has begun.]

When those two met somewhere in Italy, what else could they have done but converse as we have portrayed above?

But who knows how much further the Axis nations' suffering may spread before this issue of our journal appears?

It is said that the emperor Nero played the lyre while Rome burned. His successor now holds a conference while Rome burns. Destruction keeps drawing nearer, ready to embrace him.

*Dravida Nadu*, 25 July 1943.

## Translator's Notes

OCR forms “ஒரெல்” and “ஸ்டாலின் கராட்” are resolved as the Orel front and Stalingrad from the Second World War setting. The figures “80 இலட்சம்” and “ஏழரை இலட்சம் இராத்தல்” are retained as eighty lakhs and seven and a half lakh pounds without historical correction.

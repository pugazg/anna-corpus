## English Translation

### A Scene That Occurred to the Mind

## Image 1: 001-aab2f728ff.png

[Panam Peruthan Chettiar reclines on a sofa, the very image of sorrow. For the fourth time, his wife Mangamma calls to him in distress, “Will you come and eat?”]

**Panam Peruthan:** What is this! It has become a great nuisance... I shall eat when my stomach is hungry. Why do you shout so much about it?

**Mangamma:** Just look at the clock... It is already twelve...

**Panam Peruthan:** So what? Look here, Mangu! Do not trouble me needlessly. I shall come and eat when I wish... Go.

**Mangamma:** He is thinking, it seems! What fortress are you going to capture and live in?

**Panam Peruthan:** Yes! As though we are already living in comfort, from dawn until the day is done!

[The accountant, Ammanji, enters and stands with his hands folded.]

**Panam Peruthan:** Who is that? Why have you come and stand without speaking? Is there clay in your mouth? What happened? Did he come?

**Accountant:** Who, sir...?

**Panam Peruthan:** Your grandfather! Shame! It would be better to sit and weep than to struggle with you people. Not one of you has the least responsibility! What did that “auditor” say?

**Accountant:** He...

**Panam Peruthan:** “I went to see him. He was not at home.” That is what you are going to say, is it not? I knew it...

## Image 2: 002-063c6f40e7.png

**Accountant:** No, sir! He said he wanted to see you. He apparently wants to discuss something confidential. In the bank account...

**Panam Peruthan:** In the account...?

**Accountant:** A large sum appears to be missing... He says there are some places in the mill account that he cannot understand...

**Panam Peruthan:** Why did you go there, then? You should have explained it to him...

**Accountant:** I did, sir... He says all the expenditure is visible, but none of the sources of income has been entered...

[The sound of a car stopping outside is heard.]

**Panam Peruthan:** See who has come! If it is the college committee people, tell them I am not here!

**Accountant:** [Returning] It is not them, sir... It is the lawyer, Varadaraja Iyengar.

**Panam Peruthan:** Has he arrived? What wretched trouble is coming now!

[The lawyer crosses the threshold.]

**Panam Peruthan:** Come... come... I was just thinking that I should visit you, and you have come yourself! Sit down. Accountant, prepare that drink.

**Lawyer:** Never mind that... Why do you look rather troubled?

**Panam Peruthan:** It is nothing... Have you eaten?

**Lawyer:** Oh! That was over by ten. I thought you would have gone to the village!

**Panam Peruthan:** I must go! But where is the time? Very well, what happened to the case?

**Lawyer:** He has adjourned it to next week! I saw the Sub-Inspector. I said, “Chettiar wants to see you. Could you come for a while?” He flared up: “What business do I have with him?”

**Panam Peruthan:** I have made an arrangement for that. He seems to be asking for a “large sum”; that is why he is laying the groundwork!

## Image 3: 003-bc722d16db.png

**Panam Peruthan:** The government has become very bad. Under the white man, a Sub-Inspector was like dust beneath my feet. Look at things now: they are trying to take a bribe from me!

**Lawyer:** What can be done, Chettiar? The case is not an ordinary one. He caught them red-handed as well. Secretly sending goods abroad has become a very dangerous case...

**Panam Peruthan:** If he catches them, he should come and tell us, or at least inform us. Would I not take care of it? Who told him to register a case before that? Shame! Boys without the least sense of rank or respect have all become officers. Self-rule, they call it. Self-rule!

Who knows what he thinks of me? I am no mere spinach to be plucked from their yard! Do you know how many governors have visited this house? You know it yourself. When I received the title of “Sir”, the Collector came and sent word that he wished to see me. I told them to say I could not. He came in the morning and waited outside until noon... Those were the days!

Has the whole country now sunk beyond recovery? I shall show them!

**Lawyer:** You must not become angry in this matter. It is our name that will be damaged. It is best to settle him tactfully!

**Panam Peruthan:** Who will take five or ten thousand to him and weep? With that money one could buy this entire world! Hey! Who is there? Tell them to bring the car!

[The two men leave. Two cars depart in different directions.]

[Sandharppa Mudaliar sits in a reclining chair, reading the newspaper. No, he is merely looking at it! He became a Congressman only recently and is checking whether the speech he gave at a public meeting a few days ago has appeared. Desingu, a local “sacrificing patriot”, enters, scraping his clattering sandals as though announcing his arrival.]

## Image 4: 004-1ed8a7dda2.png

**Sandharppa:** Who is it? Oh... You! Come, what is the occasion? What brings you here?

**Desingu:** We are all on the same side... I thought I would simply visit you. Are you keeping well?

**Sandharppa:** There is no lack in that. I remain alive, at least!

**Desingu:** How can you speak like this? The whole town falls silent at the mention of your name. If you yourself speak so...

**Sandharppa:** What does the town know? My mind is in anguish! I joined only because all of you urged me... It has not been one or two months; five or six have passed! Yet what work of mine has been accomplished? I became president of the Town Congress Committee... All of them joined together and pushed me into a well!

**Desingu:** How can you speak this way? Only yesterday we were talking with him. How delighted he was! Speaking of you, he kept saying, “A splendid feast, a splendid feast!” whenever he remembered it.

**Sandharppa:** Who? MLA Ethappillai?

**Desingu:** Not him... The gentleman among our ministers... him.

**Sandharppa:** Him? What did he say?

**Desingu:** About the party you gave when he came to our town last week. He was extremely pleased!

**Sandharppa:** Desingu, you will not believe me. Do you know the total expense? Seventeen hundred rupees.

Money is nothing; can money be important? Those who govern the country have so much hardship and trouble! If their hearts are gladdened for even one day, that is enough. That is what matters to us!

**Desingu:** What you say is the pure truth! Money comes today and goes tomorrow. Service to the nation like this will endure forever, will it not?

**Sandharppa:** Yes, yes! And did you mention anything about me?

## Image 5: 005-540783d892.png

**Desingu:** How could I fail to? I told him: “He is a great rich man in our district... His heart melts at the very thought of the poor... He has built so many temples and will build still more... Though he joined Congress only now, he has always been a Congressman...”

**Sandharppa:** Leave all that aside. I asked whether you mentioned our income-tax matter as I told you...

**Desingu:** I did, sir...

**Sandharppa:** You are a capable man! I wondered whether you had forgotten...

**Desingu:** He said, “I have told him; I have informed the minister.”

**Sandharppa:** He said he would tell the minister? Who? The minister himself...?

**Desingu:** No... Our MLA Ethappillai!

**Sandharppa:** Oh, so you told him...

**Desingu:** I told him myself. He and I were in the same jail during the August Revolution. Even so, there is more effect when an MLA speaks than when I do.

**Sandharppa:** What a wretched business! What did that MLA say?

**Desingu:** He said he would come and meet you soon...

**Sandharppa:** Why?

**Desingu:** There must be some reason.

**Sandharppa:** I do not like any of this. I too am a Congressman, and president of a Town Congress Committee at that. He belongs to our party. If party men will not provide conveniences for fellow party men, then what party or aubergine is this?

When I was in the Justice Party, a mere grunt from me was enough. Do you know all that would happen? Is becoming a minister sufficient? A man must understand rank and standing. He must resolve grievances. All that requires statecraft. Where do these people possess it? They all have the pallor of corpses!

## Image 6: 006-3e9f811bf5.png

**Desingu:** Can you yourself speak this way? Will they fail to attend to everything? Should our turn not come as well?

**Sandharppa:** What turn, man? Everything is in our hands, is it not? So many days have passed. Apart from the smuggling case, what benefit have we obtained in anything else? No, I ask you!

[A car stops outside. Mudaliar does not finish speaking. He looks out. Panam Peruthan enters.]

**Sandharppa:** Come, come! What brings you all this distance? Sit down...

**Panam Peruthan:** Are you keeping well?

**Sandharppa:** By His grace, there is no deficiency...

[Panam Peruthan looks at Desingu.]

**Panam Peruthan:** This is...?

**Sandharppa:** Our Desingu. You know him, the Congress patriot.

**Panam Peruthan:** I know him. I had forgotten. When so many troubles torment a man, can he remember today even those he saw yesterday? What is he doing now?

**Sandharppa:** Service to the nation...

**Panam Peruthan:** Hmm... And are all the mills running well? How is the case?

**Sandharppa:** The case is over! The judgment is in our favour. Only that cursed income-tax matter keeps dragging on!

**Panam Peruthan:** Why ask me about it! I run about night and day earning money, only to pour it all out for these fellows in the end! I ask you, for whom does self-rule operate? Speak outside, and they shout, “Down with the rich!” Only by asking can one know the suffering of a rich man!

**Sandharppa:** Tell him! Listen, Desingu. They hold meetings at every street corner and abuse us. Ask how our condition is. Yet what use is asking?

## Image 7: 007-8fe4964859.png

Are you going to hold a meeting the next day and silence their mouths?

**Desingu:** Why do you speak so? Whenever we hold a meeting, what else do we discuss but this?

**Sandharppa:** What have we gained by speaking? Everything follows the path by which we arrived. Whom can we blame, and to what use?

And how are all your affairs? Have they been settled?

**Panam Peruthan:** Not at all... I came precisely to speak about that. [With his eyes, Panam Peruthan indicates that Desingu is present. Sandharppa Mudaliar understands.]

**Sandharppa:** What else, Desingu? Is there any business? This elder brother and I must speak privately for a while...

**Desingu:** Nothing special... I am thinking of going to the conference...

**Sandharppa:** Where? The Teynampet conference, of course. Go and come back. Tell me everything. If anyone asks whether I am coming, say I am in very poor health! I too wish to come. It would be an opportunity to see all the ministers. I shall see... What do you say, elder brother? Shall we go and return?

**Panam Peruthan:** We can think about it!

**Desingu:** If you would...

[Mudaliar realises that Desingu is laying the groundwork for some request.]

**Sandharppa:** Very well! Come tomorrow. We shall discuss it in detail.

[The party worker leaves. The two “crocodiles” begin to talk.]

**Sandharppa:** What happened to that cloth-smuggling matter?

**Panam Peruthan:** What could happen? I thought I could catch the Sub-Inspector and seal his mouth, but he is apparently leaping about uncontrollably...

## Image 8: 008-8904c8021b.png

Trouble upon trouble for a man... I feel like hanging myself and dying. To whom else can I cry if I cannot tell you? Look at me: I lend money at interest...

The income-tax man sends a notice every day demanding to see those accounts!

From the village, someone comes every day, one after another, saying the workers demand higher wages!

At the mill, their demonstrations are unbearable. “You earned six lakhs; give us half a lakh as an allowance,” says their petition! They call our earnings “plunder”... These wretches say so. Who knows the hardship we suffer? It has become a terrible nuisance. I cannot manage anything!

**Sandharppa:** Hmm... If you heard my story, it would be still more amusing! Because I had not shown accounts for three years, income tax sprang upon me with a roar! There was that smuggling case too. Elder brother knows it: our men were caught while carrying the goods... That was another mountain of trouble. It looked as though I might even have to go to jail. Our lawyer said the same. I understood nothing! Truly, elder brother, one night I even thought of shooting myself dead... But fortunately my nephew came and saved me with an idea! You know my nephew.

**Panam Peruthan:** Who?

**Sandharppa:** Younger brother from the neighbouring town, president of the District Congress. Him.

**Panam Peruthan:** Did he tell you to approach the minister?

**Sandharppa:** “Can it be done suddenly? The whole town will go ‘tom-tom’ with the news! Join Congress, uncle,” he said...

Now, one by one, all the matters are being resolved somehow! Even last week, look, I gave a party for the minister...

## Image 9: 009-02796f6e6e.png

**Panam Peruthan:** Oh! I too wondered why you had suddenly joined Congress and were spending money like this. Only now do I understand the matter!

**Sandharppa:** Yes! What else do we need? We must protect what He has given us and keep our children and families comfortable, must we not?

**Panam Peruthan:** What you did to end all your troubles was an excellent decision. Only now does the same thought occur to me!

**Sandharppa:** Yes, elder brother! We must attend to our own affairs ourselves. Then...

**Panam Peruthan:** Come along for a while. Let us go as far as the khadi store...

**Sandharppa:** It looks as though you have decided. Come, let us go! The membership book will be there. We can join.

**Panam Peruthan joined Congress!**

**A philanthropist steps forward to labour for the poor!**

The daily newspapers published such headlines together with his photograph! “Patriot” Desingu rejoiced: a great man had recognised the greatness of Congress and joined it! But to obtain the true answer, should we not ask “those two hearts”?

*Dravida Nadu, 19 February 1950.*

## English Translation

*(A humorous political play)*

## The 1936 Model

### 1. At Home

Everything has become a Sudra kingdom. All the great offices go to them. Those fellows have become brilliant at English education. The ministry is in their hands, while we sit in a corner of the opposition.

When will our rule arrive? When will our community receive ministries and other great offices? Who knows how the Goddess will bestow her grace?

If communal representation is gradually abolished, our children's future will be secure. Let us try our hand. The newspaper world is with us, and the public platform under our control. The Justice Party people are asleep.

The white man is not so bad; he seems somewhat on our side. He too belongs to the Aryan lineage. We need not worry about one of the Justice leaders; he is our man and helps us at suitable moments. The others are somewhat mad about their party. Still, let us try our hand. We know so many wonderful tricks.

### 2. On the Public Platform

Citizens of this nation! This wretched new reform must be broken. Otherwise our fate is finished.

If you vote for the Justice Party, they will again become ministers, ruin the nation and implement the reform. Send us instead. By Bharat Mata's grace, we shall break this new reform to pieces.

We do not wish to enter the Legislature for ministerial office. Fie! Why would we want that curse? Are we slaves?

If elected, we shall abolish taxes, reduce land revenue by more than half, and eliminate unemployment. Once the Justice Party is gone, milk and honey will flow through the land. Friends, therefore vote for us.

### 3. In the Legislature

Although there are only one or two of us in opposition, we alone represent the citizens of the nation.

Hey, Justice ministers! People writhe and suffer under the cruelty of land revenue. Hunger, famine and disease dance everywhere. This sin will fall upon your heads. Land revenue must therefore be reduced by fifty per cent. Yes, it must certainly be reduced.

**Justice Minister:** Honourable Congressman, is not the present arrangement dyarchy? We do not possess authority to reduce land revenue according to the Legislature's will.

**Congressman:** Shame! Shame!

**Justice Minister:** That authority rests with the British official. He asks how the administration can function if revenue is cut by fifty per cent. You tell us. If land revenue is halved, where will money come from to provide other facilities to the people? Under these conditions it cannot be reduced by fifty per cent.

**Congressman:** If you cannot do it, get out! Resign!

### The Newspaper World

Bravo! Bravo! Today in the Legislature, Congressmen splendidly demonstrated their love for the poor by introducing a resolution demanding a fifty per cent reduction in land revenue. It is a most necessary and urgent resolution.

The Justice Party ministers, members of a party that betrays the nation, opposed the patriots' resolution. What cruelty! What injustice!

The Congressmen thundered heroically that such ministers must resign at once. Bravo! Bravo! What valour is theirs! Support this tender-hearted and heroic Congress in the next election. Vande Mataram.

## The 1938 Model

### 1. At Home

Mother Goddess! The rule of those fellows has ended. The ministry is with us and the Governor is with us. The opposition leaders, poor things, are also with us: they have swooned and fallen.

We must winnow while the wind blows. Wherever an office becomes vacant, our boys must be given a "chance." Last month nineteen of twenty-six posts went to our people.

Hindi, yes, we can push a few more in through that. Moreover, if Hindi comes, those fellows will behave according to our wishes and the ways of old.

Communal representation? Let it lie. Let us attend to the work. Stopping liquor is certainly troublesome; revenue falls. Still, never mind. It brings prestige and will help win votes next time.

If there is no money, close schools and reduce expenditure on medical care. What catastrophe will occur? Those people will shout. Let them. We shall have our men shout "Jai." Let us finish whatever we can in these five years and consider the rest afterward. Who imagined we would ever obtain this opportunity?

### 2. On the Public Platform

Citizens of this nation, do not think of me as a minister. I am your servant and shall provide whatever help I can.

**A voice:** We do not want Hindi!

**Minister:** Only people without intelligence say they do not want Hindi. This is the trouble created by that Naicker.

**Voices:** No! No! We Tamils oppose it!

**Minister:** Tamils? Good heavens, what am I? It is precisely because I too am Tamil that I say Hindi is necessary. Hindi was decided long ago. However much noise you make, it cannot be abandoned. Beware!

**Voices:** Are you Hitler? Down with your arrogance!

**Minister:** Police! Police! What a cursed nuisance. Very well, the speech ends here. Let the vote of thanks proceed.

### 3. In the Legislature

**Congress Minister:** We could reduce land revenue by only seventy-five lakh rupees. We could not reduce it further. There is no money, though our desire is immense...

Even so, only seventy-five lakhs can be reduced, and it has become necessary to close some schools as well.

**Justice Party Member:** Minister, was not that seventy-five-lakh reduction made by the interim ministers?

**Congress Minister:** Hush! That is an old matter. [The Speaker orders him to sit.]

**Justice Party Member:** At least will this seventy-five-lakh reduction be made permanent under the arrangement established by the interim ministers?

**Congress Minister:** It will be reduced only temporarily.

**Justice Party Member:** What is this? Did you not say that land revenue would be cut exactly in half?

**Congress Minister:** What is that to you?

**Congress Members:** [Shout] Hoo, hoo, oo, oo!

**Justice Party Member:** Let that pass, Minister. Are you preparing to make the land-revenue reduction permanent?

**Congress Minister:** I am not ashamed; I shall tell the truth. This land-revenue matter is very, very large. It has existed traditionally. I examined it, and despite everything I could do, nothing was possible. I say this without shame. But why do you speak about it? Whether we reduce it or not is our affair. It was something we said while seeking votes.

**Justice Party Member:** Yes, indeed! It was the "rope" you twisted while seeking votes. That is precisely what I am asking about. Your speech was one way then and another way today.

**Congress Minister:** Listen, I shall tell you a story. You know the green lizard, do you not? It changes its colour from time to time. In the same way, we shall behave differently from time to time. Do not ask why we failed to do one thing or why we did another. Put the resolution to a vote if you wish.

### The Newspaper World

A splendid reply! In the Legislature today, members of the opposition caused trouble by demanding a reduction in land revenue. Since Congress ministers came to power, the people's wealth has increased and they live happily. Unable to bear this sight, the Justice crowd is trying to ruin the country as before by making trouble and demanding a reduction in land revenue.

But our minister gave them a splendid reply. The story of the "lizard" that he told in the Legislature today is enough by itself to show how qualified he is to remain a minister and receive the nation's love. Vande Mataram! [For Hindus only; temporarily not for Muslims.]

[This is Anna's play exposing the forked tongue of Congressmen. It was published in *Kudi Arasu* on 10 April 1938.]

### A Scene the Camera Will Not Accept

**Place:** The rest house at the edge of the village

**Present:** A young man, who is a reformer, and the village elders.

[The village elders are angry with the young man because he criticises ancient horoscopes and palm-leaf texts. They cannot answer the questions he asks. Nor can they refute the reasons he gives! “He certainly speaks very cleverly!”

“A city education!”

“A loudmouth and a bully!”

“The scoundrel will split an atom and show you every last particle!”

With remarks of this kind, they express their fond irritation with him.

The young man sees one of the village elders...]

**Young Man:** Elder brother! There is something I wanted to ask you...

**Elder:** When have you ever gone without asking something, boy? Very well, what subject have you found now?

**Young Man:** If you are angry, never mind. I shall speak only if you are calm...

**Elder:** Why, boy? Am I such a hot-tempered man?

**Young Man:** I did not say that. There is a proverb about taking one's anger at something out on the ox...

## Image 2: 002-9140f9f5bb.png

That is why I was afraid that if you were angry for some other reason, you might take it out on me...

**Elder:** I am not angry with anyone. Tell me, tell me...

**Young Man:** When a son speaks disgracefully about the mother who bore him, good and respectable people would surely condemn him, would they not? Their hearts would tremble: “This worthless fellow abuses his mother! His arrogance must be brought under control. It is a sin even to look upon his face!” That is how good people would speak, is it not?

**Elder:** Yes... Everyone would revile him...

**Young Man:** “You speak contemptuously of the mother who gave birth to you and raised you, who protected you so that not even a fly or an ant settled upon you, and who cherished, praised and nurtured you, you depraved fool!” They would heap up reasons and scold him, would they not?

**Elder:** Yes. What doubt is there?

**Young Man:** Suppose that same son does not stop with abusing his mother, but strikes her as well.

**Elder:** Bah! Is he even human?

**Young Man:** The world would not respect him. Is that not so?

**Elder:** Yes! It would spit upon him...

**Young Man:** Suppose that son does not stop with abuse and assault, but actually cuts off his mother's head...

**Elder:** [Startled] Accursed wretch! What, what? He cut off his mother's head? Who is that monster? Where is he?

**Young Man:** Wait, elder! I merely asked what the world would think and say about a man if he did such a thing, and you roar before me, “Bring him here!” You fool... If a son cuts off his own mother's head, what would the world say about that son? Tell me.

## Image 3: 003-7765d2d9de.png

**Elder:** The world would call him a great sinner, a vile man, a murderer...

**Young Man:** It would hurl at him every word of abuse it could find.

**Elder:** We would have to summon Adisesha, with his thousand tongues, to curse him...

**Young Man:** Such would be its fury.

**Elder:** How could it be otherwise? Would the world spare the great sinner who cut off the head of the mother who bore him?

**Young Man:** A snake must be killed when it is seen; a scorpion must be crushed. Likewise, the son who kills his mother...?

**Elder:** A son, is he? He is a great sinner!

**Young Man:** Quite so. If you were to see that great sinner who was her son...?

**Elder:** He must be hacked into pieces and served as a feast to the crows, dogs and jackals...

**Young Man:** You have pronounced judgment, elder! Are you not an elder? Would you speak unjustly?

**Elder:** Is it only I? The whole world would pronounce the same judgment.

**Young Man:** Very well. Now watch: both your judgment and the world's judgment will change in a single instant...

**Elder:** What is this absurd talk? If a son cuts off his own mother's head...

**Young Man:** You have already explained it at length! Imagine the crows, vultures, dogs and jackals all waiting; knives and sickles have been put into your hands; and the man who cut off his mother's head has been brought and made to stand before you. If you were then told, “Go on, carry out the punishment”...

**Elder:** I would carry it out without the least hesitation...

**Young Man:** That is precisely what I say you would not do.

**Elder:** What? I would not carry out the punishment?

**Young Man:** Yes. You could not...

**Elder:** An absolute lie. I would certainly punish him...

**Young Man:** You simply could not...

**Elder:** Hey! What do you take me for, that you mock me like this?

There is a difference between one human being and another as well. When you see a rickshaw puller staggering along the street, you feel like rebuking him: “Get lost, you drunken rascal!” You find the courage.

## English Translation

"Seth Gangaram is a terrible swindler. He seizes the town's wealth and feeds it into his own furnace. He squeezes people dry and amasses money. He lets his bull graze in our sugarcane field and ruin it. He tramples the buds in our garden underfoot. He lets loose a marauding monkey in an orchard heavy with fruit... Shame! There is no limit to his injustice! There is no limit to his domination! Are we to remain subject to his domination and outrage? We must make our people understand Seth Gangaram's evil designs and awaken their indignation and honour. There must be no delay. Before the cause is ruined at its roots, we must perform our duty."

My delight was beyond words. The desire to suppress Seth Gangaram's arrogance was already blazing within me. Koovam Kuralappan not only thought as I did but had arrived to speak with even greater passion and awaken everyone with his valiant words. How could that sight fail to delight me?

I had believed that Seth Gangaram and Koovam Kuralappan shared great affection and loyalty, and so had thought Kuralappan would never join us. Just then this stirring speech arose. It was an appealing speech, and I believed it would also produce great benefit.

"Kalathuran and Koovam Kuralappan have united. Now only Rattinam Rajappan remains on Seth Gangaram's side," many people said.

I worked together with Koovam Kuralappan to explain the urgent necessity of curbing Seth Gangaram's insolence. The days rolled by. Everyone said, "Kuralappan now says exactly what Kalathuran has been saying. And Kuralappan is a man who associated with Seth Gangaram for many years! Together, the two are now exposing Seth Gangaram's tricks."

I would say that Seth Gangaram's domination must end. Kuralappar would thunder, "That is not enough! What our friend Kalathuran says is not enough. Not only his domination, but Seth Gangaram himself must be driven from here!" I was overjoyed.

I heard that even Seth Gangaram had become disturbed.

"That Kalathuran has been speaking against me for a long time. But Kuralappan used to speak to me with a smile. Now even that man has joined Kalathuran! What rotten luck!" Seth was said to have muttered.

Joining me, Kuralappar condemned not only Seth Gangaram but also the cruelty of Koyilaran and the folly of Madatharan.

Then one day, the same Kuralappar suddenly began to say: "Condemning Seth Gangaram is pointless and only creates needless hostility. Kalathuran, Kovaiyan, Konguran, and Kollathan, who claim that Seth Gangaram commits injustice, all speak with one voice. Yes! I will say it plainly, even if it turns everything upside down."

I was stunned. I could not understand the reason for Kuralappar's change of heart.

Seth Gangaram's mischief remained just as before. If anything, it was growing. Yet Kuralappar's speech had changed completely.

"This Kalathar gang is angry with Koyilarar and is going away to Kattur," he said.

"These great sinners, who pick a quarrel with Madatharan, have joined an outsider," he said. I could not understand the cause of this hostility and stood bewildered.

Muppuriyan and Seth mocked the news that "Kalathuran and Kuralappan have separated" and clapped their hands in laughter.

For a few days I remained perplexed. Then I resolved: "Very well. The help we received along the way lasted only this long. From now on, we must carry on our own work." I returned to the task.

Only gradually did the reasons emerge for the transformation of Kuralappan, who had once thundered that Seth Gangaram must be opposed and driven out.

One reason was this: many people already opposed Seth Gangaram. If Kuralappan merely joined them as one among many, how could he make himself conspicuous? If he instead joined Seth, he could instantly attract everyone's attention.

There was another reason. Seth Gangaram had appointed Kuralappar to guard his field.

To conceal the fact that it was field-guard duty, Kuralappar went about calling his job "border protection."

"What do I care which border it is? Kuralappar guards my field, and my troubles are over," Seth Gangaram reassured himself, unaware of the fierce storm embodied by his guard.
